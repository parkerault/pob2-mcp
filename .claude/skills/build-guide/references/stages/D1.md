# D1: the endgame rungs (`build-guide-coordinator`)

Slice 03 §5.3 step 1; spine C-36, D-104, D-115…D-122, D-147, D-148, D-193, D-217, D-277, D-279, D-281,
D-282, D-293, D-295, D-296. The endgame is designed first and the campaign is planned backward from it (D-104). D1 writes the three
endgame stages of `plan.json`, the rungs of the endgame ladder: entering maps (`m`), budget (`b`) and
non-budget (`n`). It prices every slot and tree jewel of each, and measures them. A fresh D1 gate step
(`D1-gate.md`) then checks them; D2 (`D2.md`) adds the campaign.

## Inputs

- **Header:** `stage: "D1"`, `attempt`, `concept`, `mode`, `runFolder`, `runFolderRel`,
  `compile: "measure-only"`, `writes: ["plan.json"]`, and:
  - `findings`: on a retry, the earlier D1 gate attempt's verdict file and its findings (`{part, code,
    stage, message}`; fix what each names); a note when an earlier attempt returned without writing; on a
    guided retry, the escalated verdict's findings followed by the user's guidance
    (`code: "user-guidance"`), which you follow;
  - `findings`, also: in the fix round, the V4 findings routed to this step (`{id, artifact, location,
    claim, issue, evidence, owner}`); after a revision at the final gate, the user's requests mapped to
    this step (`code: "user-request"`), which you make. The prompt says which; "The fix round and revisions" below
    says how;
  - `accepted`: findings the user accepted at an escalation. They need no fix.
- **Prompt:** whether to write `plan.json` anew or edit it, and how to price in this run (offline;
  stopped; or paced).
- **Read:**
  - `dossier.md`: the card's H3 (`<id>: <title>`) with its seven fields and its R1b result, the
    `Mechanics`, `Synergy search` and `Config gaps` sections, and `Roll and mode` (the league line);
  - the card `cards/<id>.md` (its chain, enabler, links, checklist results and estimates), and its skeleton
    `concepts/<id>/plan.json` with its `compile-record.json`;
  - `docs/build-guide/plan-reference.md` (every field, and the worked example) and `pob-pin.json`;
  - the C1/E1 facts under `data/`: `data/campaign/limits.json` (passive points, ascendancy points, the
    resistance penalty), `data/enemies/map-monsters.json` (monster life and damage by level),
    `data/enemies/waystones.json` (area levels and modifiers), `data/enemies/arbiter-of-divinity.json`;
  - `run-log.json` (has pricing stopped? a `stages[]` entry whose `priceOutcomes` counts a `locked_out` or
    `rate_limited` call) and the other plans of this run (`concepts/*/plan.json`), for prices to reuse;
  - `.claude/skills/build-guide/references/lookups.md`, `.claude/skills/build-guide/references/pricing.md`,
    `.claude/skills/build-guide/references/gates.md` (what the gate will check) and
    `.claude/skills/build-guide/references/source-markers.md`.

## Work

1. **Start.** When the prompt says to write `plan.json` anew (attempt 1 of a fresh design), write it
   whole from the card's skeleton, at `schemaVersion: 2` (`docs/build-guide/plan-reference.md`): its `roll`
   and `versions`, and only the endgame stages below. A
   `plan.json` already in the run folder belongs to an earlier design and is replaced. Otherwise edit
   `plan.json`: fix what each finding names, and keep any campaign stage D2 wrote.
2. **League** (C-31, D-233):
   - offline: `null`;
   - online: `mcp__pob2__get_leagues`' current trade league (not Standard, Hardcore or a private league);
   - if `mcp__pob2__get_leagues` can't be reached, a league a price result of this run recorded (an `ok` entry of a
     card's skeleton, whose league the trade site gave);
   - **never** a league the dossier or a skeleton marks unverified (R1b's fallback, `leagueUnverified`),
     and never a guess such as Standard. With no league known, write `"league": "unverified"`, make every
     price `unavailable` (note `trade unreachable`), and return `leagueUnverified: true`; the guide says
     the league couldn't be verified.
3. **The three rungs**, after any campaign stage, in ladder order: `m`, `b`, `n`. Design `b` and `n` first,
   then `m` (D-281):

   | | Entering maps | Budget variant | Non-budget variant |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | `id`, `variant` | `m`, `entering-maps` | `b`, `budget` | `n`, `non-budget` |
   | `loadoutName` | `Entering maps (L<level>) {m}` | `Endgame budget (L<level>) {b}` | `Endgame non-budget (L<level>) {n}` |
   | Aim | the player's setup just after the campaign, plus buys within 5 divines: T1 to T5 maps, gated at T5 (area 69: `enemies/waystones.json#area-level.offset` plus 5); the cheapest setup that passes its gate with the same skill and supports as `b` (D-281) | T15 maps (area 79: `enemies/waystones.json#area-level.offset` plus the tier) without dying often, as cheaply as possible, at most 20 divines (D-116, D-147, D-219) | as strong as possible around the ~200-divine reference: T16 (area 80) up to area 82, heavily modded waystones and tablets (D-115, D-117) |

   Each stage has `progress: "endgame"`, its own `level`, `ascendancyPoints` and per-gem levels, and
   `variantBasis` giving the source of each (D-193): a C-30 tag, or a `judgment` tag stating the
   rationale. The spec fixes no number: choose what a player of that rung has. `m`'s level is above the
   campaign's last stage. At 5 divines the key item may fit `m` (a unique, or a rare with a stat floor, such as
   a weapon's elemental DPS or an energy shield total); when it doesn't, it is the trigger into `b`.
   - **One tree track** (D-282): `m` continues from the campaign's last stage, `b` from `m` and `n` from `b`,
     so each rung's tree contains the one before it, except where its `respec` drops nodes (`respec
     {dropped, goldCost, note}`, the gold cost a `judgment`); V1 checks each rung's respec and its gold cost.
   - **The buy order.** `m`'s claims give its buys in order, one claim per step with a short reason where it
     isn't obvious; `b`'s give its buy order the same way, and `n`'s its upgrade order from `b`. The guide
     numbers them as you order them.
4. **Skills** (D-120, D-122, D-148, D-217, D-295). The card's named damage source is the plan's
   `roll.skill` (D-286).
   - **The build's named damage source is the main source of damage, never a token role.** (D-122, as D-295
     words it.) For a fixed skill that skill is the named source, and its own hits, ailments and projectiles
     count when another skill fires, spreads or copies them. Its group is `skills.rolledGroup` (the name
     stays, D-295).
   - **The named damage source should be the clear skill. It takes the single-target role only when it can't
     clear, and the guide says so.** (D-148, as D-295 words it.) Its group's role is then `single_target`, a
     stage claim says why, and the guide's How to play it repeats it.
   - Its figure, × its Count, must be the largest of the stage's groups with that role: the gate compares
     one Count-scaled record field for every group (`countedSkillOutputs[g<group>s<skill>p<statSet>].fields.<Field>`,
     the same `<Field>` for each, named `g<group>s<skill>p<statSet>.<Field>`), or a ledger value. Never give
     a damage skill the `utility` role to keep it out of the comparison.
   - Put the named source's group first (`skills.main.group: 1`, `main` on the named source): every
     stage shares the main group's index, and D2 holds a bridge skill there until the named source's
     requirement (D-301).
   - **Set `skills.main.statSet`** on each variant stage to the named source's stat set whose figure the
     gate compares, `p<statSet>` in `g<group>s<skill>p<statSet>.<Field>` (amendment 1 S7): the PoB file
     opens on the main stat set, so the guide's headline figure must be that set's. A skill whose damage
     comes from a later stat set (an explosion after a dash) names that set here. `skills.main.group` is the
     `rolledGroup`, and the rolled figure, or every breakdown field a ledger figure's formula reads, is
     `<stage>.g<main.group>s<main.activeSkill>p<main.statSet>`.
   - One loadout has a one-click clear and a single-target setup (D-120). Every group has a `role`; every
     gem its level and quality. `mcp__pob2__suggest_supports` ranks supports and lists what PoB can't measure (C-47).
   - Never quote a `mcp__pob2__suggest_supports` gain as a number in a claim: it is a transient measurement no record
     field holds. State a support's effect from its gem text, or from a ledger entry or a record field.
   - **Count and custom modifiers** (D-293; `docs/build-guide/plan-reference.md`, "Count and custom
     modifiers"). Where several copies of a group act at once (totems, minions that each cast it, mirages),
     set the group's `count` with a `countReason` saying why that many act at once and what grants them
     (a node, an item or gem text, with its source). Where PoB doesn't model an effect the build relies on and
     a modifier line can stand for it, add a `config.customMods` entry `{text, reason}`: one PoB modifier line,
     its reason saying what it stands for and how its number was worked out. A missing reason is a V1 finding
     (`missing-reason`). Both are estimates (D-296): the record applies Count to the named source's figures
     (`countedSkillOutputs`) and marks every figure of a stage with a custom modifier
     (`computedWithCustomMods`), and the guide lists each with its reason. **After setting Count** on a group
     whose skill reserves once per copy, on totems under Ancestral Bond, or on a group whose Umbral Well buffs
     it scales, check the stage's reservation and those buffs in the record: Count multiplies them too.
5. **Tree.** A complete node list connected to the class start, within the points the level allows
   (`data/campaign/limits.json`: `passive-points.max`; the ascendancy at most `ascendancy.max`), with its
   weapon-set nodes and attribute choices. Start from the skeleton's tree. After a compile, the loaded build
   answers `mcp__pob2__find_path_to_node`, `mcp__pob2__get_nearby_nodes` and `mcp__pob2__get_passive_upgrades` (with
   `include_ascendancy: true` for ascendancy notables); both rankers list what PoB can't measure (C-47).
6. **Items.** Every slot the variant uses (`Weapon 1` first) and every tree jewel, flasks and charms with
   their toggles. A unique's text comes from `mcp__pob2__lookup_game_data {mode: "unique", query: <exact name>}`. A rare
   is an affix target: realistic rolls a buyer finds at that budget. An off-hand made legal only by Giant's
   Blood, Instruments of Power or Lord of the Wilds needs that node or item in the same stage.
7. **Config** (C-32, D-114; `config.earned` and `config.scenario`).
   - **Earned state**, `{option, value, producer, uptime}`: every stack, charge, buff or condition the
     numbers rely on, with the producer present in the stage (V2 checks it) and an honest uptime. Every
     skill-conditional option PoB shows for the stage's skills is earned state too, listed with its
     producer and uptime (D-250): the baseline list covers such an option only while PoB hides it.
   - **Campaign reward picks** (D-265): the endgame stages carry the campaign's reward picks. For every
     reward choice a campaign stage picks (a `config.scenario` entry whose `source` is
     `campaign/progress.json#q.<key>.options[<i>]`), `m`, `b` and `n` list the same entry, the same pick, so
     the record's endgame numbers include the reward the guide tells the player to take. D2 adds them to the
     three rungs when it makes the picks (you design the endgame first); whenever you edit a plan that has
     campaign stages, keep them, and add any a campaign stage picks that a rung lacks. The run checker fails an
     endgame stage that leaves a picked choice at Nothing or picks it another way.
   - **Scenario inputs**, `{option, value, source: {file, key}, tag}`, each value the cited fact's own,
     with the fact's tag copied:
     - `enemyIsBoss` from `enemies/map-monsters.json#pob-enemy-is-boss`: the variants model mapping
       (D-119);
     - `resistancePenalty` from `campaign/limits.json#resistance-penalty.endgame` (−60, D-118);
     - the **reference hits** (D-119, D-254), waystone-scaled at the conservative top of each
       modifier's range. The area level is the rung's: 69 for the entering-maps rung's T5
       (`enemies/waystones.json#area-level.offset` plus 5, D-281, D-327), 79 for the budget variant's T15
       (`enemies/waystones.json#area-level.offset` plus 15), the top of its range for the non-budget
       variant (`enemies/waystones.json#area-level.max`). With `base` =
       `enemies/map-monsters.json#damage.by-level[<area level>]`, `increased` = the top of
       `enemies/waystones.json#mods.increased-monster-damage` and `extra` = the top of
       `enemies/waystones.json#mods.extra-damage-as-element`:
       - `enemyPhysicalDamage` = `base × (1 + increased / 100)`;
       - `enemyFireDamage`, `enemyColdDamage`, `enemyLightningDamage` and `enemyChaosDamage`, each
         `base × (1 + increased / 100) × extra / 100`: the waystone adds one of the four, and the variant
         must take any of them.

       A ledger entry per hit, id `hit_<stage id>_<type>` (`hit_m_fire`, `hit_b_physical`, `hit_n_chaos`),
       derives it:
       the formula above over the inputs `base`, `increased` and `extra`, each with its fact's tag (the
       waystone modifiers are confidence M, A-29), and no other input (no rounding input). Each hit's
       scenario entry cites the damage fact (`source` `enemies/map-monsters.json`,
       `damage.by-level[<area level>]`, with that fact's tag), and its value is a **whole number**: PoB's
       `enemy<Type>Damage` options take whole numbers, so the value is the ledger entry's value rounded to 2
       decimals, then rounded **up** (D-256): 96.4789 gives 97, and 402.000003 gives 402. The D1 gate
       checks every hit against that ceiling of its entry (a derived scenario value is V4's to re-derive,
       A-P11);
     - on a stage whose scenario is a boss (`enemyIsBoss` other than `None`), the enemy's level, armour,
       evasion, resistances and crit are scenario entries citing E1 (D-252).
8. **Survival floor** (D-118, D-119, D-234), on each of the three rungs. At the −60% penalty, fire, cold,
   lightning **and chaos** resistance are capped (`Missing<Elem>Resist` 0), and each of the five waystone-scaled reference hits
   (the scenario's whole-number value, D-256) is below its `<Type>MaximumHitTaken`. Cap chaos resistance. Only when capping it is **infeasible** (Chaos
   Inoculation; no slot can carry chaos resistance without dropping a core item or breaking the budget)
   add `chaosResistException {reason, tag}` to that stage: one sentence naming what makes the cap
   infeasible, tagged. Never add it to avoid work; a stage with the exception and a capped chaos
   resistance needs none.
9. **Prices** (`.claude/skills/build-guide/references/pricing.md`). One `prices[]` entry per filled slot
   and tree jewel of **every** rung (D-249, D-281), ids `m-<slot>`, `b-<slot>` and `n-<slot>` (`m-boots`,
   `b-ring1`, `b-jewel-<node>`). `m`'s total aims at 5 divines, `b`'s at 20 (the gate's budgets, `gates.md`).
   Two slots share an entry only when their item text is identical. Rares are affix-target searches
   (`mcp__pob2__search_trade_items` with `mods`). Reuse an entry with the same item, query and league from any plan of
   this run first. Offline: no price call; every entry is `unavailable`, note `offline run`, `league` null,
   dated the run folder's date. Pricing stopped: no call; `unavailable`, note `pricing stopped`. Count
   every call by its outcome for your return.
10. **Measure.** Validate with `mcp__pob2__guide_run {validate: "<runFolderRel>/plan.json"}` until it reports no
    error, then compile measure-only (`mcp__pob2__compile_plan {plan_path: "<runFolderRel>/plan.json",
    measure_only: true}`) and read `compile-record.json`: the floor values, `TotalEHP`, the named source's
    Count-scaled figures (`countedSkillOutputs`) beside `skillOutputs`, and the V1 and V2 verdicts. Iterate on damage once the floor holds (D-118). Never a
    final compile.
    - **A `possibly unread` line** (`mcp__pob2__check_model_coverage`) the variant depends on is confirmed by
      measurement before any hand calculation (D-243): add a temporary stage that copies the stage under
      test without that line, compile measure-only with `stages` naming the two, and compare their
      outputs. Numbers that move mean PoB reads the line. Remove the temporary stage before you finish. A
      **partly parsed** line means PoB applies none of it: hand-calculate the whole line.
11. **Ledger and claims** (D-106, C-33). Where PoB doesn't model a mechanic, a ledger entry
    `{id, claim, formula, inputs}` holds the hand calculation, its inputs tagged. Always, for `b` and `n`
    (`m` gets neither, D-281):
    - the Arbiter of Divinity report (D-116, A-30): its hits (`enemies/arbiter-of-divinity.json`) against
      each variant's `<Type>MaximumHitTaken`, and a time-to-kill range: one entry per fight-life
      assumption (`fight-life.datamined-base`, `fight-life.extrapolated`, `fight-life.community-report`),
      each its life over the named source's Count-scaled single-target figure, all confidence L;
    - the mapping signals (D-121, D-132): the clear skill's hit against `enemies/map-monsters.json#life.by-level[79]`
      times the waystone life modifier; movement speed first; areas 80 to 82 as a stated judgment.

    **Claims** (D-279). The guide renders the plan with no markers, so the plan's claims carry its sources. A
    claim exists only for a fact the guide states or a gate needs, each with a source or a citation
    (`ledger:<id>`, `price:<id>`, `data:<file>#<key>`): the mechanics the build depends on; why a support,
    node or item is in or out; the gate lines (the chaos exception's reason, a trigger into the next rung);
    rules of thumb with a number, as `judgment` claims; the buy orders (step 3). Gem levels, stage levels,
    record numbers, ledger values and prices are held already: no claim restates them. Where the build has
    them (D-277; none is required):
    - **traps**, up to 4: an interaction that breaks or kills the build, each sourced to item text, gem text
      or the record;
    - **either/or picks**, up to 2: two options for one slot or support, the deciding factor measured as a
      PoB damage or survival difference (a measure-only compile with a temporary stage for the other option,
      as step 10 measures a `possibly unread` line), the claim citing that comparison, and online the price
      difference;
    - **fallbacks** you already weighed while designing: the affix to give up first, or the cheaper item.
12. **Affix sources** (D-277). When an affix target can't roll on every base of its slot, say where it comes
    from. Look the affix up with `mcp__pob2__lookup_game_data {mode: "mechanic", query: <the affix text>,
    kind: "mod"}`: under each `[mod]` hit, `Base types:` lists the bases that can roll it; and with
    `kind: "essence"`, each `[essence]` hit names an essence and the item types it applies that mod to
    (`.claude/skills/build-guide/references/lookups.md`). When the base types, or the essences' item types,
    narrow the base, add a claim that names the affix's source and the base it needs ("Arcane Surge on Crit
    is desecrated, so the gloves need energy shield"), tagged `pob-data` with the lookup as its ref, and fit
    the slot's item to that base. Write affix targets as text: no tier labels and no trade links.
13. **Claim.** `done` when the three rungs are written and you expect them to pass. `infeasible` only when
    this card can't meet the survival floor, a budget, or the named damage source as the main source of
    damage at any arrangement you tried; give the reason in a sentence. The gate still runs and decides.

Offline mode: no WebSearch, WebFetch or price tool; a claim that would need an external source is tagged
`external-unavailable` (ref exactly `external source unavailable (offline run)`, with a confidence).

## The fix round and revisions (D-264)

With V4 findings or the user's requests in `findings`, you edit the plan in place; you never write it anew.

1. **Fix the claims in place**, with Edit. For each finding, fix every claim the issue touches, wherever it
   is in your stages, not only the flagged location: its wording, its citation (`source`), its caveat, or
   its source itself.
2. **Recompile only for a build change.** A fix changes the build when it changes gear (an item, a tree
   jewel, a flask or charm, or its price entry, which the D1 gate re-prices), the tree, the gems, the config
   (earned or scenario), or a ledger entry's formula or inputs. Make that change and measure it as step 10
   does; the D1 gate and the D2 gate run after you.
3. **Otherwise compile nothing.** A claims-only edit changes only `stages[].claims` and `source` and `tag`
   fields; anything else, a ledger entry's `claim` text, a respec's note or a `chaosResistException`'s reason
   included, takes step 2's path. After your edits, validate once
   (`mcp__pob2__guide_run {validate: "<runFolderRel>/plan.json"}`) so the plan still validates, and leave
   `compile-record.json` and the PoB file as the final compile left them: no gate runs after you, and the
   run checker requires the sha1 of the plan without its claims, sources and tags to equal the final
   record's `buildSha1` (D-266).

## Writes

`plan.json` only. The server writes the record and the XML beside it.

## Returns

At most 8 lines: your claim, `done` or `infeasible` with its reason in a sentence; how many
`mcp__pob2__compile_plan` calls you made; your price calls counted by outcome (`pricing.md`); `league
unverified` when no league could be verified online (step 2); and in the fix round or a revision,
`recompiled: yes` or `recompiled: no`, the finding ids you fixed and the locations you changed
(`plan.json stages[3].claims[2]`, `plan.json ledger[4]`).

