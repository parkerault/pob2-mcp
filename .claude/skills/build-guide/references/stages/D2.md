# D2: the campaign stages (`build-guide-coordinator`)

D2 plans the campaign backward from the endgame rungs D1 wrote, from the entering-maps rung `m` (D-281): the
stages a player follows from level 1 to the endgame, each a PoB2 loadout valid at its level (V1) with its config
justified (V2). **The endgame gets the effort** (D-354, carrying out the user's D-346 and D-348): per act, D2
plans the tree path order, the support gem order, a rough upgrade order, the build-specific mods to target and
a target life, and the loadouts themselves stay in the PoB file. The campaign gets no resistance, max-hit or
EHP planning and no crafting: the player buys gear on trade. A fresh D2 gate step (`D2-gate.md`) then checks the
whole plan and runs the final compile.

## Inputs

- **Header:** `stage: "D2"`, `attempt`, `concept`, `mode`, `runFolder`, `runFolderRel`,
  `compile: "measure-only"`, `writes: ["plan.json"]`, and:
  - `findings`: on a retry, the earlier D2 gate attempt's verdict file and its findings (`{part, code,
    stage, message}`; fix what each names, a `final-compile` one included); a note when an earlier attempt
    returned without writing; on a guided retry, the escalated verdict's findings followed by the user's
    guidance (`code: "user-guidance"`), which you follow;
  - `findings`, also: in the fix round, the V4 findings routed to this step (`{id, artifact, location,
    claim, issue, evidence, hinge, owner}`); after a revision at the final gate, the user's requests mapped to
    this step (`code: "user-request"`), which you make. The prompt says which; "The fix round and revisions"
    below says how;
  - `accepted`: findings the user accepted at an escalation. They need no fix.
- **Prompt:** how to price in this run (offline; stopped; or paced).
- **Read**, the independent calls in one response:
  - the rungs through `mcp__pob2__guide_run`'s views, never the plan whole: `mcp__pob2__guide_run {view: {plan:
    "<runFolderRel>/plan.json", stage: "<id>"}}` for `m`, `b` and `n`, batched. A view gives the stage's
    section of the plan (its tree, skills, items and config) and its key-figure line from the record. Its JSON
    isn't `plan.json`'s formatting: to anchor an Edit, Grep or Read `plan.json` at that place. In the fix round,
    the claims too: `mcp__pob2__guide_run {view: {plan: "<runFolderRel>/plan.json", claims: true}}`;
  - the card `cards/<id>.md` (its chain and enabler: what arrives when), and the card's row of `dossier.md`,
    the verification gate's comparison table (Grep `^| <id> |`);
  - `run-log.json`: has pricing stopped? (a `stages[]` entry whose `priceOutcomes` counts a `locked_out` or
    `rate_limited` call). Prices to reuse: Grep this run's plans (`plan.json`, `concepts/*/plan.json`) for
    `"prices"` and Read the hits;
  - the C1 facts by key, never a file whole (Grep the key, then Read its fact): `data/campaign/progress.json`
    (`order`, the progress keys in order; per quest `q.<key>.region`, `.area-level`, `.reward`,
    `.passive-points`, `.weapon-set-points`, `.options` and `.pob-config-key`), `data/campaign/areas.json`
    (`<act>.area-levels`), `data/campaign/ascendancy.json` (`trial.<n>.available-from` and `.points`),
    `data/campaign/gems.json` (the uncut-gem availability, `tier-rule` and `support-sockets.tree-granted`) and
    `data/campaign/limits.json` (`resistance-penalty.<region>`);
  - `docs/build-guide/plan-reference.md` by section, never whole: Grep `^#` for its headings and Read "Progress
    keys" and "Tree tracks"; when `validate` names a field, Grep that field's table row.

## Work

1. **Stages, backward from the endgame** (D-104, D-281). Add the campaign stages to `plan.json` before the
   endgame rungs `m`, `b` and `n`, in progression order. The campaign's last stage leads into `m`, the
   player's setup just after the campaign: plan backward from it. One stage per act, and one more where play
   changes inside an act: a new skill or support, an ascendancy trial, a respec or a key unique. Ids `1`, `2`,
   … in plan order; `loadoutName` `<where> (L<level>) {<id>}`, e.g. `Act 2 Keth (L22) {3}`.
2. **Progress and level.** Each stage's `progress` is the last C1 quest it has passed (`start`, or a
   `q.<key>` of `data/campaign/progress.json`'s order), and its `level` one a player has there (the area
   levels in `data/campaign/areas.json`; interludes add the dynamic +8, confidence M, A-29).
3. **Skills, bridge skills and the support gem order** (D-110). The named damage source (the plan's
   `roll.skill`) takes over once a player can use it; until then a **bridge skill** carries the campaign. The
   named source is level-gated by its gem's level requirement (`mcp__pob2__lookup_game_data {mode: "gem"}`
   gives it per gem level) and by its enabler, when the card names one that arrives later (an ascendancy trial,
   a unique, a gem tier, a support it needs). Plan one or more bridge skills for the stages before it, each a
   skill the stage's uncut gems allow, chosen to use what the build already invests in. Keep the main group's
   index the endgame stages use (`skills.main.group`, the same in every stage): before the named source, that
   group holds the bridge skill. Set `skills.rolledGroup` wherever a stage has the named damage source, and
   there `skills.main.group` is the `rolledGroup` (S7: the PoB file opens on the main skill). Every group has a
   `role` (`leveling` for a skill that leaves later).
   - **The support gem order** (D-354). In each group, list the supports in the order a player adds them; a
     later stage keeps the earlier supports first and adds its new ones after them. That order, stage by stage,
     is the support gem order the guide states.
   - **Gem levels** only as the uncut gems available at `progress` allow (`data/campaign/gems.json`; V1's
     `gem-availability`), with the gem's level requirement at or below the stage level. A skill gem's level is
     at least its tier (the gem lookup shows it, D-258): an uncut gem of level N cuts a skill of tier N or lower
     at gem level N (`campaign/gems.json#tier-rule`; V1 reports a gem below it as `gem-below-tier`, D-259).
   - **Supports on a granted skill** (D-359). A skill an item grants holds the supports its item's sockets
     allow. A skill a passive grants (the tree or the ascendancy) holds at most the estimated count at the
     stage's level: 3 supports below level 40, 4 from 40, 5 from 60 (`campaign/gems.json#support-sockets.tree-granted`,
     a judgment of confidence L). V1's `support-sockets` reports a group over it. Jeweller's Orbs add sockets to
     an item, never to a skill a passive grants: plan none for it.
4. **The tree path** (D-144, D-282, D-354). Each stage's tree contains the previous stage's, so the trees grow
   toward the endgame. In `tree.nodes`, keep the previous stage's nodes first, in their order, then the stage's
   new nodes in the order a player takes them: that order is the tree path the guide states. Name a passive node
   by the name the tool gives it, never by one of its stat lines.
   - **The tree's shape** (D-360, as D1-build states it), on every stage: a tree is a connected group of
     allocated nodes, the class start not counted; a start holds at most one tree; an alternate start is opened
     only while its node is allocated; the ascendancy tree is connected to its start. V1's `tree-shape` refuses
     the rest, naming the stage, the start and the trees.
   - **Respec.** Where a stage drops nodes, it carries `respec {dropped, goldCost, note}`: exactly the dropped
     node ids, a gold cost with a `judgment` tag (no source table exists, A-32), and a `note` naming the
     dropped nodes as the tool names them and saying why (W1 reads no node ids). The endgame is one
     track, the ladder: `m` continues from the last campaign stage, `b` from `m` and `n` from `b`, and V1 checks
     each rung's respec and its gold cost. If `m`'s tree doesn't contain the last campaign tree, add the
     `respec` to `m`; change nothing else in `m`, `b` or `n` but the reward picks of step 7.
   - **Points** within `level − 1` plus the quests' points up to `progress`; weapon-set points and ascendancy
     points only as C1's quests and trials allow (V1's `passive-points`, `weapon-set-points`,
     `ascendancy-points`).
5. **Gear: the upgrade order and the build's mods** (D-109, D-354, D-348). Bases and simple rares a player
   finds or buys cheaply while leveling; uniques only if buyable in a trade league (a dated listing shows
   it), never hard-to-get drops. Item level requirements at or below the stage level; attributes enough for
   every gem and item (V1's `attributes`).
   - **The build-specific mods** are a rare's mod lines: what this build's skills and stages use (its damage
     type and skill levels, the attributes its gems and items need, spirit for its buffs, life, movement speed
     on boots). Never pick a mod or an item for a resistance it lacks: the campaign plans no resistance.
   - **The rough upgrade order**: list a stage's new or changed slots first in `items.slots`, in the order a
     player should buy them.
   - **No crafting** (D-348): no item-base or mod-tier search, no crafting step. The guide names the mods to buy
     on trade.
6. **Target life** (D-354). The stage's `Life` in the record, its key-figure line after the compile, is the
   target life the guide states. Plan life mods so it rises act by act. No max-hit, EHP or resistance target
   in the campaign.
7. **Config** (C-32, D-114).
   - Scenario: `enemyIsBoss` from `enemies/map-monsters.json#pob-enemy-is-boss`, and `resistancePenalty`
     from `campaign/limits.json#resistance-penalty.<region>` of the stage's progress key: V1 requires it
     (`resistance-penalty`), and it changes no pick. At a region's last progress key either region's value is
     accepted (D-251).
   - Quest rewards: a stage before a quest lists that quest's reward option `false` (V2's
     `quest-not-reached`).
   - **Reward choices** (D-265): pick every campaign reward choice C1 lists, each a quest with a
     `q.<key>.options` fact in `data/campaign/progress.json`, its PoB option the quest's
     `q.<key>.pob-config-key`. Pick the option this build gains most from: what its skills use, or the
     defence its endgame leans on. List the pick in `config.scenario` of **every** stage at or after the quest,
     the same pick on each, the endgame stages `m`, `b` and `n` included (D1 designed them before any pick
     existed, and their numbers must include the reward): `{"option": <the pob-config-key's value>, "value":
     <the picked option, exactly as the fact lists it>, "source": {"file": "campaign/progress.json", "key":
     "q.<key>.options[<i>]"}, "tag": <the fact's tag>}`. A stage before the quest lists nothing for it (PoB's
     default is Nothing). The run checker fails a plan whose campaign passes a choice without that pick on
     every stage after it, or that picks it two ways.
   - Earned state as D1 has it: producer and uptime for every state that is on, and every skill-conditional
     option PoB shows for the stage's skills (D-250).
8. **Prices**: each campaign unique, one `prices[]` entry each, id `<stage>-<slot>`. Reuse an entry with the
   same item, query and league from any plan of this run first; otherwise `mcp__pob2__get_item_price`, one item
   per call.
   - An entry: `{id, item, query, amount, currency, date, league, status}`, `league` the plan's, `date` the
     result's `Checked:` date; `unavailable` has `amount` and `currency` null and a `note`.
   - Keep the plan's `league` as D1 set it: when it is `unverified`, D2 keeps it so, and records every campaign
     price under it (its entries' `league` is `unverified` too); D2 never picks a league of its own.
   - Classify a failed call by its `[trade:<kind>]` prefix only: `would_wait` (price others, call it again
     later; its third refusal makes it `unavailable`, note `rate-limited`); `locked_out` or `rate_limited` (stop
     pricing for the rest of the run: every remaining item `unavailable`); any other kind, `unavailable`.
   - Offline: no call; `unavailable`, note `offline run`, `league` null, dated the run folder's date. Pricing
     stopped: no call; `unavailable`, note `pricing stopped`. Count every call by its outcome for your return.
9. **Claims** (D-351, as D-354 applies it to the campaign). A claim is written only where the build hinges,
   and its `kind` says which. What the compile proves carries no claim: gem levels and availability, trial
   timing, requirements, attributes, stage levels, record numbers, tree lists and prices, which V1 checks and
   the guide renders from the plan and the record. Superfluous content is removed, not claimed: no resistance,
   max-hit or EHP line for the campaign, no kill time, no minor pick.
   - `hinge`: a campaign fact whose being wrong would break the build at its stage: a trap (an interaction
     that switches the skill off or kills the character), or an enabler the named source can't work without
     before it arrives.
   - `reason`, two kinds only, because the guide's writer has no tree tool and the plan holds no text but
     claims: **the path**, one claim on each campaign stage whose tree adds a notable or keystone, naming them in
     the order taken, each by the name `mcp__pob2__search_tree_nodes` gives it, with what it does for the build
     in a few words, tagged `pob-data` with the lookup as its ref; and **each reward pick** of step 7, one
     claim on the first stage that lists it: the quest, the option taken and a one-line reason, citing
     `data:campaign/progress.json#q.<key>.options[<i>]`. W1 names the pick under Campaign route and rewards.
   - **Sources.** Each claim has a `source` tag `{kind, ref, confidence?}` (`pob-computed` with a record field
     `<stageId>.<Field>`, `pob-data` with a lookup result, `judgment` with its reasoning and a confidence) or
     cites `price:<id>` or `data:<file>#<key>`. Its source supports every part of it. A claim citing a fact
     whose `note` records a dispute carries the dispute (both readings), not one side as settled.
   - Offline: no WebSearch, WebFetch or price tool; a claim that would need an external source is tagged
     `external-unavailable` (ref exactly `external source unavailable (offline run)`, with a confidence).
10. **Measure.** Validate with `mcp__pob2__guide_run {validate: "<runFolderRel>/plan.json"}` until it reports
    no error; compile measure-only (`mcp__pob2__compile_plan {plan_path: "<runFolderRel>/plan.json",
    measure_only: true}`, with `stages` for a subset while you work). Its reply gives each stage's V1 and V2
    verdict and key-figure line (the life step 6 reads): read them there, and Grep `compile-record.json`'s
    `stages.<id>.v1.findings` and `stages.<id>.v2.findings` only for the findings the reply leaves out. Fix
    what they find. Never a final compile.

## The fix round and revisions (D-264, D-352)

With V4 findings or the user's requests in `findings`, you edit the plan in place; you never write it anew.

1. **Fix the claims in place**, with Edit. For each finding, fix every claim the issue touches, wherever it
   is in your stages, not only the flagged location: its wording, its citation (`source`), its caveat, or
   its source itself. A `hinge: no` finding is fixed by removing or narrowing the statement, never by new
   research or a recompile.
2. **Recompile only for a build change**: gear (an item, a tree jewel, a flask or charm, or a price entry), the
   tree, the gems or the config (earned or scenario, a reward pick included). Make that change and measure it
   as step 10 does; the D2 gate runs after you.
3. **A ledger edit needs no recompile.** A change to a ledger entry's formula, inputs or claim text needs no
   recompile and no gate: the record's `buildSha1` leaves out the ledger, and the run checker re-evaluates
   the plan's current ledger (D-352).
4. **Otherwise compile nothing.** A claims-only edit changes only `stages[].claims`, `source` and `tag` fields
   and the `ledger`; anything else, a respec's note included, is a build change (step 2). After your edits,
   validate once (`mcp__pob2__guide_run {validate: "<runFolderRel>/plan.json"}`) so the plan still validates,
   and leave `compile-record.json` and the PoB file as the final compile left them: no gate runs after you, and
   the run checker requires the sha1 of the plan without its claims, sources, tags and ledger to equal the
   final record's `buildSha1` (D-266, D-352).

When D1 recompiled before you in the same round, the D1 gate's compile replaced the final record: your D2
gate runs whatever you change, so measure as step 10 does.

## Writes

`plan.json` only: the campaign stages, their prices and claims, and a `respec` on an endgame stage where
step 4 needs one. The server writes the record and the XML beside it.

## Returns

At most 8 lines: `done`, the campaign stage ids in plan order, how many `mcp__pob2__compile_plan` calls you
made, your price calls counted by outcome, and in the fix round or a revision, `recompiled: yes` or
`recompiled: no`, the finding ids you fixed and the locations you changed.

```
D2 r1-a-2 attempt 1: done
stages: 1, 2, 3, 4
compiles: 3
prices: none (offline)
```

## Fault directives

None.
