# D2: the campaign stages (`build-guide-coordinator`)

Slice 03 §5.3 step 3; spine C-36, D-104, D-108…D-110, D-131, D-138, D-144, D-277, D-279, D-281, D-282. D2
plans the campaign backward from the endgame rungs D1 wrote, from the entering-maps rung `m` (D-281): the
stages a player follows from level 1 to the endgame, each a PoB2 loadout that is valid at its level (V1) with
its config justified (V2). A fresh D2 gate step (`D2-gate.md`) then checks the whole plan and runs the final
compile.

## Inputs

- **Header:** `stage: "D2"`, `attempt`, `concept`, `mode`, `runFolder`, `runFolderRel`,
  `compile: "measure-only"`, `writes: ["plan.json"]`, and:
  - `findings`: on a retry, the earlier D2 gate attempt's verdict file and its findings (`{part, code,
    stage, message}`; fix what each names, a `final-compile` one included); a note when an earlier attempt
    returned without writing; on a guided retry, the escalated verdict's findings followed by the user's
    guidance (`code: "user-guidance"`), which you follow;
  - `findings`, also: in the fix round, the V4 findings routed to this step (`{id, artifact, location,
    claim, issue, evidence, owner}`); after a checkpoint-2 revision, the user's requests mapped to this step
    (`code: "user-request"`), which you make. The prompt says which; "The fix round and revisions" below
    says how;
  - `accepted`: findings the user accepted at an escalation. They need no fix.
- **Prompt:** how to price in this run.
- **Read:**
  - `plan.json` (D1's endgame stages `m`, `b` and `n`: their trees, skills, items and config), `dossier.md`
    (the concept's entry and `Mechanics`);
  - the C1 data under `data/campaign/`: `data/campaign/progress.json` (the progress keys in order, with each
    quest's region, area level, reward, points and PoB config key), `data/campaign/areas.json`,
    `data/campaign/ascendancy.json` (the trials), `data/campaign/gems.json` (uncut-gem availability) and
    `data/campaign/limits.json` (the resistance penalty per region);
  - `docs/build-guide/plan-reference.md`; `run-log.json` and the run's other plans, for prices;
  - `.claude/skills/build-guide/references/lookups.md` and `.claude/skills/build-guide/references/pricing.md`.

## Work

1. **Stages, backward from the endgame** (D-104, D-281). Add the campaign stages to `plan.json` before the
   endgame rungs `m`, `b` and `n`, in progression order. The campaign's last stage leads into `m`, the
   player's setup just after the campaign: plan backward from it. One stage per campaign milestone that changes what the player does: a new
   skill or support, an ascendancy trial, a respec, a key unique, or a big resistance step (an act
   boundary). Ids `1`, `2`, … in plan order; `loadoutName` `<where> (L<level>) {<id>}`, e.g.
   `Act 2 Keth (L22) {3}` (D-103, D-138).
2. **Progress and level.** Each stage's `progress` is the last C1 quest it has passed (`start`, or a
   `q.<key>` of `data/campaign/progress.json`'s order), and its `level` one a player has there (the area
   levels in `data/campaign/areas.json`; interludes add the dynamic +8, confidence M, A-29).
3. **Skills** (D-110). The leveling skill carries until the rolled skill's level requirement
   (`mcp__pob2__lookup_game_data {mode: "gem"}` gives it per gem level, D-228); then the rolled skill. Keep the main
   group's index the endgame stages use (`skills.main.group`, the same in every stage): before the rolled
   skill, that group holds the leveling skill. Set `skills.rolledGroup` wherever a stage has the rolled
   skill, and there `skills.main.group` is the `rolledGroup` (S7: the PoB file opens on the main skill). Every group has a `role` (`leveling` for a skill that leaves later). Gem levels and supports only
   as the uncut gems available at `progress` allow (`data/campaign/gems.json`; V1's `gem-availability`),
   with the gem's level requirement at or below the stage level. A skill gem's level is at least its tier
   (the gem lookup shows it, D-258): an uncut gem of level N cuts a skill of tier N or lower at gem level N
   (`campaign/gems.json#tier-rule`; V1 reports a gem below it as `gem-below-tier`, D-259).
4. **Tree** (D-144, D-282). Each stage's tree contains the previous stage's, so the trees grow toward the
   endgame. Where a stage drops nodes, it carries `respec {dropped, goldCost, note}`: exactly the dropped
   node ids, a gold cost with a `judgment` tag (no source table exists, A-32), and why. The endgame is one
   track, the ladder: `m` continues from the last campaign stage, `b` from `m` and `n` from `b`, and V1 checks
   each rung's respec and its gold cost. If `m`'s tree doesn't contain the last campaign tree, add the
   `respec` to `m`; change nothing else in `m`, `b` or `n` but the reward picks of step 6. Points within `level − 1` plus the quests' points up to `progress`; weapon-set points and
   ascendancy points only as C1's quests and trials allow (V1's `passive-points`, `weapon-set-points`,
   `ascendancy-points`).
5. **Items** (D-109). Bases and simple rares a player finds while leveling; uniques only if buyable in a
   trade league (a dated listing shows it), never hard-to-get drops. Item level requirements at or below the
   stage level; attributes enough for every gem and item (V1's `attributes`).
6. **Config** (C-32, D-114).
   - Scenario: `enemyIsBoss` from `enemies/map-monsters.json#pob-enemy-is-boss`, and `resistancePenalty`
     from `campaign/limits.json#resistance-penalty.<region>` of the stage's progress key (V1's
     `resistance-penalty`). At a region's last progress key either region's value is accepted (D-251).
   - Quest rewards: a stage before a quest lists that quest's reward option `false` (V2's
     `quest-not-reached`).
   - **Reward choices** (D-265): pick every campaign reward choice C1 lists, each a quest with a
     `q.<key>.options` fact in `data/campaign/progress.json`, its PoB option the quest's
     `q.<key>.pob-config-key`. Pick the option this build gains most from: a resistance it lacks at that
     point, the defence its endgame leans on, or what its skills use. List the pick in `config.scenario` of
     **every** stage at or after the quest, the same pick on each, the endgame stages `m`, `b` and `n`
     included (D1 designed them before any pick existed, and their numbers must include the reward): `{"option":
     <the pob-config-key's value>, "value": <the picked option, exactly as the fact lists it>, "source":
     {"file": "campaign/progress.json", "key": "q.<key>.options[<i>]"}, "tag": <the fact's tag>}`. A stage
     before the quest lists nothing for it (PoB's default is Nothing). The run checker fails a plan whose
     campaign passes a choice without that pick on every stage after it, or that picks it two ways.
   - Earned state as D1 has it: producer and uptime for every state that is on, and every skill-conditional
     option PoB shows for the stage's skills (D-250).
7. **Prices** (`.claude/skills/build-guide/references/pricing.md`): each campaign unique, one entry each
   (id `<stage>-<slot>`), reusing this run's entries first. Keep the plan's `league` as D1 set it: when it
   is `unverified`, D2 keeps it so, and records every campaign price under it (its entries'
   `league` is `unverified` too); D2 never picks a league of its own. Offline: no call; `unavailable`, note
   `offline run`, `league` null, dated the run folder's date. Pricing stopped (a `run-log.json` `stages[]`
   entry's `priceOutcomes` counts a `locked_out` or `rate_limited` call): no call; `unavailable`, note
   `pricing stopped`. Count every call by its outcome for your return.
8. **Claims** (D-279). The guide renders the plan with no markers, so the plan's claims carry its sources,
   and a claim exists only for a fact the guide states or a gate needs: each campaign stage's gem and trial
   timing, its requirements, its tree reasons, its swap trigger and its respec, each with a source or a
   citation. Gem levels, stage levels, record numbers and prices are held already: no claim restates them.
   Where the build has them (D-277; none is required): a threshold trigger the plan holds (a stat, an item
   owned, a level), with what to keep until then; a hold row (a gem kept at a lower level, or an investment
   delayed, with why) or a save row (what to stockpile before a swap); a rotation clause for a stage whose
   damage is a combo; a fallback you weighed. Each reward pick of step 6 has one
   claim, on the first stage that lists it: the quest, the option taken and a one-line reason, citing
   `data:campaign/progress.json#q.<key>.options[<i>]`; W1 names it under Campaign route and rewards. Name a passive node by the name the
   tool gives it, never by one of its stat lines. A claim citing a fact whose `note` records a dispute
   carries the dispute (both readings), not one side as settled.
9. **Measure.** Validate with `mcp__pob2__guide_run {validate: "<runFolderRel>/plan.json"}` until it reports no error;
   compile measure-only (`mcp__pob2__compile_plan {plan_path: "<runFolderRel>/plan.json", measure_only: true}`, with
   `stages` for a subset while you work), read each stage's `v1` and `v2` verdict in
   `compile-record.json`, and fix what they find. Never a final compile.

Offline mode: no WebSearch, WebFetch or price tool; a claim that would need an external source is tagged
`external-unavailable` (ref exactly `external source unavailable (offline run)`, with a confidence).

## The fix round and revisions (D-264)

With V4 findings or the user's requests in `findings`, you edit the plan in place; you never rewrite it.

1. **Fix the claims in place**, with Edit. For each finding, fix every claim the issue touches, wherever it
   is in your stages, not only the flagged location: its wording, its citation (`source`), its caveat, or
   its source itself.
2. **Recompile only for a build change.** A fix changes the build when it changes gear (an item, a flask or
   charm, or its price entry), the tree, the gems, the config (earned or scenario, a reward pick included),
   or a ledger entry's formula or inputs. Make that change and measure it as step 9 does; the D2 gate runs
   after you.
3. **Otherwise compile nothing.** A claims-only edit changes only `stages[].claims` and `source` and `tag`
   fields; anything else, a ledger entry's `claim` text, a respec's note or a `chaosResistException`'s reason
   included, takes step 2's path. After your edits, validate once
   (`mcp__pob2__guide_run {validate: "<runFolderRel>/plan.json"}`) so the plan still validates, and leave
   `compile-record.json` and the PoB file as the final compile left them: no gate runs after you, and the
   run checker requires the sha1 of the plan without its claims, sources and tags to equal the final
   record's `buildSha1` (D-266).

When D1 recompiled before you in the same round, the D1 gate's compile replaced the final record: your D2
gate runs whatever you change, so measure as step 9 does.

## Writes

`plan.json` only: the campaign stages, their prices and claims, and a `respec` on an endgame stage where
step 4 needs one. The server writes the record and the XML beside it.

## Returns

At most 8 lines: `done`, the campaign stage ids in plan order, how many `mcp__pob2__compile_plan` calls you
made, your price calls counted by outcome (`pricing.md`), and in the fix round or a revision,
`recompiled: yes` or `recompiled: no`, the finding ids you fixed and the locations you changed.

```
D2 c1 attempt 1: done
stages: 1, 2, 3, 4
compiles: 3
prices: none (offline)
```

## Fault directives

None.
