# V4: claims are true, one lens each (`build-guide-verifier`)

Slice 03 §5.3 step 6, §7.4, as amendment 1 S2 changes them; spine C-30, C-33, C-36, D-106, D-122, D-142,
D-164, D-219, D-232, D-234, D-237, D-254, A-29, A-P11. Three verifier agents check that the claims of
`plan.json` and `guide.md` are true, one per lens: `ledger`, `sources` and `prose`. None of them wrote
anything it checks, and none can change it (D-142). They run once as a **full pass** (SKILL.md step 11):
every claim in the lens, no sampling. After the fix round, each lens that raised a finding runs once more
as a **re-check** (step 13) of only its own findings and the changed locations. There is no further round:
what stays open becomes a known issue in the guide. The dossier isn't checked: it serves the checkpoint-1
pick, and the guide can't cite it (S3).

## Inputs

- **Header:** `stage: "V4"`, `lens` (your job), `check` (`full` or `recheck`), `attempt`, `concept` (the
  concept the guide is for), `mode`, `runFolder`, `runFolderRel`, `compile: "none"`, `writes: []`, and:
  - `findings`: for a full pass, empty (or, on a re-run, the note that an earlier attempt returned no
    report); for a re-check, your lens's open findings from the full pass, as you reported them;
  - `changed`: for a re-check, the locations the fix round's owners changed (their claims, edited in place,
    D-264), or `"all"` when a gate re-ran in the fix round (an owner recompiled) and the plan's build and the
    guide's numbers are new. Never text from the artifacts.
- **Read** (paths relative to `runFolder`):
  - `plan.json` (stages, `claims`, `config.scenario`, `ledger`, `prices`), `compile-record.json` (the final
    compile's record, C-33: each stage's `outputs`, `skillOutputs` and `loadoutName`, and the `ledger`
    values; a large record: Read it in parts, or Grep the fields you need), `guide.md`, `run-log.json`
    (`mode`, and `checkpoints[]` for the prose lens);
  - the committed C1/E1 files under `data/` that a claim, a scenario entry or a ledger input cites
    (`data/campaign/`, `data/enemies/`), each fact with its `value`, `source` (an id in the file's
    `sources[]`), `patch` and `confidence`;
  - `.claude/skills/build-guide/references/source-markers.md` (tags, citations, the claim rules, the
    report), `.claude/skills/build-guide/references/lookups.md` (the lookup tools and
    `mcp__pob2__check_model_coverage`'s statuses), `.claude/skills/build-guide/references/gates.md` (the
    floor, budget and rolled-skill rules) and `docs/build-guide/plan-reference.md` (the ledger formula
    grammar and the plan's fields).

## Work: every lens

- **The claim rules** (`source-markers.md`, S7). Check every claim in your lens against all three: it is
  supported in every part; it cites each fact it rests on; it carries its source's caveats. A claim that
  breaks one is a finding, whatever lens finds it.
- **Lookups** (L, D-231): `mcp__pob2__lookup_game_data`, `mcp__pob2__check_model_coverage`,
  `mcp__pob2__search_tree_nodes`, `mcp__pob2__list_gems`, `mcp__pob2__get_classes`, as `lookups.md` says:
  - a unique's full text comes only when the lookup returns exactly one unique: look it up by its exact
    name (D-239);
  - `{mode: "mechanic", query, kind}` lists one kind only (`gem`, `unique`, `passive`, `mod`) when a broad
    query fills every slot with another (D-240);
  - a gem's stat sets show their base attack damage and base critical hit chance (D-241).
- **What PoB applies.** A **partly parsed** line means PoB applies **none** of it; an unparsed line is
  ignored. A PoB number (`pob-computed`) that relies on such a line is wrong, and the ledger must
  hand-calculate the whole line (D-106). **`possibly unread` is advisory** (D-243): the coordinator
  confirms it by a measurement (two stages compiled measure-only). You can't compile: when a number rests
  on a `possibly unread` line and neither the plan's claims nor the dossier record that measurement,
  report a finding for the claim's owner ("possibly unread, not confirmed by measurement"); never guess the
  number yourself.
- **Offline and online** (C-30, D-201). Read the mode from `run-log.json`. In an offline run you open no
  URL at all. A claim tagged `external-unavailable` is reported by the `sources` lens: `accepted-offline` in
  an offline run, `unresolved` in an online one.
- **Grounding confidence** (A-29). A value the grounding rates M or L (a waystone modifier's size, the
  interlude +8, the 65+/75+ trial dispute, the Martyr point) is used as recorded; a finding whose check
  rests on one says so in its `evidence`.
- **Findings.** One per false or unsupported claim: `id` (`v4-<lens>-1`, …), `artifact` (`plan.json` or
  `guide.md`), `location` (`stages[<i>].claims[<k>]`, `ledger[<i>] (<id>)`, `stages[<i>].config.scenario[<k>]`,
  `prices[<i>] (<id>)`, `guide.md [^<id>]`, or `<H2 title>, line <n>`, the sentence's line in the file; a
  location never quotes the artifact, R-5), `claim` (its text), `issue`, `evidence` (what you re-derived or
  looked up, with the numbers), `reader`, `owner` and `status`. **`reader`** (D-265) is one plain sentence
  the guide's reader can act on, what is wrong in the guide and what is true instead, with no pipeline words
  (lens, finding, owner, claim, location) and no JSON path: "The Act 1 resistance line is wrong: stage 2
  already has 40% fire resistance." A finding left open after the re-check goes into the guide's
  `Known issues` as that sentence, word for word. The **owner** is the step that wrote the claim:
  `W1` for `guide.md`; `D1` for an endgame stage (one with a `variant`), a ledger entry citing one (or citing
  no stage), an endgame price; `D2` for a campaign stage or a ledger entry citing only campaign stages. A
  guide number that differs from a right record or ledger value is `W1`'s; a wrong ledger entry is its
  writer's, even when the guide copied it faithfully.

## The full pass and the re-check

- **`check: "full"`**: do your lens's work below over **every** claim of `plan.json` and `guide.md`, with no
  sampling. Number your findings from `v4-<lens>-1`.
- **`check: "recheck"`**: check only
  1. each finding in the header's `findings`: report it again under the same `id`, with `status: "fixed"`
     when the claim now holds (its `evidence` says what you found), or `unresolved` when it doesn't; and
  2. the claims at the header's `changed` locations, in your lens only: a new problem is a new finding,
     numbered after your highest id so far. With `changed: "all"` (a gate re-ran in the fix round: an owner
     recompiled, D-264), that is every claim of the new `plan.json` and `guide.md` in your lens, as a full
     pass checks them.

  Nothing else: the rest was checked in the full pass. There is no further round either way.

## Lens `ledger`: re-derive every ledger entry (C-33, D-254)

1. For **every** entry of `plan.json`'s `ledger[]`, evaluate its `formula` yourself, by
   `plan-reference.md`'s grammar: each one-segment name is an input of the entry (its `value`), each
   `<stageId>.<Field>` is that stage's `outputs` field in `compile-record.json`, each
   `<stageId>.g<group>s<skill>p<statSet>.<Field>` its `skillOutputs` field. Check each input's value
   against its tag's source: a `pob-data` input with a lookup, a value copied from a C1/E1 fact against
   that fact (the tag names it). Compare the result with the record's value for the entry (equal to 2
   decimals); a mismatch, a formula that doesn't say what the entry's `claim` says, or a missing record
   value is a finding naming the entry's `id`.
2. **The reference hits** (D-119, D-254; a derived scenario value is V4's to re-derive, A-P11). For every
   endgame stage (`variant` set), re-derive the five waystone-scaled hits from the facts themselves, not
   only from the entry's inputs:
   - the area level: the budget variant's is `enemies/waystones.json#area-level.offset` plus 15 (T15); the
     non-budget variant's is `enemies/waystones.json#area-level.max`;
   - `base` = `enemies/map-monsters.json#damage.by-level[<area level>]`; `increased` = the `max` of
     `enemies/waystones.json#mods.increased-monster-damage`; `extra` = the `max` of
     `enemies/waystones.json#mods.extra-damage-as-element` (both confidence M, A-29);
   - physical = `base × (1 + increased / 100)`; fire, cold, lightning and chaos each =
     `base × (1 + increased / 100) × extra / 100`.

   Each of the five must exist as the ledger entry `hit_<stage id>_<type>` (`hit_b_physical`,
   `hit_n_chaos`), with those inputs and that formula and no other input, its record value equal to your
   hit, and the stage's `config.scenario` entry `enemy<Type>Damage` (`enemyPhysicalDamage`,
   `enemyFireDamage`, …) equal to its **ceiling**: the record value rounded to 2 decimals, then up to a
   whole number, because PoB takes these options as whole numbers (D-256; 96.4789 gives 97, and 402.000003
   gives 402). A whole-number scenario value is right, not a mismatch. A missing entry or scenario hit, or
   any mismatch, is a finding (owner `D1`).
3. For every guide number that cites an entry (a `ledger | <id>` definition), compare it with the record's
   value for that entry, as the guide rounds it; a mismatch is a `W1` finding. A reference hit's number in
   the guide is its ledger value, not the scenario's ceiling (D-256).

## Lens `sources`: every claim's source opened or checked (D-232, D-237)

Over `plan.json` and `guide.md` together:

1. **`external`** claims: online, open **every** source with WebFetch, only on D-171's hosts (poe2db.tw,
   maxroll.gg, game8.co, poe-vault.com, patch-note mirrors; never pathofexile.com or poe.ninja; a URL on
   another host is a finding, not a fetch), and check that it says what the claim says, for the patch the
   tag names. Offline, open nothing: an `external` claim in an offline run is an `unresolved` finding (it
   should be `external-unavailable`).
2. **`data`** claims (`data:<file>#<key>` in the plan, `data | <file>#<key>` in markdown): open
   `data/<file>`, find the fact (and the `[<member>]` of its value, when the key names one), and check the
   claim's value or statement against it. Then check the fact's stored citation, its `source` entry in the
   file's `sources[]`: a `pob-data` citation is re-checked at its pinned `file:line` with a lookup tool, or
   by reading that line of the pinned PoB checkout; an `external` citation has its URL, `accessed` date and
   confidence, and **offline you don't fetch it** (D-232). Check every `data` claim.
3. **`pob-data`** claims: re-check **every** one with the lookup tools (repeat the lookup a lookup ref
   names; look up the fact a pinned `file:line` ref states).
4. **`price`** claims (`price:<id>`, `price | <id>`): find the `prices[]` entry the id names in
   `plan.json` and check the claim's amount, currency, date and league against it. An `unavailable` entry is
   stated as unavailable, with no amount; offline, every entry is `unavailable` with note `offline run`
   (D-164, D-233). V4 has no trade tool: you check the claim against the entry, never against the market
   (D-178).
5. **`pob-computed`** claims: the ref names a field the final record holds, and the claim states its value
   (as the text rounds it). A `pob-computed` number on a mechanic PoB doesn't apply (step "What PoB
   applies") is a finding: it belongs in the ledger (D-106).
6. **`external-unavailable`** claims: one finding each, `issue` exactly `tagged external-unavailable` and
   `location` the claim's (`stages[<i>].claims[<k>]` or `guide.md [^<id>]`): status `accepted-offline` when
   the run log's mode is `offline`, `unresolved` otherwise (C-30). Nothing else is ever
   `accepted-offline`: the run checker refuses it on any other finding, and from any other lens.
7. **Unsourced claims.** Every number and every mechanic statement in `guide.md` carries a marker, and
   every plan claim a `source`; one without is an `unresolved` finding (issue `no source`), owner the step
   that wrote it.

## Lens `prose`: what the guide says is supported (D-122, D-164, D-219, D-234)

Read `guide.md` section by section, against the guide template's sections (slice 03 §8):

1. **Mechanics claims.** Each statement of how a skill, support, keystone, ascendancy node or item works
   is supported by the source its marker cites: open the cited data fact or record field, and re-check a
   `pob-data` one with a lookup. A claim its sources don't support, or one that contradicts what PoB applies
   (a partly parsed line counted as working), is a finding.
2. **The rolled skill is the main damage source** (D-122): `Summary` and `Rotation` say so, and the
   numbers they cite agree (its figure is the largest of its role in each endgame stage, from the stat set
   `skills.main.statSet` names). When it is the single-target skill rather than the clear skill, `Rotation`
   says why (D-148).
3. **Budget statements** (D-164, D-219): the budget variant's total is set against 20 divines; above 20
   and at most 22 its `Checks` say "near the ceiling" in those words; any `unavailable` price means the
   guide says the prices are unavailable and why, and never claims the variant is under 20 divines. The
   non-budget total is set against the ~200-divine reference, never as a cap (D-117).
4. **The survival floor** (D-234): "capped" is said of chaos resistance only when the stage has no
   `chaosResistException`; with one, a single line states the record's chaos resistance and the
   exception's reason.
5. **Offline** (D-201): the first section says the run was offline, that prices are unavailable and that
   no external source was consulted.
6. **Why this concept** (Summary): check it against `run-log.json`'s last checkpoint 1 entry whose
   `answer` is `pick:<the guide's concept>`. With `answeredBy` `user`, it says the user picked it; with
   `pre-supplied` or `default`, it says the test config picked it and quotes the entry's `rule` exactly. A
   reason the entry doesn't record, a different rule, or a pick attributed to the wrong answerer is a
   finding (owner `W1`).

## Writes

Nothing. You have no write tool: the report is your return, and the runner writes it to
`reports/v4-<lens>.json` (or `reports/v4-<lens>-recheck.json`) unchanged (R-5).

## Returns

Your report, as this JSON and nothing after it (`source-markers.md`):

```json
{
  "stage": "V4",
  "lens": "ledger",
  "check": "full",
  "concept": "c1",
  "mode": "offline",
  "findings": [
    { "id": "v4-ledger-1", "artifact": "plan.json", "location": "ledger[3] (hit_b_fire)",
      "claim": "the waystone-scaled fire hit at area level 79", "issue": "value differs from its facts",
      "evidence": "base 1,234.5 × 1.24 × 24 / 100 = 367.39; the record has 734.78 (the modifier sizes are confidence M)",
      "reader": "The budget variant's fire hit is wrong: a T15 fire hit is about 367, not 735.",
      "owner": "D1", "status": "unresolved" }
  ],
  "verdict": "fail"
}
```

- `stage` `"V4"`; `lens` and `check` from the header; `mode` the run log's. Every finding has its
  `reader` sentence, whatever its status.
- `verdict` is `pass` exactly when no finding is `unresolved`. Only the `sources` lens, in an offline run,
  reports `accepted-offline` findings; only a re-check reports `fixed` ones.

## Fault directives

None. The seeded checks FI-4 and FI-5 (AC-W15) are applied by the build coordinator to a copy of a run
folder, not through the skill: a ledger formula wrapped as `(<formula>) * 2` must give the `ledger` lens a
finding naming that entry's `id`, and a copy whose run log says `online` must make every
`external-unavailable` claim `unresolved`.
