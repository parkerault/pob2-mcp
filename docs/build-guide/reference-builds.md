- **Video:** `GsLBMXy3tDk` in yt-library. Frames are in `~/projects/yt-library/videos/GsLBMXy3tDk/frames/`.

## perra's "Broken Off-Meta Builds" (Mobalytics, read 2026-10-10)

- **Index:** https://mobalytics.gg/poe-2/profile/perra/guides/broken-off-meta-builds, updated Jul 26, 2026.
  "There are some of the best off-meta builds in PoE2, as showcased in my video."
- **Index video:** `VGQ8TB9hQFY`.
- **What the four readable guides share:**
  - All are Gemling Legionnaire on 0.5.5.
  - All use Rakiata's Flow on the main skill (it inverts enemy elemental resistances).
  - All use Morior Invictus, the Absent Amulet (Eternal Rage or Trinity) and Virtuous Barrier.
  - All balance support colours for Gem Studded, socketing filler gems that are never used.
  - All rely on lineage supports, From Nothing, Prism of Belief and Megalomaniac.
  - Each gives weapon set 2 a non-damage job.

### 1. Mirage Sniper (J_Macc): Snipe fired by Mirage Archer, Gemling Legionnaire

- **Guide:** "0.5 Mirage Archer Snipe Gemling Legionnaire Endgame Build Guide", updated Jul 27, 2026.
- **Videos:**
  - Guide: `FUeS6JNqHrw` (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FUeS6JNqHrw&t=11s; the embed starts at 544 s).
  - "200% Deli Map Showcase": https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MgM-IHM5sJg (not fetched; gameplay only).
- **PoB:** none linked; only Mobalytics' "Download Build File".
- **Chain:**
  - Mirage Archer: "While active, dodge rolling will create a Mirage that uses socketed ranged Attacks for a
    short duration". In 0.5 it can use channelled skills such as Snipe.
  - Snipe sits in Mirage Archer with Cooldown Recovery II, Prolonged Duration II, Rakiata's Flow and Rapid
    Attacks II.
  - Perfect Timing makes the arrow explode. The guide: "Paired with the Gemling Legionnaire and quality
    stacking, Snipe gains massive attack speed, allowing the Mirage Archer to quickly hit the perfect timing
    window".
    - Advanced Thaumaturgy: "Gem Quality grants Socketed Skills an additional effect".
    - The step from quality to attack speed is inferred.
  - Up to 6 mirages: "cooldown is 3.16 seconds and duration is 21.78 seconds". Sources:
    - Uhtred's Sidereus ("Boots: Can roll Chronomancy modifiers"), with cooldown recovery and duration rolls;
    - Time-Lost Emeralds ("Notable Passive Skills in Radius also grant (1-3)% increased Cooldown Recovery
      Rate");
    - Heart of the Well;
    - From Nothing around Hollow Palm Technique.
  - Multipliers:
    - Rakiata's Flow;
    - armour break, and Uruk's Smelting on self-cast Snipe (up to 20% increased physical damage taken);
    - Window of Opportunity II (35% more on Perfect Timing);
    - Cadiro's Gambit (each arrow a random special arrow) and Countess Seske's Rune (+1 arrow);
    - Rend with power charges from Thaumaturgic Generator;
    - Pounce marks.
  - Gem Studded with red-majority supports: "Hits against you have no Critical Damage Bonus".
- **Defences:**
  - Decree of Acuity: blindness reversed, and "Gain (15-30)% of Evasion Rating as extra Armour";
  - Morior Invictus;
  - Arctic Armour, Ghost Dance and Wind Dancer.
- **Investment:** high, "Gear is pretty specific". Mageblood or Headhunter variants, Prism of Belief +2 Snipe,
  crafted emeralds, Megalomaniac, and corrupted gloves and boots. No leveling.

### 3a. Glacial Bolt, whirling version (Phylaris POE): Gemling Legionnaire

- **Guide:** "WHIRLING Glacial Bolt Gemling [POE2 0.5]", Aug 13, 2026. Tabs: Endgame, Endgame (Early),
  Leveling.
- **Videos:**
  - Guide: `7WCQ-xjkRNw`.
  - Glove craft: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1t5Y4AUIWkI (not fetched; crafting).
- **Links:**
  - PoB: https://poe.ninja/poe2/pob/25d58, plus an on-page "Path of Building Code" block.
  - Jewel trade search: https://www.pathofexile.com/trade2/search/poe2/Runes%20of%20Aldur/LgO3nbMlun
- **Chain:**
  - Glacial Bolt's ice-crystal walls are placed along fissures by Ahn's Citadel, with 3 more fissures from
    Kaom's Madness. Rakiata's Flow, Elemental Armament II and Vorana's Siege support it.
  - Whirling Slash on weapon set 1 is both movement and detonator. Its supports:
    - Biting Frost II;
    - Mórrigan's Insight ("Consume Freeze… 50% more Damage", triggers Nature's Exchange);
    - Rigwald's Ferocity;
    - Rage III.
  - More breakers:
    - Verisium Manifestations ("Hitting with an Attack will spend Runic Ward to summon a Verisium
      Manifestation") with Biting Frost II;
    - Pounce with Biting Frost II and Brutus' Brain;
    - Seismic Cry with Vruun's Aftermath ("Supported Warcries cause Ice Crystals to explode…").
  - Melee builds Rage, so no Eternal Rage is needed. Its spirit goes to Scavenged Plating, and Blasphemy
    with Temporal Chains.
  - Trinity comes from the Absent Amulet, balanced by a tri-element crossbow. Embitter is on Spear Stab.
  - Padding Gem Studded:
    - "Time of Need and Explosive Shot are disabled and just used for extra motes + support slots… via
      equipping 2 unset rings… then removing the unset rings";
    - "Gem Studded only works off your active weaponset's supports";
    - anoint priority: Augmented Flesh (2 extra skill slots) over Paragon.
- **Investment:** "Scalable from zero currency".
  - Early Endgame: Headhunter and Skysliver.
  - Endgame: Mageblood and a crafted Desolate Crossbow.
  - Leveling tab: Thrillsteel, Tabula Rasa, 2× Blackheart, Meginord's Girdle, Rampart Raptor and Wanderlust,
    with Permafrost Bolts and Fragmentation Rounds.
- **Defences:** "100k+ armour", Blasphemy with Temporal Chains, and "% of Armour also applies to Elemental
  Damage".

### 5. HotG "Infinite Bonker": "[0.5.5] Ulf's Hammer Engine" (Ulfhednar), Gemling Legionnaire

- **Guide:** Sep 1, 2026.
- **Video:** `r6yQKNRoVlI`.
- **PoBs:**
  - Olrovasara: https://poe.ninja/poe2/pob/25c7f
  - Chaos Dreamer: https://poe.ninja/poe2/pob/26184
  - An on-page "Path of Building Code" block.
- **Chain:**
  - Hammer of the Gods "Requires 20 Glory to use", and generates Glory on Heavy Stun.
  - Advanced Thaumaturgy's quality effect gives a chance not to consume Glory. With 85% plus Everlasting
    Glory's 15%, that is 100%, so the skill never spends Glory. Quality sources:
    - 20% from Gemcutting Prisms;
    - the Paragon anoint;
    - a Fox Idol or Loreweave;
    - a desecrated amulet mod;
    - 6% from small ascendancy nodes.
  - Glory is seeded from set 2's Fury of the King, which grants Molten Crash ("overcorrupted that has 6
    glory").
  - Zero mana: Defy II reads "Insufficient Mana doesn't prevent the use of Supported Skills while
    Surrounded", and Constricting Command rolled at 4 makes one enemy enough to count as Surrounded.
  - Rage: Medved's Felling (5% attack speed per 5 Rage, life lost per Rage) and Berserk (more Rage effect,
    life lost per Rage). Chaos Inoculation on a Flesh Crucible jewel removes both drawbacks.
  - HotG supports: Zerphi's Infamy ("requires 50 Glory", 50% more damage), Rakiata's Flow, Aftershock II and
    Defy II.
  - Unused gems are socketed for Virtuous Barrier motes and Gem Studded balance.
- **Variants:**
  - Olrovasara: lightning converted to cold, so Herald of Ice can run.
  - Chaos Dreamer: Giant's Blood with Palm of the Dreamer, Sacred Maul and Hateforge.
- **Defences:** CI, Decree of Flight (Guard on dodge roll), Blood of the Warrior, Arctic Armour, Ghost Dance
  and Wind Dancer.
