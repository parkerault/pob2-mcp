## Findings: what ronarray's six Martial Artist videos add beyond his written guide

Sources were the six transcripts plus six frames. The captions are machine-made, so I normalised the names: "verin/villain assault" is Whirling Assault, "Shavron satchel" is Shavronne's Satchel, and "Garukhan" is the Garukhan support. **He never opens PoB in any of the six videos.** Every number he quotes comes from the in-game skill panel. A frame at 00:58 of EbyMEBDURpE shows Whirling Assault at 54,941 in combat with Rage 30/30, against 28,893 in the hideout (02:25 frame).

### 1. Per video

**j0xi8FhKYVQ (main build, the AoE version)**
- **AoE comes last.** The AoE setup is only for when "your damage is high enough" (01:14, 15:35). He gives no number for that.
- **Crit targets.** About 80% crit is "mandatory" and anything above it doesn't matter (03:08). With the Garukhan support, crit must stay at or under 50%: drop Careful Assassin and take Critical Exploit (05:50–06:39). The two targets depend on which support you run.
- **Support ladder by budget** (05:04–06:15). The cheap set comes first, then Verana's replaces Magnified Area, then Garukhan replaces Pinpoint. Rage comes from a jewel, not the support (04:47).
- **Traps:**
  - Mana leech off the Hollow Focus bells needs "physical attack damage leeched as mana" on a ring or gloves (10:28).
  - Shavronne's Satchel needs a flask with an instant-recovery prefix (07:48).
  - Keep Herald of Ash at level 4 so its Strength requirement doesn't rise (08:34).
  - Ailith's Chimes needs Culmination socketed first (08:55).
  - The Rigwald support's weapon must sit in weapon set 2 (05:37).
- **The fallback form.** "If your gear is mid": refund Resolute Reach's 2 points into defence, losing only a little AoE (12:21–12:52).
- **Weapon affix priority with a floor** (14:39–15:12). The base needs at least 10% crit, ideally 14–15%. +4 melee levels is fine. Crit damage bonus is "the last stat that I would search". Per slot, he names what to drop on a budget (13:47).

**i6vzzrQIJ6w (leveling)**
- **A rotation per stage.** Whirling Slash three times, then Twister (01:22). Barrage goes between them (03:16). Ice-Tipped Arrows plus Frost Nexus replaces Parry (03:47–04:55).
- **Prep before the swap.** Save level-7 uncut gems ahead of the Hollow Palm swap at about 22–23 (06:56).
- **The boss charge loop, as a sequence** (08:14–09:51): shock the enemy, Siphoning Strike consumes the shock for a power charge, Rend (a talisman in weapon set 2: 50% of damage gained as lightning while you have a power charge), then Falling Thunder. Killing Palm on the bells replaces this at the first ascendancy. "Mostly for bosses" (09:57).
- **Spirit-gem picks with a deciding factor.** Clear, bossing or survival (05:55–06:37), with Herald of Ash kept low-level (06:07).
- *General or campaign practical, not build-specific:* making a Huntress first to take her spear and gem (00:34), and the Red Vale weapon racks (02:49).

**SKTko6cEckQ (T15, the swap to Whirling Assault)**
- **The swap trigger** (03:03–03:50). In maps around level 65–70, and only once you own a quarterstaff with cold plus lightning or fire and at least 300 elemental DPS. Until then, "don't respec" and keep playing Storm Wave.
- **Crit numbers.** 44% without Pinpoint and about 71% with it (04:38, confirmed by frame). The reason is that crits trigger Hollow Resonance.
- **Boss prep.** Use Killing Palm on the bells for full power charges, cast Charged Staff, then enter the arena (05:42–05:53). Trap: Charged Staff can kill the bells first, and then you get no charges (01:43).
- **Conditions he states:**
  - Herald of Ice needs lots of cold damage; otherwise run Herald of Thunder (06:54).
  - Rage can't support Hollow Resonance (02:45).
  - Keep chaos resistance at 30% or more while not on CI (10:04).
  - Don't consider CI below 4k energy shield (12:18).
- **A reward pick with a reason.** At the Act 4 shark-fin quest, take the evasion and energy shield reward, because deflection comes from other sources (00:56).
- **Evidence for our entering-maps rung.** He tells T15 players to sit between two of his written loadouts, because the next one "needs significantly better gear" (08:07–08:29).
- *General or time-bound:* Ward at the runic anvil (08:35), and the Trial of Chaos bug (13:58).

**EbyMEBDURpE (Trinity and Hollow Form)**
- **Trinity math** (01:23–02:04). 6% more elemental damage per 30 affinity, with each element capped at 100. Two elements at about 200 give about 36% more, and staying over 250 is "kind of hard". With Trinity and Rage active, his hit roughly doubles from the hideout figure (00:45–01:05).
- **How to tell Trinity is working.** On the skill panel, the per-element hit ranges must overlap. An element whose maximum is below another element's minimum adds nothing (02:14–02:51). The frame shows it: fire 776–2,036 against cold 2,039–3,790. The fix is the weapon's element pair, then rings, then "damage as extra" tree nodes (02:56–03:43).
- **Spirit** (03:49–04:32). Trinity reserves 100. With a plain amulet, run only Trinity and Herald of Ash. With about 60 spirit on the amulet, add Herald of Ice or Wind Dancer.
- **An either/or with numbers** (05:26–06:22). The charge-generating unique gloves give 16,000. A rare pair with flat damage gives 27,700 and costs 1–3 divines, so the rare wins.
- **Hard-boss sequence** (08:15–08:45). Whirling Assault a few times, bell on the boss, then Hollow Form. Map bosses die to a plain cast. Close Combat needs you near the boss (07:48).

**WLT5_uRXR60 (when to go CI)**
- **The CI trigger** (00:58–01:12, 02:01). Minimum about 4,000 energy shield, better 6,000, and he swapped at about 6,000. CI also needs about 50% evasion without Wind Dancer.
- **CI's consequence** (01:18–01:55). Life becomes 1, so take one stun-threshold node and a jewel granting "stun threshold equal to 14% of maximum energy shield". Add more only if you're still being stunned.
- **Energy shield recovery with numbers.** Satchel plus a flask with 30% applied instantly gives about 300 per press, and nine presses give about 3,000 (02:37–03:04). Recharge only works between packs. In fights, Ghost Dance with Cooldown Recovery does the work (03:24–03:53).
- **Defence upgrade order, each step with a reason** (09:31–10:43):
  1. Helmet: about 450–500 energy shield with two or three resistances. The Subterfuge Mask node turns helmet evasion into more.
  2. Body armour: the Spectral Ward node gives energy shield from its evasion.
  3. Boots last. Keep the gloves you have.
- **Ranked mana fixes and spirit options** (04:23–05:24, 12:14–13:05).
- **A finding from playing:** Hollow Keeper doesn't stop chill (07:09–07:29). *General:* the anti-slow, anti-stun and anti-freeze charms (11:16).

**L38rrIb9TsM (free Trinity)**
- **Free Trinity** (00:35–01:32). An amulet that grants level-18 Trinity frees 100 spirit for three 30-spirit auras. Fire Mastery is still needed. A Perfect flux to level 20 costs about 12 divines and is optional; that is a "hold" row (07:20–07:49).
- **A decision rule tied to Trinity balance** (06:33–07:15). Use Charged Staff more only if it doesn't break the balance, so check which element lags. Too much fire: drop Blazing Critical. Too much lightning: drop Charged Staff.
- **Tooltip against real damage** (08:16–08:39). When crit is high, Blindside instead of Pinpoint lowers the tooltip but deals more real damage, because the bells and Wind Dancer blind enemies. Pinpoint and Elemental Armament are "temporary" slots, the first to replace (08:46–09:03).
- **Stacked charge sources and an ascendancy swap.** Charges come from Wind Dancer with Culmination and Chimes, plus crit gloves (01:52–03:15). Hollow Resonance gives way to Runic Meridians for +5 to all attributes (04:16–04:29).
- *Crafting, which we hold back:* Arcane Surge desecration only rolls on energy shield bases (03:34).

### 2. Patterns across the videos

- **Thresholds come in threes: a floor, a target, and what he did.** Examples: CI at 4k, 6k, and "I swapped at 6k". Crit at 80%, with anything above unimportant. Helmet at 450–500 energy shield, 600 if you can.
- **Swap triggers are items you own or stats on the character sheet, rarely levels.** Each one comes with "until then, keep X": "don't respec" until you have the staff.
- **His "why" is a mechanism plus one number from the panel.** Examples: Trinity's 6% per 30, the 16k against 27.7k gloves, crit 44% to 71%.
- **He demonstrates rather than states:**
  - the overlap check on the skill panel;
  - Thrill of the Kill working on the bells (01:21);
  - the flask spam;
  - the Hollow Keeper chill test.
- **Every slot has a budget ladder,** and he names the temporary pick.
- **The page is a snapshot.** The videos record why the build changed (Hollow Resonance dropped, the unique gloves rejected, free Trinity) and sometimes contradict each other. A page-only reader misses the swap triggers, how to balance Trinity, the mana-fix chain, the boss prep and the traps.

### 3. Implications for amendment 2

1. **Triggers as stat thresholds with a floor and an "until then".** This goes in the reader layer's "Move here when" lines and the leveling stage sections (G1), and it strengthens D (the loadout ladder with triggers). Example: "Move here when you have a staff with at least 300 elemental DPS; until then keep Storm Wave." Evidence: SKTko6cEckQ 03:44, WLT5_uRXR60 00:58. **Clear improvement.** Energy shield, evasion, crit and weapon DPS all come from PoB. The floor is a D1 rule of thumb with a number, which claims-and-verification (G3 §4.1) already allows.
2. **The entering-maps rung (G5) for this build.** The skill swap happens at maps and is gated on a rare with a stat floor, not a unique. G5 says that when the key unique doesn't fit in 5 divines, it becomes the gate into the budget rung; that should cover key rares with a stat floor too. SKTko6cEckQ 08:07 shows the gap G5 fills. **Clear improvement**, a wording change only.
3. **"What PoB can't show" names the assumptions the headline figure needs.** PoB models Trinity only through a user-set "Total Resonance Count", capped at 300 (`vendor/PathOfBuilding-PoE2/src/Modules/ConfigOptions.lua:673`). The guide should state the count it assumes (about 200 with two elements) and that the hideout panel will show about half (EbyMEBDURpE 00:45, 01:44). **Clear improvement** for the assumption. The hideout figure needs a second PoB pass with combat buffs off, so that part is a **judgment call**.
4. **One "you'll know it's working when…" line per build-defining conditional mechanic, under "How to play it" (G1).** Evidence: the Trinity overlap check (EbyMEBDURpE 02:14). **Judgment call.** Our curated stat lists (`pob-api/API/BuildOps.lua:40-50`) don't expose per-element hit ranges, so without adding them it can only be first-person advice.
5. **H (one or two either/or picks) gets a numeric deciding factor:** a PoB damage or survival difference plus a price (EbyMEBDURpE 05:26). Our compare and price tools produce exactly this. **Clear improvement.**
6. **F (the numbered slot upgrade order) gives each step its reason,** usually the tree node that makes the slot matter (WLT5_uRXR60 09:31–10:20). The slot table's fallback column becomes "the affix to drop first" (j0xi8FhKYVQ 13:47, 15:06; EbyMEBDURpE 10:17, 11:24). **Clear improvement.** Keep one list; don't add a second list for damage.
7. **"Save" rows in the gem and ascendancy timing table (G1)**, for what to stockpile before a swap: level-7 uncut gems (i6vzzrQIJ6w 06:56), or buy the staff before respeccing. **Clear and cheap**, because D2 already knows the next stage's gems.
8. **One rotation clause in a leveling stage when its damage is a combo** (i6vzzrQIJ6w 01:22, 03:16, 09:38). **Judgment call**, given the 110-word cap. It passes the not-a-tutorial test (G4) because it names this build's skills.
9. **B and C get stronger, with a limit on C.**
   - B (the "when you're short" line): use a spirit drop order (EbyMEBDURpE 04:01) and a mana-fix order. PoB gives unreserved spirit and mana, so these are verifiable.
   - C (the traps bullets): only use traps that item text, gem text or PoB can source: the Satchel's instant flask, Rage on Hollow Resonance, the Herald level cap, the leech stat, Chimes needing Culmination.
   - **Don't copy** traps he found by playing: Charged Staff killing the bells, Hollow Keeper and chill, Thrill of the Kill on the bells. PoB doesn't model them, and the user picks the sources.
10. **Check the CI case against the survival floor.** The floor (D-234) requires capped chaos resistance unless an exception is recorded. A CI rung needs the chaos line to read as immunity rather than an exception. His own pre-CI floor is 30% chaos (SKTko6cEckQ 10:04). This is a check on the gate, not a change to G.
