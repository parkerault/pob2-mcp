## 1. Mirage Sniper: "0.5 Mirage Archer Snipe Gemling Legionnaire Endgame Build Guide"
**J_Macc** · Mercenary / Gemling Legionnaire · End Game · tag "0.5.5 FR" · updated Jul 27, 2026. Main skill: Snipe fired by Mirage Archer.

**Chain:**
1. **Mirage Archer** (gem): "While active, dodge rolling will create a Mirage that uses socketed ranged Attacks for a short duration, then vanish." The guide says that in 0.5 it "can now use channeled skills such as Snipe". Snipe is socketed in Mirage Archer with Cooldown Recovery II, Prolonged Duration II, Rakiata's Flow and Rapid Attacks II.
2. **Snipe:** "Releasing with Perfect Timing causes the arrow to explode on impact…" The guide: "Paired with the Gemling Legionnaire and quality stacking, Snipe gains massive attack speed, allowing the Mirage Archer to quickly hit the perfect timing window."
   - Ascendancy nodes from the page data: Essence of Virtue, Advanced Thaumaturgy, Implanted Gems, Gem Studded. Advanced Thaumaturgy reads "Gem Quality grants Socketed Skills an additional effect" (tooltip read on Ulf's page).
   - Quality sources: the amulet's "+(3-5)% to Quality of all Skills" and the Paragon anoint ("+5% to Quality of all Skills").
   - *Inferred:* the quality-to-attack-speed step goes through Advanced Thaumaturgy. The page never shows Snipe's thaumaturgy effect.
3. **Up to 6 mirages at once**, from cooldown and duration stacking. The guide gives "cooldown is 3.16 seconds and duration is 21.78 seconds". Sources:
   - Uhtred's Sidereus ("Boots: Can roll Chronomancy modifiers"), with boots rolling "(3-5)% increased Cooldown Recovery Rate" and "(5-10)% increased Skill Effect Duration".
   - Time-Lost Emeralds: "Notable Passive Skills in Radius also grant (1-3)% increased Cooldown Recovery Rate".
   - Heart of the Well, rolled for cooldown recovery and duration.
   - From Nothing to allocate around Hollow Palm Technique, overlapping an emerald.
4. **Multipliers:**
   - Rakiata's Flow: "Hits with Supported Skills treat Enemy Monster Elemental Resistance values as inverted".
   - Armour break everywhere (Armour Break III, Armour Demolisher II), plus Uruk's Smelting on self-cast Snipe: "Fully Breaking Armour… permanently take 5% increased Physical Damage, up to 20%".
   - Window of Opportunity II: "35% more Damage when released with Perfect Timing".
   - Cadiro's Gambit: "Each Arrow fired is a Crescendo, Splinter, Reversing, Diamond, Covetous, or Blunt Arrow". Countess Seske's Rune: "Bow Attacks fire an additional Arrow".
   - Rend (from the Set-2 talisman) consumes a power charge for a lightning buff, with Heightened Charges. Power charges come from Thaumaturgic Generator, via a Raven-Touched Shard on the helmet, an anoint or Megalomaniac.
   - Pounce marks tanky enemies.
   - Gem Studded with red supports most numerous: "Hits against you have no Critical Damage Bonus".

**Damage source:** Snipe hits and perfect-timing explosions from mirage clones that dodge-roll creates, so it plays like a totem build.

**Clever:**
- A dodge-triggered clone channels a perfect-timing skill. Quality-driven attack speed makes the clone's timing reliable.
- Chronomancy boots plus duration stacking turn a short trigger into six standing archers.

**Investment:** no cost tiers. Mageblood (mapping) or Headhunter (Grand Expedition) variants. The Eternal Rage amulet is "a luxury item… craft it yourself". Prism of Belief +2 Snipe ("+3 is very expensive"), crafted Time-Lost Emeralds, a Megalomaniac, and corrupted gloves and boots. "Gear is pretty specific." No leveling content.

**Defences and weaknesses:** "Very Tanky".
- Decree of Acuity: "You are Blind / The Effect of Blind on you is reversed", "Gain (15-30)% of Evasion Rating as extra Armour".
- Morior Invictus: "(300-400)% increased Armour, Evasion and Energy Shield".
- Arctic Armour, Ghost Dance, Wind Dancer, Refutation, plus deflection from self-cast Snipe.
- Weaknesses: screen clutter, "plays kind of like a totem build", clunky mirage targeting.

## 3a. Glacial Bolt (Phylaris): "WHIRLING Glacial Bolt Gemling [POE2 0.5]"
**Phylaris POE** · Mercenary / Gemling Legionnaire · "0.5.5 FR" · Aug 13, 2026. Variants: Endgame, Endgame (Early), Leveling. Main skills: Glacial Bolt (crossbow, set 2) and Whirling Slash (spear, set 1).

**Chain:**
1. **Glacial Bolt:** "create two walls of Ice Crystals". Supported by:
   - Ahn's Citadel ("Walls… are created along a Fissure instead")
   - Kaom's Madness ("create 3 additional fissures", with 20% less damage and speed and 30% less AoE)
   - Rakiata's Flow, Elemental Armament II, Vorana's Siege
2. **Whirling Slash** is both movement and detonator: it "automatically breaks all your Glacial Bolt ice crystals with Biting Frost II/Morrigan's Insight". Supports:
   - Mórrigan's Insight: "Consume Freeze… 50% more Damage", "trigger Nature's Exchange"
   - Rigwald's Ferocity: "30% increased Attack Speed if used in Weapon Set I"
   - Rage III
3. **More crystal breakers:**
   - Verisium Manifestations (with Biting Frost II): "Hitting with an Attack will spend Runic Ward to summon a Verisium Manifestation".
   - Pounce with Biting Frost II and Brutus' Brain.
   - Seismic Cry with Vruun's Aftermath: "Supported Warcries cause Ice Crystals to explode… Empower 3 additional Attacks if they destroy at least 5".
4. **Rage from melee** means he skips Eternal Rage and spends Spirit on Scavenged Plating and Blasphemy + Temporal Chains.
   - Trinity comes from the Absent Amulet: "+(3-6)% more Elemental Damage per 30 Affinity". He balances it manually with a tri-element crossbow, since "Glacial Bolt will convert the phys damage to cold anyway".
   - Embitter on Spear Stab: "gain extra Cold Damage instead".
5. **Gem Studded balancing trick:** "Time of Need and Explosive Shot are disabled and just used for extra motes + support slots… via equipping 2 unset rings… then removing the unset rings." Also: "Gem Studded only works off your active weaponset's supports."
   - Anoint priority: Augmented Flesh ("Grants 2 additional Skill Slots") > Paragon.

**Damage source:** ice-crystal explosions, set off by his own melee movement, minions, a wolf and a warcry. *Inferred* from "consistency on your ice crystal explosions".

**Clever:**
- An attack-to-move melee skill on the other weapon set doubles as the crystal detonator and the rage engine.
- Disabled gems and unset rings pad Gem Studded and Virtuous Barrier.

**Investment:** "Scalable from zero currency".
- Endgame (Early) uses Headhunter and Skysliver.
- Endgame uses Mageblood (Silver, Bismuth, Jade or Granite) and a crafted Desolate Crossbow ("+3 to Level of all Attack Skills", "(20-25)% chance to gain Onslaught").
- The glove craft costs "about 1/4 a divine".
- The Leveling variant uses Thrillsteel, Tabula Rasa, 2× Blackheart, Meginord's Girdle, Rampart Raptor and Wanderlust, with Permafrost Bolts and Fragmentation Rounds.

**Defences and weaknesses:** "Super tanky with 100k+ armour and Blasphemy+Temp Chains". The helmet and boots carry "% of Armour also applies to Elemental Damage". Weaknesses: attack-to-move is "hit or miss", and piloting is more involved.
- FAQ: avoid the glove "surpassing chance for extra projectile" mod. "It is bugged."
- "Moba doesn't properly display the From Nothing notable jumps", meaning the Blood Magic notables are in the PoB.

## 5. HOTG Infinite Bonker: "[0.5.5] Ulf's Hammer Engine"
**Ulfhednar** · Mercenary / Gemling Legionnaire · tags End Game and Speed Leveling (but there's no leveling content) · "0.5.5 FR" · Sep 1, 2026. Main skill: Hammer of the Gods (HotG).

**Chain:**
1. **HotG:** "Requires 20 Glory to use"; "Generates 100% of Monster Power as Glory… on Heavy Stunning".
2. **Never spend Glory:** "the extra quality from Advanced Thaumaturgy… gives Hammer of the Gods % chance of not consuming Glory". You need the quality description at 85%, plus Everlasting Glory ("Skills have a 15% chance to not consume Glory"), to reach 100%. Quality sources:
   - 20% from Gemcutting Prisms
   - Paragon anoint
   - Fox Idol in the body armour, or Loreweave
   - Desecrated amulet mod
   - 6% from small ascendancy nodes
3. **Seed Glory:** swap to set 2's Fury of the King ("Grants Skill: Level (16-20) Molten Crash"). Get one "overcorrupted that has 6 glory".
4. **Zero mana:** Defy II reads "Insufficient Mana doesn't prevent the use of Supported Skills while Surrounded" and "Attack Damage with Hits… is Lucky while you are Surrounded". Constricting Command ("Require (2-4) fewer enemies to be Surrounded") must roll 4, so Surrounded is active "as long as there is at least 1 Enemy". The guide says Surrounded also gives "over 200% Attack Damage, 20% Attack Speed". *Inferred:* those bonuses come from the tree.
5. **Rage engine:**
   - Medved's Felling: "5% increased Attack Speed per 5 Rage", "Lose 1% of Maximum Life per 5 Rage". The guide: "80 rage aka 80% Attack Speed".
   - Berserk: "(53-59)% increased Rage effect", "Lose 0.1% of maximum Life per Rage per second".
   - Both drawbacks are neutralised by Chaos Inoculation ("Maximum Life is 1") on a Flesh Crucible jewel.
6. **HotG supports:** Zerphi's Infamy ("requires 50 Glory", "50% more Damage", "50% increased Area of Effect"), Rakiata's Flow, Aftershock II, Defy II.
7. **Variants:**
   - Olrovasara Ice Herald: a runemastered Olrovasara "converts 100% Lightning damage to Cold damage thus enabling Herald of Ice for screen shatters". The tooltip shows only the base line, "gains maximum added Lightning damage equal to the enemy's Power… up to a total of 500".
   - Chaos Dreamer: Giant's Blood ("wield Two-Handed… Maces… in one hand"), with Palm of the Dreamer rolled for "reduced Skill Effect Duration to make Hammer of the Gods fall faster" (it also has "Gain 27% of Damage as Extra Chaos Damage"). Adds a Sacred Maul (+4 melee) and Hateforge ("Gain (3-6) Rage on Hit"; "DO NOT have Lose All Rage on it!").

**Damage source:** the hammer's weapon-based slam, plus Herald of Ice shatters in the Olrovasara variant.

**Clever:**
- Gem quality, through a Gemling node, turns a resource-gated nuke into a spammable skill.
- CI deletes the life cost of both rage multipliers.
- A one-enemy Surrounded makes skills free.
- Sniper's Mark, Tame Beast and Boneshatter are inactive, socketed only for Virtuous Barrier motes. The Raise Shield and Mace Strike supports exist only to balance Gem Studded.

**Investment:** high-end, with no tiers stated. Mageblood (Jade and Granite for evasion), optional Grip of Kulemak, overcorrupted Fury of the King, a CI Flesh Crucible, Prism of Belief HotG, and Heroic Tragedy (Vorana) with From Nothing around Zealot's Oath.

**Defences:**
- CI, so effectively no life.
- Decree of Flight: "Gain Guard equal to (10-20)% of missing Energy Shield… when you Dodge Roll".
- Blood of the Warrior flask: "Damage taken during effect Recouped as Life".
- Arctic Armour, Ghost Dance, Wind Dancer.
- Strengths: "Speed, Sustain, Able to spam skills at 0 mana". No weaknesses listed.

## Closing
**Index framing:** Perra's "Broken Off-Meta Builds" (updated Jul 26, 2026): "There are some of the best off-meta builds in PoE2, as showcased in my video. Links below." His own video is embedded at youtube.com/embed/VGQ8TB9hQFY. On Build #3 he writes: "Phylaris uses Whirling Slash for extra speed, whereas Josiah uses a regular xbow setup. They're both fantastic options."

**What the four readable guides share:**
- All are Gemling Legionnaire on 0.5.5.
- All use Rakiata's Flow on the main skill.
- All use Morior Invictus, the Absent Amulet (granting Eternal Rage or Trinity) and Virtuous Barrier.
- All build around the Gemling Legionnaire's Gem Studded node, balancing support-gem colours to choose its bonus, often by socketing filler gems that are never used.
- All rely heavily on lineage supports and on the jewels From Nothing, Prism of Belief and Megalomaniac.
- Each puts a second weapon set to non-damage work: Pounce/Rend, a melee detonator, or a Glory seeder.
