- Roll: Smith of Kitava (Warrior) with Bone Cage.

- How much reasoning there is: a moderate amount. It is mostly a recap with narration, and the reasoning is concentrated in about six decisions.

THE ARC
- 03:21 Bone Cage arrives. He reads its stats (15% base crit, pins enemies) and it one-shots trash. Druid spells (Entangle, Volcano, later Thrashing Vines and Thunderstorm) supply side damage.
- 05:55 He tries Iron Ward because his AoE is weak, heralds don't work with spells, and he has armour. Blocking with the shield fills it fast (08:41).
- 10:21 The first wall is the Zalmorath fight. Bone Cage only spawns on solid ground, the boss's hitbox isn't there, and he does 10% damage in 2 minutes. He falls back on the staff's built-in fireball.
- 15:02 and 15:52 He finally adds Astral Projection (cast at the cursor) and Spell Cascade, and admits he should have had them from the start.
- 16:16 to 18:16 He plans ahead for Smith's Masterwork, a node that locks you to a normal-rarity body armour whose mods you buy with ascendancy points. He corrupts a white full plate for a socket in advance. Then Tantalum Alloy (75% fire resistance) plus Coal Stoker (fire resistance also counts at 50% for cold and lightning) caps his elemental resistances at level 37.
- 21:44 He buys Cast on Minion Death and the Dark Defiler sceptre, tests them, finds neither helps yet, and parks them as "the pillars of our build later on".
- 29:41 In maps, the Dark Defiler grants 5% of damage as extra chaos per undead minion, so 9 skeletons give about 45%. Losing the focus drops his energy shield to 0, and he hits a wall at tier 2. He buys life gear (1,233 to 1,948 life).
- 37:30 to 39:28 He builds the Cast on Minion Death engine. Minion Pact goes on the self-cast Bone Cage. His own max life is kept below the skeletons' life, and he swaps a max-life ascendancy node for spirit. It triggers on every other cast.
- 40:22 to 41:17 He dies repeatedly at tier 15 and gets stunlocked in a trial. He respecs into energy shield and crafts a Vaal robe, ending at 3.8k energy shield and 1.5k life.
- After the Arbiter: Effigy of Cruelty goes in weapon set 2 (47:37). Dialla's Desire makes the trigger fire on every cast (51:37). Trample Toes follow (52:23), and he splits his gear into a bossing set and a mapping set (54:58).

THE PROCESS
1. Check where the skill can land, not only its numbers (10:21). Bone Cage's ground-only spawn turns some bosses into hard walls, and a weapon's built-in skill is the backup. Sharper form: read a skill's placement rules against boss types such as raised or flying hitboxes.
2. Buy the pillars early, test them in place, and park them with a re-test condition (21:44, then 29:41 and 37:30). He doesn't sell them when they fail early. He knows which missing piece (minions, spirit, a way for minions to die) will turn each one on.
3. An enabler's number can reverse a normal goal (38:50 to 39:08). Minion Pact only works if a minion has at least your max life, so he deliberately caps his own life. That forces knock-on changes: drop the life node, then move defence into energy shield (41:12). When the trigger fell short on energy, he closed the gap with quality rather than more minions (51:37).
4. Chain defence into offence, then prune (05:55, 16:38, 29:09). Scavenged Plating (breaking enemy armour raises your own) fed Iron Ward's armour-scaled nuke. When it "wasn't doing much", he replaced it with Mana Remnants, which fed his mana problem. Each slot has to justify itself against the current bottleneck.
5. Run the standard kit before calling a skill weak (15:02, 15:52, 52:23). He says himself that being "stubborn trying not to use the meta solution" cost him acts of poor play.
6. A counter-example to learn from: guessing a fix without a cause (40:33). He bought chaos resistance on a hunch and kept dying. The fixes that worked targeted a named cause: no energy shield after the offhand swap (30:04), and stunlock (41:04).
7. Gate expensive attempts (56:34). He warms up on the normal Arbiter before each 25-divine Uber attempt.

PATTERNS
- Iron Ward: defence that scales damage. Blocking to fill it: routine action as damage.
- Scavenged Plating feeding Iron Ward, and Thunderstorm's guaranteed shock feeding Mana Remnants' mana orbs: inputs-and-outputs.
- Skeletons as the Dark Defiler's headcount, kept alive by Clerics plus Brutus' Brain: inert minions as triggers or headcount.
- Dialla's Desire raising Cast on Minion Death's energy gain: quality as a stat line.
- Effigy of Cruelty plus the druid spells on weapon set 2, used to apply Critical Weakness: weapon set 2 doing a non-damage job.
- NEW, "a support's cost as trigger fuel": Minion Pact's life drain, plus Minion Instability, produces the minion deaths the trigger needs. Search step: for each trigger event, search supports whose drawback causes that event.
- NEW, "a debuff loop split across two skills": Uruk's Smelting on Vines fully breaks armour (+5% physical damage taken per break, up to 20%), and Exploit Weakness on another spell consumes the break so Vines can reapply it (34:54). Search step: for a consumed debuff with a permanent per-application bonus, pair an applier with a consumer that cannot apply it.
- NEW, "one stat broadcast to its siblings": Coal Stoker plus a single 75% fire line. Search step: grep the ascendancy and tree text for "also applies to ... at N%".

Checked against our pinned PoB data (0.23.1): Minion Pact I drains 100% of your max life and needs a minion with enough life. Quality on Cast on Minion Death raises energy generated, and underlevelled minions are penalised. Exploit Weakness consumes fully broken armour and cannot break armour itself. Uruk's caps at 20%. The Dark Defiler is 5% per undead minion. Effigy of Cruelty applies 1–3 stacks. Dialla's quality differs (+5% in the pin, +10% on screen).

FOR OUR PHASES
- Verification should check placement and targeting: flag skills that spawn at a location or are centred on the player, plus boss types they can't reach. PoB can't show this, so it goes to the user as a stated risk.
- Verification should also check whether the enabler's conditions are met at the target level. Compute the numeric condition (your life vs minion life, energy per trigger) and list what it forces off the build.
- Brainstorm should give every candidate the standard kit (placement or multi-cast supports, AoE uniques, curses) before judging it weak.
- Present a staged plan: a self-cast core that works alone, pillars marked "buy now, works at stage X", and a fallback if the trigger engine falls short ("every other cast" was acceptable to him).
