# Gear pricing: price levels by league day, and one guide's gear sets (2026-10-16)

How should a guide budget gear? The target reader gets the guide at league start, buys early endgame gear around
day 7 and reaches late endgame two to three weeks in. The run that writes the guide sees one market, on one day:
last league's, or the current league's on whatever day it runs. This study measures how prices move with the
league day, so a price seen on one day can be turned into a rough estimate for another. It then tests the result
on a real guide's gear.

The user's anchors, from experience (`../worklog.md`, 2026-10-15 and 2026-10-16):

- By day 7, most early endgame items cost a few divines. That gear is a big upgrade on campaign gear, usually
  enough for most six-mod T15 maps; past it, prices hit diminishing returns. Early endgame gear peaks around day 7
  and declines after.
- Late endgame gear plateaus around day 21 and rises slowly to league end. Guides top out at 100–200 divines.
- Estimates need to be ballpark, not exact: the user can re-price a build at any point.

## 1. Summary

1. **Price in divines, not exalts.** In every league the median exchange item's exalt price rose 2–7× between
   day 7 and day 40. The divine level moves far less.
2. **The divine level is steady from day 21 to day 40** (0.97–1.26 of day 21 in four leagues), **unsettled in week
   one** (0.70–2.21, median 1.45) **and 2–4× above day 21 at a league's end.** Fate of the Vaal is the outlier
   throughout: 2.47 on day 40 and 23 at its end.
3. **Dear items are much cheaper early.** An item worth over 10 divines on day 40 cost about a quarter of that on
   day 7 (median ×0.26) and about half on day 21 (×0.56). Items under 10 divines kept more: ×0.55–0.58 on day 7
   and ×0.57–0.76 on day 21. This is the top arm of the K shape the user described.
4. **The guide's trade links search for the author's own near-perfect items.** Exact affixes with high minimum
   rolls return a few very dear listings: on day 41 the cheapest early endgame armour and jewellery cost 5–25
   divines, the weapons 130 and 155, the amulet 2,500. Converted to day 7 with §5's table, armour and jewellery come
   to about 2–10 divines a slot, near the user's anchor. Weapons and the amulet don't.
5. **How a search is built matters more than the day.** The user dropped one low-value affix ("#% increased Energy
   Shield from Equipped Body Armour") from the guide's 420–900 divine mid endgame amulet search, and the cheapest
   listing fell to 10 divines: a 40× drop, far beyond any day conversion. As the second most popular Mobalytics
   guide, its followers also bid its exact items up. A guide we generate is unlikely to meet that premium unless
   its gear overlaps a meta build's.
6. **The trade site rate-limited the capture** on its 18th search (HTTP 429, a 600-second lockout). Fifteen more
   searches at one every ~100 seconds drew no limit.

## 2. Data and method

- **Exchange prices:** the user's poe2scout dumps (`poe2-exchange-archive/out/<league>/daily_long.csv`, kept outside
  this repo): the daily average price in exalts and the volume of every currency-exchange item. The leagues and
  dates:

  | League | Data from–to | Last day used |
  |---|---|---|
  | Dawn of the Hunt | 2025-04-05 to 2025-08-27 | 142 |
  | Rise of the Abyssal | 2025-08-30 to 2025-12-08 | 98 |
  | Fate of the Vaal | 2025-12-12 to 2026-05-25 | 162 |
  | Runes of Aldur | 2026-05-29 to 2026-10-15 | 137 |
  | Forbidden Rites | 2026-09-04 to 2026-10-15 | 39 |

- **League day 0** is the first date the divine traded. An item's price on day *n* is the median of days *n*−1 to
  *n*+1.
- **The general level** is the median, over items, of each item's price on day *n* against its own price on a
  reference day: day 40 in exalts (table 1), or day 21 in divines (table 2).
- **Tiers** group items by their day-40 price in divines: under 0.1, 0.1–1, 1–10 and over 10. Only items with at
  least 100 trades in the first 41 days count, and a tier needs at least 3 items in a league.
- **The script** is `data/2026-10-16-pricing.py`:
  `python3 -I data/2026-10-16-pricing.py <path to poe2-exchange-archive/out>`. It prints the four tables below.
- **The gear sets:** `data/2026-10-16-forbidden-rites-basket.json`. A capture agent read the Mobalytics guide the
  user named (`mobalytics.gg/poe-2/builds/martial-artist-monk-build-league-starter-to-endgame`, a 0.5 guide whose
  links target Forbidden Rites) and priced each gear slot's trade query through the pob2 server on day 41. The
  file has no seller names or listing ids.
- **Limits.** The exchange is a proxy for gear: some pairs are thinly traded, divines are scarcer than gear early in a
  league, and drops keep adding gear supply where the exchange has none. The general level is a median over
  currency, fragments, gems and the like, not gear. One guide's gear sets are one test, at one league day.

Table 3 in the script's output splits the same ratios into the general level and each tier's move against it. Its
two findings:

- **The top arm is robust.** Grouped by day-40 price, items over 10 divines sat at 0.11–0.69× the general level on
  day 7 (median 0.15). Grouped by their day-7 price instead, they still sat at 0.25–0.81× (median 0.34).
- **The bottom arm is not.** Grouped by day-40 price, the cheapest items look dearer early (1.42× on day 7); grouped
  by day-7 price, they track the general level (1.05×). Grouping by the later price picks items that happened to
  fall. Gear may still differ, since drops keep adding supply.

## 5. The conversion table

For prices seen between day 21 and day 60 of a league. The ranges are the four leagues other than Fate of the Vaal,
rounded; Fate of the Vaal sat below every range, because its whole level more than doubled after day 21.

| Item's price when seen | To day 21 | To day 7 |
|---|---|---|
| Under ~10 divines | ×0.6–0.85 | ×0.4–0.9 |
| Over ~10 divines | ×0.5–0.85 | ×0.2–0.4 |
| Seen late in a league (past day 60) or after it ended | the row above, then ÷2–4 | the row above, then ÷2–4 |

The user found an earlier draft "really close" to what they expect of day-7 and day-21 prices. That draft gave
×0.2–0.6 to day 7 for items under ~10 divines; it leaned on Forbidden Rites' own week one, the lowest of the five.
The data across leagues put that row at ×0.4–0.9, and the table above uses the data.

## 6. The guide's gear sets on day 41

The guide has four gear sets. Campaign End / Early Maps, Early Endgame and Mid Endgame were priced; Uber Endgame was
not, being beyond the user's ~200-divine ceiling. The rate was 715.3 exalts to the divine. The cheapest listing is
used, because for strict searches the 5th–10th listings run far above what anyone actually pays.

**Campaign End / Early Maps:** under 1 divine in all (cheapest listings), about 4 divines by the median of listings
5–10. Six of its fifteen slots found nothing; some queries can't match any item (one asks seven explicit affixes of
a rare, which has at most six).

**Reading it:**

- The armour and jewellery land at about 2–10 divines a slot on day 7, near "a few divines". They are the author's
  near-perfect items, so a budget version of each would cost less.
- The weapons and the amulet carry skill levels, the strongest single damage source and the dearest affix. The
  guide's amulet is a chase item at any price level.
- The user's amulet test (§1, item 5) shows the main fix: a search that requires only the affixes the build can't
  do without, and lets the rest vary, finds a far larger and cheaper pool.

## 7. What this means for the build guide

The design follows in amendment 5 (`../spec/amendment-5-pricing.md`). In short:

- price endgame rares with PoB's weighted search (the server's `find_upgrades`), the item's mandatory affixes
  required and a converted budget as the maximum price, so the result is what the budget buys;
- convert with §5's table and show ranges, with the league and day the prices were seen;
- treat the budgets as targets and report them; never fail a design on price, and let the user judge the cost.
