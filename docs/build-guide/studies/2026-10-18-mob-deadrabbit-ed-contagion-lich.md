## 2. What the build is

- **Damage source:** Essence Drain (ED), a projectile whose hit applies a chaos damage-over-time debuff (443.4 per
  second for 4 s at gem level 20, **checked**). Contagion is a smaller debuff and the spreader: when an afflicted
  enemy dies, it spreads itself, curses and other chaos DoT debuffs to nearby enemies, refreshing them, and its own
  debuff deals 100% more per spread, up to 300% (**checked**, gem text).
- **Packs:** Contagion into the pack, then ED (with Chain while mapping); each death carries both debuffs onward,
  so a pack falls in a chain reaction. Dense high-tier maps are its best content.
- **Bosses:** Dark Effigy, a totem that bombards enemies afflicted by chaos DoT debuffs (**checked**), is the hit
  layer; Despair is cast from weapon set 2; Chaos Bolt from the Withered Wand keeps Wither stacks on the boss; ED
  swaps Chain for Deliberation.
- **Ascendancy:** Lich. Eternal Life (your life cannot change while you have energy shield, **checked**) is the
  defensive core, with energy shield (ES) stacked past 10K.
- **Feel:** strengths Simplicity, Clearing and Survivability; one weakness, "Mana Hungry".

## 3. The kit

- **Weapons:** set 1 is a Withered Wand and a focus (Antler Focus while leveling, Tasalian Focus at the end); set 2
  is a Chiming Staff. The wand grants Chaos Bolt (not checked).
- **Endgame links** (boss swaps in parentheses):

| Skill | Full Life | Low Life |
|---|---|---|
| Essence Drain | Chain II, Swift Affliction II, Rapid Casting II, Intense Agony, Chaos Mastery (bosses: Deliberation for Chain) | Chain II, Chaos Mastery, Zenith II, Swift Affliction II, Sione's Temper (bosses: Deliberation and Considered Casting) |
| Contagion | Unleash, Rapid Casting II, Magnified Area II, Chaotic Freeze | the same plus Arcane Surge |
| Dark Effigy | Overabundance II, Urgent Totems III, Rapid Casting III, Concentrated Area, Controlled Destruction | Overabundance II, Execute III, Controlled Destruction, Urgent Totems III, Extraction |
| Chaos Bolt | Withering Touch, Rapid Casting II, Chaos Mastery, Extraction | the same four |

- **Effigy's swaps:** Ancestral Urgency while mapping, Execute against bosses. The pin's Effigy has a limit of one
  totem (**checked**), so Overabundance presumably raises it (not checked).
- **Curses:** Blasphemy with Temporal Chains (Slow Potency, Ritualistic Curse, Magnified Area II); Despair with
  Heightened Curse and Cursed Ground, on weapon set 2.
- **Buffs and Spirit:** Withering Presence with Clarity I and Prolonged Duration II (40 Spirit), Blasphemy (55 with
  quality), Convalescence (30): 125 Spirit, the last 25 from an amulet or a helmet corruption. Low Life adds Ravenous
  Swarm (30) for 155. Sigil of Power sits on set 2.
- **Uniques:** Atziri's Disdain, the core piece, wanted early (§7); Ingenuity if the rings are strong; Visage of
  Ayah only to set up the low-life lock; Wondertrap, optional rarity on low life.
- **Ascendancy order:** Soulless Form (Act 2), Eternal Life (Act 3), Eldritch Empowerment once its ES cost is
  sustainable, then Rupture the Soul (cursed enemies killed may explode for chaos) for early maps, swapped late for
  Crystalline Phylactery (a basic jewel at double effect). All **checked**; Eldritch Empowerment sacrifices 5% of
  maximum ES per spell cast for 30% more damage.
- **Tree direction:** damage notables first (Potent Incantation, Lingering Horror, Void, Dark Entries), then ES
  (Melding, Heavy Buffer) once ES gear arrives. Set 1's points serve the chaos spells; set 2's go to Bonestorm in
  Act 2 and to Despair's potency at the end. Cast speed comes from Mental Alacrity and Spiral into Mania (Full Life)
  or Final Barrage and Quick Response (Low Life). Reverberation is anointed for Contagion's spread range.

## 4. The levers

In the guide's order:

1. **+levels to chaos spells** on the wand, focus and amulet: the first offensive priority on every endgame slot
   that rolls it.
2. **Spell and chaos damage**, then chaos damage on rings.
3. **Cast speed,** for feel more than ED's damage: it shortens both casts while mapping.
4. **Wither.** Chaos Bolt on bosses keeps Withered stacks, which the guide says raise chaos damage a lot; jewels
   with increased Wither effect are its pure single-target pick.
5. **Gem investment:** Jeweller's Orbs go to ED, Dark Effigy, Despair, then Contagion; quality to Withering
   Presence, Dark Effigy, Despair, Contagion and Convalescence.
6. **Eldritch Empowerment's 30% more,** only when ES and its recharge can pay 5% per cast.
7. **Shock:** gain-as-extra-lightning on the wand lets Dark Effigy's hits shock (not checked).

**What it skips:** Contagion's level (a level 1 gem saves mana; a higher one takes Arcane Surge); Bonestorm once
Dark Effigy arrives; Volcano after Act 1.

## 5. Leveling

- **Act 1:** Contagion clears and Volcano is the single-target spell until ED (from the Rust King), then Bonestorm;
  each gem's source is named. ED takes Swift Affliction I and Zenith I; Contagion, Unleash and Magnified Area I.
  Chaos Bolt (Withering Touch, Rapid Casting I) on bosses from the start.
- **Act 2:** Lich and Soulless Form; Living Lightning on Bonestorm; the first Lesser Jeweller's Orb on Bonestorm,
  the next for Chain II on ED. The weapon-set split starts here.
- **Act 3 (level 31):** Dark Effigy (Overabundance I, Urgent Totems I, Poison I) replaces Bonestorm; Despair, and
  Blasphemy with Temporal Chains. Eternal Life from the second trial. Contagion gains Chaotic Freeze.
- **Act 4 on:** the build stops changing; Considered Casting joins ED, the Greater Jeweller's Orb from Ngakanu goes
  to ED, and Blasphemy and Temporal Chains are levelled so the curse keeps landing.
- **Gear:** one set of bases for all four tabs (Withered Wand, Antler Focus, Hexer's Robe and so on). Stats:
  +chaos spell levels, spell or chaos damage, life, cold resistance; ES bases from Act 4; a vendor regex.
- **Rewards:** charm charges, stun threshold, resistances; life flask recovery swapped for mana, and Interlude
  resistances for movement speed, later.

## 6. Gear and budget

No prices anywhere: budget is signalled only by the tab names ("High Investment") and the item order.

- **Buy order:** Full Life starts with Atziri's Disdain, then Ingenuity, the wand and the focus; Low Life starts
  with Ingenuity and lists the helmet last.
- **Endgame bases:** Tasalian Focus, Sacramental Robe (a Flowing Raiment recommended for its mana regeneration),
  Solar Amulet, two Prismatic Rings.
- **Per slot:** wand, focus and amulet: chaos levels first, then damage (focus) or ES (amulet), then cast speed.
  Body, gloves, boots, belt: high ES, resistances, life. Rings: chaos damage, cast speed, resistances, life. The
  author prefers a three-charm rare belt with resistances, life and mana to Ingenuity.
- **Atziri's Disdain:** a high life-as-ES roll, corrupted to two sockets.
- **Socketables:** a +1 spells, chaos-as-extra or Greater Iron rune on the wand; Fenumus' Rune of Draining in the
  gloves; Tecrod's Gaze in the boots on Low Life only; Soul Core of Tacati for chaos resistance anywhere.
- **Jewels:** increased maximum ES and spell or chaos damage as prefixes, cast speed and mana regeneration (or
  ES-based stun threshold) as suffixes; ES recharge rate or faster recharge start in place of damage; increased
  Wither effect for bosses. One goes into Crystalline Phylactery.

## 7. Defences

- **ES as the only pool:** high-ES bases, nearly every increased-ES node, high recharge rate and a fast recharge
  start; "up to and above 10K" ES.
- **A 25% layer from bypass:** Soulless Form (10%), Atziri's Disdain (10%) and Heavy Buffer (5%) each send damage
  past ES (all **checked**). With Eternal Life, life cannot change while ES holds, so the guide counts that 25% as
  mitigation.
- **Resistances and control:** elemental capped, chaos at or near the cap; Temporal Chains on Blasphemy.
- **Life still matters:** Atziri's turns 10–15% of maximum life into extra ES, and Soulless Form regenerates mana
  equal to 6% of maximum life per second in place of inherent regeneration (both **checked**), so life is a stat
  even though the pool never moves (**mine**).
- **Mana:** the stated weakness, met by Soulless Form, Clarity, a level 1 Contagion or Arcane Surge, a Flowing
  Raiment, and mana on the belt and jewels.
- **The low-life lock:** put on Visage of Ayah and refund Enhanced Barrier to drop ES, pay Dark Effigy's cost with
  Lifetap until life is below the threshold, then put Atziri's Disdain back: ES returns and Eternal Life freezes
  life low. Payoffs: Final Barrage (20% increased cast speed on low life, 10% reduced on full life), Execute III
  (30% more damage with hits while you are on low life), Tecrod's Gaze, Wondertrap. Visage of Ayah grants Eldritch
  Battery, and the first two read as stated (**checked**); the rest are not checked. Lifetap can lower life only
  while ES is zero, hence the order (**mine**). The guide doesn't say what happens when ES breaks with life low.

## 8. What players pick

The guide never says what most players do; it is one author's setup. The places closest to a norm:

- It bills itself as one of the best league starters, for its defences and simple play, and offers its leveling
  half as a campaign route for any class.
- Capping every resistance, chaos included, is called a given for the endgame.
- Atziri's Disdain is the core piece; a Flowing Raiment is recommended; Ingenuity is possible with strong rings,
  though the author prefers a rare belt.
- Eldritch Empowerment is "incredibly good" only when the ES cost is sustainable.

## 9. For the library

**Against the Damage over time kit.** It agrees on the shape: a debuff that deals the damage (ED, not an ailment),
a spreader (Contagion, cast by hand rather than triggered as in [eraser]), Wither from Withering Presence, and
Despair. It differs in three ways: Despair is cast from weapon set 2 with its own tree points, while Blasphemy
carries Temporal Chains; the boss layer is a hit skill that targets the debuff (Dark Effigy); and the levers are
+chaos levels and cast speed, with no ailment magnitude or duration floor. Its campaign line holds: Contagion and
ED from Act 1.

**Kit lines** (Damage over time):

- **Kit:** Essence Drain as the debuff and Contagion as the spreader, cast by hand into packs: an afflicted enemy's
  death spreads Contagion, curses and other chaos DoT debuffs and refreshes them (pin: Contagion's gem text); Chain
  on ED to map, Deliberation against bosses [mob-ed-lich] §2, §3. (Checked: spread text; support swap unverified.)
- **Ramp:** Contagion's debuff deals 100% more per spread, up to 300% (pin: gem text), which PoB may not model
  (coverage: possibly unmodelled) [mob-ed-lich] §2, §9. (Checked.)
- **Bosses:** Dark Effigy, a totem that bombards enemies afflicted by chaos DoT debuffs (pin), with Overabundance
  and Urgent Totems, Execute against bosses [mob-ed-lich] §3. (Targeting checked; supports unverified.)
- **Wither:** Withering Presence with Clarity, and on bosses Chaos Bolt from a Withered Wand with Withering Touch
  to hold the stacks; increased Wither effect on jewels [mob-ed-lich] §3, §4. (Unverified.)
- **Curses:** Temporal Chains on Blasphemy, and Despair from weapon set 2 with set 2's points for its potency
  [mob-ed-lich] §3. (Unverified.)
- **Levers:** +levels of chaos spells on wand, focus and amulet first, then spell and chaos damage, then cast speed,
  which the guide values for feel over damage [mob-ed-lich] §4. (The guide's priority.)
- **Mana:** the guide's one weakness: Contagion at level 1 (or Arcane Surge on a higher one), Clarity, a
  mana-regeneration body [mob-ed-lich] §4, §7. (Unverified.)
- **Lich defence** (for a Lich or Spells note): Eternal Life with three bypass lines (Soulless Form, Atziri's
  Disdain, Heavy Buffer, 25%) on 10K ES [mob-ed-lich] §7. (Text checked; the mitigation reading is the guide's.)

**Candidate pattern shapes:**

- **Zeroed resource** (existing): Eternal Life makes life unchangeable while ES holds, so the "bypasses ES"
  drawbacks become a 25% layer. Checked text; the mitigation is the guide's reading.
- **Forced condition** (existing), a new mechanism: lock rather than reserve. Lower life with ES removed, then
  restore ES so Eternal Life holds life below the threshold. Payoffs checked (Final Barrage, Execute III). Caution:
  it holds only while ES lasts.
- **Cost redirection** (existing): Eldritch Empowerment pays 5% of ES per spell for 30% more damage, so ES recharge
  becomes a damage stat. Checked.
- **State-keyed payoffs** (existing): Dark Effigy fires only at enemies carrying a chaos DoT debuff, a hit layer
  keyed to the DoT's state. Checked.
- **Weapon set 2 job** (existing): Despair and Sigil of Power on set 2, with set 2's points on the curse. The
  guide's.
- **Low-level utility gems** (existing): Contagion at level 1 for mana. The guide's.
- **Amplified slot as stat stick** (existing): Crystalline Phylactery's 100% increased jewel effect (checked);
  Ingenuity with rings (unverified).
- **New: a frozen pool as a stat.** A pool locked in place still feeds every "per maximum X" line (Atziri's life as
  ES, Soulless Form's life-based mana), so it is bought as a stat. Mine; checked text.

**What PoB might not model** (coverage at the pin, checked, except PG-21, the library's):

- Contagion's spread is not modelled (PG-21), and its "100% more per spread" line is flagged possibly unmodelled:
  set it by hand.
- Eldritch Empowerment: the 30% more is parsed, but the sacrifice line is only partly parsed and the
  recharge line unparsed, so PoB shows the damage without the ES drain.
- Eternal Life parses; whether PoB's effective-HP figures treat bypassed damage as removed is not established.
- The low-life lock is a config stand-in (low life); Final Barrage parses.
- Dark Effigy's restriction to afflicted enemies, Wither stacks from Chaos Bolt and the totem count with
  Overabundance are not established.
