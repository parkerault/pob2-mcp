## 2. What the build is

- **Damage source.** Arc, cast by Spell Totem, the meta gem that raises a totem casting its socketed spells
  (checked). The player presses one button; the totems pick targets and chain Arc off screen.
- **The ascendancy.** Gemling Legionnaire. Advanced Thaumaturgy gives each gem's quality a second line
  (checked), and the plan is to "double-dip on the quality bonuses" of two gems in one group:
  - **Arc:** 3% more hit damage per remaining chain, per 20% quality (checked). The guide quotes 6% a chain,
    about 180% more at 30 chains, which implies about 40% quality (my inference).
  - **Spell Totem:** 10% increased cast speed for totem spells per summoned totem, per 20% quality (checked).
    The guide quotes 120% with six totems: 20% a totem, again about 40%.
- **Totem count.** Spell Totem's limit is 2 at gem level 20 and 3 at 23 (checked). Ancestral Bond doubles the
  limit, drops the three-charge requirement and reserves 75 Spirit per totem (checked): six totems, 450
  Spirit. Hence the overview's rule: hold Spell Totem near level 23, the cheapest level with a third totem.
- **Chains.** Dominus' Grasp, a lineage support, makes Arc chain 100% more times (checked). Arc chains 9 times
  at quality 0, 2 more per 20% quality, 6 more when Lightning Infused (checked); the guide doesn't account for
  its full 30 (the Elon tree names Branching Bolts).
- **Mana.** Archmage gives extra lightning per 100 maximum mana (4% at level 20, plus 1% from its Gemling
  line; checked), so the build stacks mana on energy-shield gear: 3.5k to enter, about 5k to feel safe.
- **Packs and bosses.** Packs: drop totems ahead, let Arc chain. Bosses: the same totems, with Frost Bomb and
  Elemental Weakness on weapon set 2, later Blasphemy with Temporal Chains. My inference, unverified: a lone
  boss spends no chains, so its hits keep most of the remaining-chain bonus.
- **Weapon sets.** Archmage, the totems and Mana Tempest on set 1, debuffs on set 2, each set with its own
  passives. The FAQ says to end every sequence on a totem or Mana Tempest so the character rests on set 1.
  Mana Tempest empowers your mana-costing spells while you stand in it (checked); whether that reaches the
  totems' Arc isn't stated.

## 3. The kit

- **Weapon and off hand.** A wand with spell, lightning or "as extra" prefixes, finished with a Perfect Essence
  of Sorcery for +3 spell levels; a Shrine Sceptre for Spirit (200+ from Besos 1); both locked to both weapon
  sets. From Besos 3, Runeseeker's Call ("the fork") with five, then six, runes: Aldur's Legacy (Lifespring
  variant, +2 spell levels), Hedgewitch Assandra's Rune of Wisdom, Perfect Vision and Iron Runes, or a Perfect
  Mind Rune for safety.
- **Arc's group.** Arc, Dominus' Grasp and Urgent Totems III on every endgame tab; Zenith II and Sione's Temper
  on budget tabs; Execute III replaces Zenith II at low life; Embitter from Besos 3, shattering enemies against
  on-death effects. Dialla's Desire may replace Urgent Totems at the Pinnacle, with a much slower first cast;
  the author keeps Urgent Totems.
- **Other skills.** Archmage (Lightning Mastery); Mana Tempest (Advancing Storm, Efficiency II); Frost Bomb
  (Spell Echo, Potent Exposure) and Elemental Weakness (Heightened Curse) on set 2; Blasphemy with Temporal
  Chains and Enfeeble from Besos 1; a Purity with Clarity II, Vitality II, Cool Headed and Warm Blooded; Mana
  Remnants; Virtuous Barrier, the Gemling's granted skill (checked); Charge Regulation from Besos 1; Entangle
  late. Step 2's Comet, Spark and Ball Lightning exist only for Virtuous Barrier's motes.
- **Uniques.** Midnight Braid and, optionally, Dream Fragments at the entry; Mnemonic Rings from Breach; Split
  Personality Ruby; Darkness Enthroned (body-armour variant) with Rune of the Blossom and Fox Idol; Kalandra's
  Touch; Prism of Belief (+2, then +3 Arc); Heart of the Well; Runeseeker's Call; From Nothing (Eldritch
  Battery variant) and Mageblood at Elon; Voices Sapphire at the Pinnacle.
- **Ascendancy.** No order is given. The tabs imply Advanced Thaumaturgy, Gem Studded, Neurological Implants
  (+2 levels to skills with an Intelligence requirement, checked) and Essence of Virtue. At the Pinnacle, +2
  Arc levels from gear free Neurological Implants for Integrated Efficiency.
- **Gem Studded filler.** Red and blue supports are kept tied so both lines apply (checked: red, hits against
  you have no crit bonus; blue, 30% less cost); the author pads with random red supports.
- **Tree.** Ancestral Bond, Eldritch Battery, Mind over Matter, the Split Personality socket south of the
  Mercenary start, Pain Attunement at low life; Elon re-paths around From Nothing (Raw Mana, Branching Bolts,
  Lightning Quick and others). The helm anoint Thaumaturgic Generator enables Charge Regulation for crit.

## 4. The levers

In the order the guide spends:

1. **Totem count.** Each Spirit gain buys a totem (2 → 6). Early, an amulet with Spirit and mana beats one
   with +levels if it reaches the next totem. Spirit comes from the sceptre, desecrated "increased Spirit
   efficiency" suffixes on chest and helm, and Atziri's Communion moving Archmage's reservation to life.
2. **Maximum mana**, for Archmage and defence: rings with mana and mana regeneration (required early), a
   Mnemonic Ring finished with a Perfect Essence of the Mind, Iron Runes, Medved's Tending in the chest.
3. **Gem quality**, for both Gemling lines: the amulet's desecrated quality suffix, the Fox Idol, and a 21/23
   Arc, which the guide says crosses a breakpoint worth 30% more (unverified).
4. **Arc levels:** +3 wand, +3 amulet, the Prism of Belief, Aldur's Legacy.
5. **Crit**, from Besos 1: Charge Regulation, the anoint, crit sapphires.
6. **A level hold:** Spell Totem near 23; more levels buy totem life but cost more mana.

**Skipped:** amulet levels early; crit nodes before Split Personality; Hardy and Reinforced Totems appear on no
tab. Past the damage it needs, the guide buys safety instead (Perfect Mind Rune, Embitter).

## 5. Leveling

- **No totem campaign.** Ancestral Bond is too many points away, so the campaign is a crossbow grenade
  Mercenary, swapping at about level 65–70; any leveling build will do.
- **Acts 1–4.** Explosive Grenade clears (Multishot I, Elemental Armament I, Short Fuse I); Flash Grenade
  builds stun; Gas Grenade bursts on ignited enemies; Oil Grenade (Act 3) adds exposure; Cluster Grenade (Act
  4). Also Frost Bomb, Herald of Ash, Pounce. Jeweller's Orbs go to Explosive Grenade.
- **Interludes+.** Mirage Archer carrying Cluster Grenade; start buying ES gear with mana.
- **Gear.** Flat added damage on the crossbow and both rings is the baseline; armour pieces with life and
  resistances.
- **Swap gate.** Level 70 and about 3.5k mana, both required. The author cleared tier 16 maps at level 66 on the
  minimum setup, squishy and played at range.
- **Twink tab.** Spark with Tabula Rasa, Levinstone rings and Goldrim, only with twink gear.

## 7. Defences

- **Mana as the buffer:** Mind over Matter and Eldritch Battery on ES gear. The only numbers: 3.5k mana to
  start, 4.2–4.5k to feel better, about 5k plus a Kurgal's Gaze to feel tanky.
- **Crit immunity** from Gem Studded's red line (checked).
- **Debuffs:** Enfeeble and Temporal Chains on Blasphemy, Entangle late, Embitter's shatter against on-death
  effects, which the author names as the last remaining threat.
- **Totems:** Spell Totem gives them 75% elemental and 35% chaos resistance (checked).
- **Mageblood flasks:** Diamond and Quicksilver, Amethyst for chaos resistance, or Granite with Arctic Armour.
- No resistance, ES or max-hit totals are given.

## 8. What players pick

- The guide never says what most players do. The nearest lines are market notes: starter wands "VERY cheap";
  Midnight Braid cheap, Dream Fragments maybe not; the Solar Amulet the best base but costly; Mnemonic Rings
  the easiest, biggest mana source; Split Personality liable to spike.
- The author's own choices: Urgent Totems over Dialla's Desire, Enfeeble over Blink.
- Its budget claim: no tier needs a high budget to clear all content.

**Pattern shapes.**

- **A count as a multiplier** (existing): two counts multiply. Totems feed Spell Totem's Gemling cast-speed
  line, each paid in 75 Spirit; chains feed Arc's, doubled by Dominus' Grasp. Lines checked, totals not.
- **Quality as a stat line** (existing): one ascendancy lights two gems' quality lines in one group; 21/23 Arc
  is a quality stage (unverified).
- **Proxy multiplier** (existing): totems as copies; instances = the limit, six (checked).
- **Cost redirection** and **Forced condition** (existing): Atziri's Communion moves Archmage to life, holding
  low life for Pain Attunement (unverified).
- **Weapon set 2 job** (existing): debuffs on set 2, resting on set 1 (unverified).
- **Filler to satisfy a count** (existing): Gem Studded's red/blue tie, padded with reds (lines checked).
- **Socket amplifier plus latent lines** (existing): Darkness Enthroned (50–100% increased effect of socketed
  augments, body-armour variant, checked) carries a Fox Idol whose quality line is Bonded in the pin; whether
  it applies on a Gemling is unchecked.
- **New: gear buys out an ascendancy node.** When gear supplies a node's flat bonus (+2 Arc levels for
  Neurological Implants), move the point to a multiplier (Integrated Efficiency). Close to question 13 (find
  a supply that frees a slot propping up the engine).
- **New, unverified: the player's stat, the totem's cast.** Totem-cast Arc reads the player's Archmage bonus,
  while Archmage's extra mana cost may never reach the player. The guide doesn't say so; it is my reading of
  why 5k mana pays without a cost problem. Needs in-game testing.

**What PoB might not model.**

- **The totem count.** Coverage flags Spell Totem's totem stats as unmapped (PoB may derive them from skill
  types); how it counts summoned totems for the Gemling line, and whether six totems multiply the damage, is
  not established.
- **The Gemling lines** on Arc and Spell Totem: not established (coverage checked them at quality 0; PG-31).
- **Remaining chains:** the bonus changes with a target's place in the chain; PoB's figure is not established.
- **Also:** Spell Totem's charge lines are possibly unmodelled (moot under Ancestral Bond); Archmage and Mana
  Tempest on totem casts, the weapon-set split, Virtuous Barrier's motes and Embitter's shatter are not
  established; Gem Studded ties (PG-1).

**Against the library's Totems kit.** The kit's one example (Empyrean's Shockwave Totem) puts survival
supports before a second totem. This guide puts count first, takes survival from the gem, keeps placement speed
at every tier, and raises one totem six times. The Limits line (Ancestral Bond, 75 Spirit a totem) is its core.
