# Mobalytics review 11: Lazy Exile's 1-Button Ballista Totem Cluster Grenade (Gemling Legionnaire)

An agent's study report, kept verbatim (2026-10-18). Brief: `briefs/2026-10-18-mobalytics-review.md`, row 11,

## 2. What the build is

- **Damage source:** Cluster Grenade, a crossbow grenade dealing physical damage partly converted to fire. The
  player never fires it: Mortar Cannon, a crossbow skill, raises a Ballista Totem that fires the grenade skills
  socketed into it (**checked**, gem text; Cluster Grenade lists Mortar Cannon among the meta gems that can use
  it). The grenade bounces, explodes on its fuse and throws a ring of 18 mini grenades (**checked**).
- **Ascendancy:** Gemling Legionnaire. Its one ascendancy line calls Advanced Thaumaturgy (gem quality grants
  socketed skills an extra effect, **checked**) the build-enabler: one more totem, and quality-of-life on Cluster
  Grenade. At the pin, Mortar Cannon's Gemling line at 20% quality raises the totem limit by one, and Cluster
  Grenade's is 40% reduced detonation time (**checked**).
- **How it plays:** place ballistas, and place them again; the Interlude's rotation reads only "What
  rotation?". A totem lasts 8 seconds and can be placed while moving (**checked**); packs and bosses get the
  same. Weaknesses: screen clutter and PC load.
- **Leveling is another build:** a crossbow "shotgun" to level 52, then a gated swap (§5).

## 3. The kit

- **Weapon:** a two-handed crossbow, no off hand; Mortar Cannon and Cluster Grenade both require one
  (**checked**). From level 79, a crafted Siege Crossbow (§6).
- **Main group:** Mortar Cannon (level 19 by Budget) socketing Cluster Grenade, Urgent Totems III, Payload and
  Multishot II; Mid-Budget adds Magnified Area II; Endgame swaps it for Atalui's Bloodletting. At the pin
  (**checked**): Mortar Cannon gives its grenades 100% increased cooldown recovery, 50% less attack speed and 50%
  less damage; Payload trades 70% reduced cooldown recovery for a 50% chance that each grenade activates twice;
  Atalui's turns mana costs into life costs and adds 2% of damage as extra physical per 20 life cost, up to 40%.
- **Other skills (Endgame):** Rite of Restoration, granted by Reverie (**checked**), with Prolonged Duration II,
  Second Wind III, Cooldown Recovery II and Magnified Area II; Eternal Rage with Herbalism II, and Berserk;
  Freezing Mark with Mark of Siphoning II; Infernal Cry, Seismic Cry, Shockwave Totem, Orb of Storms and Gas
  Grenade with four supports each. Virtuous Barrier, from the Essence of Virtue notable (**checked**), is in every
  panel from A2; the guide says it isn't used.
- **Why so many groups:** Budget explains them as colour balance, so that Gem Studded gives all three effects;
  later tabs ask for ten supports per colour for Gem Studded and Gem Enthusiast. Gem Studded rewards whichever
  colours are most numerous (**checked**), so all three need a three-way tie; the Endgame panel holds exactly 30
  supports. The guide never says which cries or the mark the player presses. Gem Enthusiast isn't a Gemling node
  at the pin, presumably a main-tree node (unverified).
- **Spirit:** Budget runs Eternal Rage (20% quality) and Berserk on 114 Spirit, at least 14 of it from the Idol
  of Uldurn amulet. Mid-Budget and Endgame want 40+ Spirit on the amulet for Herbalism II, socketed as Herbalism
  I and then swapped (the direct route can bug Eternal Rage).
- **Uniques and when:** Igniferis (amulet, A3); Reverie (body, Budget on); Idol of Uldurn (Budget, Mid-Budget);
  Ryslatha's Coil (belt, Mid-Budget); Shavronne's Satchel (belt, Endgame). Endgame jewels: Split Personality
  (Templar variant), From Nothing for the Blood Magic keystone, Undying Hate (Amanamu), Prism of Belief at +2
  Cluster Grenade (the variant rolls +1 to +3, **checked**).
- **Ascendancy:** no order. Named: Advanced Thaumaturgy and Gem Studded; Essence of Virtue is implied from A2.
- **Tree:** unreadable. Main points left of 123 fall from 106 (A1) to 39 (Interlude), then −14 on Budget and
  Mid-Budget and 5 on Endgame; weapon-set points are overdrawn (−4 of 20) on Budget and Mid-Budget only. A note
  explains it: the tree really starts from the Templar area through Split Personality, which neither PoB nor
  Mobalytics let the author plan, so the Mercenary-area points shown are refunded once the jewel is in.

## 4. The levers

The page ranks nothing. In the order its text raises them:

1. **Gemling quality.** The extra totem comes first; the swap needs four or more Gemcutter's Prisms, Cluster
   Grenade first, the spare to Mortar Cannon.
2. **The crossbow's extra ballista.** The craft ends by unveiling +1 to maximum summoned ballista totems, certain,
   it says, as the only Ulaman option. The pin has that Ulaman desecrated crossbow prefix (**checked**), and two
   sources the guide doesn't mention: the Artillery Strike notable (attack skills get one more ballista) and a
   Cumbrous Crossbow's implicit (**checked**). Mortar Cannon lacks the Attack type, so whether Artillery Strike
   reaches it is unverified.
3. **Weapon physical damage:** a % increased physical roll above 60% (Budget) or a Tyrannical one above 155%
   (Mid-Budget), quality, Iron runes rising to a Perfect Iron Rune, Farrul's Rune of the Hunt.
4. **Cluster Grenade levels:** Prism of Belief.
5. **Rage:** Eternal Rage and Berserk; whether the player's Rage reaches the totem's attacks is unverified.

My reading of the checked text: each ballista fires at half speed for half damage, so the count (three from the
two sources the guide names) and cooldown recovery carry the build. If cooldown recovery adds up, Payload leaves
100% + 20% (Cluster Grenade's quality) − 70% = +50% against +120%: a rate of 1.5/2.2 ≈ ×0.68 times 1.5 activations, about
×1.02 while the cooldown sets the pace, and a pure gain while attack speed does.

**Skips:** Oil Grenade's exposure in Acts 3–4; an Act 4 weapon (Bombard holds to 59); From Nothing if its price
rises; Virtuous Barrier; Fragmentation Rounds except on frozen or rare targets.

## 5. Leveling

- **A1 (1–15):** Permafrost Bolts, Fragmentation Rounds, Frost Bomb; play slowly until Herald of Ice. Bosses:
  Frost Bomb for exposure, Permafrost Bolts to freeze, Fragmentation Rounds on the frozen target. Packs:
  Permafrost Bolts with Herald of Ice's explosions. Elemental Armament I on all three. The "dual shotgun" locks
  each ammo skill to one weapon set and binds each set's Crossbow Shot to a mouse button, so neither needs
  loading. Crossbows: Makeshift (1), Tense (5), Sturdy (bought at 10).
- **A2 (15–31):** Freezing Mark joins; Elemental Armament II, Freeze, Volt and Concentrated Area on the ammo.
  Varnished Crossbow at 16, all in on crafting it.
- **A3 (31–45):** Oil Grenade with Potent Exposure; Eternal Mark; Shard Scavenger. Bombard Crossbow at 33.
- **A4 (45–52), the gate:** don't swap before level 52, uncut skill gems of level 13 (Cluster Grenade's tier,
  **checked**; Mortar Cannon is 11), a Greater Jeweller's Orb, four or more Gemcutter's Prisms and about 30k gold.
  Time of Need with Vitality I, Clarity II and Cooldown Recovery II; Mortar Cannon linked and waiting.
- **Interlude (52–65):** the swap. Eternal Rage replaces Time of Need at 58; Cannonade Crossbow at 59, crafted.
  The ammo skills stay in the panel at level 12.
- **Budget on:** Siege Crossbow at 79, Reverie, and the weapon-set snapshot (§7).

## 6. Gear and budget

- **Leveling:** take what drops; flat damage on the weapon and rings first, movement speed on boots, flat damage
  on gloves.
- **Budget, about 1 divine:** a challenge from the video, prices moving. Gladiatorial Helm, Siege Crossbow,
  Topaz and Iron rings, Idol of Uldurn, Reverie, Feathered Mitts, Rawhide Belt, Bastion Sabatons. The weapon
  recipe in outline: an essence for physical damage, a desecrated prefix guided by omens, a guided exalt,
  quality and Iron runes, then the unveil.
- **Mid-Budget:** Prismatic and Unset rings, Ryslatha's Coil, a Tyrannical crossbow with a Perfect Essence of
  Battle and Farrul's rune. Its checklist: Reverie with the smallest "less recovery" roll, Ryslatha's Coil with
  two or more charm slots, capped resistances, an Ultimate Life Flask with high increased life recovery (90%+).
- **Endgame:** Ancestral Tiara, two Amethyst Rings, Lunar Amulet, Reverie, Sirenscale Gloves, Shavronne's
  Satchel, Sandsworn Sandals, the Siege Crossbow and four jewels. The unique jewels are the pieces whose price
  moves; the rest it calls cheap for an endgame setup.
- **Order:** the numbered lists follow slot order, except Endgame's, which puts the crossbow last.

## 7. Defences

- **No numbers:** no life, energy shield, armour or max-hit figures; the listed strengths include high
  recovery and running all map mods.
- **Reverie** (armour and energy shield, **checked**): life flasks can't be used, but non-unique ones apply
  constantly, at 40–60% less recovery (**checked**).
- **The snapshot** (Budget, Mid-Budget): weapon set II carries life flask recovery passives. With Rite of
  Restoration bound to set II and Mortar Cannon to set I, you swap with Rite, re-trigger the flask or Reverie (or
  enter a zone on set II), and the higher recovery holds for the zone. The creator's claim; the Endgame tab drops
  it, and its counters show no weapon-set points spent.
- **Rite of Restoration** with Second Wind III and Cooldown Recovery II.
- **Gem Studded:** red removes the critical damage bonus of hits against you; blue cuts costs 30% (**checked**).
- **Resistances** capped (Mid-Budget); the Amethyst rings presumably cover chaos (my reading).
- **Campaign:** Time of Need with Vitality; quest picks include a charm slot and two +30% global defences.

## 8. What players pick

The guide says little about common practice:

- Flat damage on the weapon and rings is called effective for any attack build.
- It strongly recommends the dual-shotgun binding for "the shotgun build", treating crossbow-shotgun leveling as
  a known template (my reading).
- Price signals: From Nothing cost 9–10 exalts when filmed; the unique jewels move with the market; the
  1-divine setup is a showcase, not a typical spend.
- It offers no alternative to Cluster Grenade and says nothing of what ballista players usually run.

## 9. For the library

**Kit lines** (Totems, unless marked):

1. **Kit (ballistas):** Mortar Cannon, a crossbow-only Ballista Totem that fires the grenade socketed in it,
   carrying Cluster Grenade with Urgent Totems III, Payload and Multishot II; the player only re-places the
   ballistas, which last 8 s [mob-ballista] §2, §3. Checked.
2. **Count:** one ballista, +1 from Mortar Cannon's Gemling quality line, +1 from an Ulaman desecrated crossbow
   prefix [mob-ballista] §4; Artillery Strike and a Cumbrous Crossbow's implicit add one each (pin: lookup).
   Checked, except whether Artillery Strike reaches Mortar Cannon.
3. **The trade:** Mortar Cannon's grenades take 50% less attack speed and 50% less damage for 100% increased
   cooldown recovery, so the count and cooldown recovery carry the damage; Payload's second activation about
   pays for its 70% reduced cooldown recovery [mob-ballista] §4. Text checked; the arithmetic is mine.
4. **Levers:** Gemling quality on both gems, Cluster Grenade first; weapon physical damage; Cluster Grenade
   levels (Prism of Belief); Rage [mob-ballista] §4. Unranked by the guide.
5. **Crossbows, leveling:** a freeze shotgun to level 52: Permafrost Bolts freezes, Fragmentation Rounds hits
   frozen and rare targets, Herald of Ice clears, Elemental Armament on each; one ammo skill per weapon set so
   neither needs loading [mob-ballista] §5. The binding is unverified.
6. **Swap gate:** ballistas at level 52, once uncut skill gems reach level 13 (Cluster Grenade's tier), with four
   Gemcutter's Prisms for quality [mob-ballista] §5. Tier checked.
7. **Crossbows, Spirit:** Eternal Rage and Berserk on about 114 Spirit [mob-ballista] §3, agreeing with the

8. **Defence:** Reverie's constant life flask, with Second Wind and Cooldown Recovery on its Rite of Restoration
   [mob-ballista] §7. Item text checked; the snapshot is the creator's claim.

**Against the current kits.** The Totems kit (a totem repeating the player's combo, survival supports, then a
second totem) doesn't fit: the ballista is the whole damage source, takes no survival supports (the gem grants
65–75% elemental resistances, **checked**), and its count comes from gem quality and the weapon, not Ancestral
Bond. In the Crossbows kit, Fresh Clip, Rapid Attacks and Emergency Reload vanish once the player stops firing;
Rage with Berserk agrees. The crossbow is bought for physical damage and the extra ballista, not projectile
levels; whether those reach a grenade fired by Mortar Cannon wasn't checked.

**Pattern shapes:**

- **Proxy multiplier** (existing): ballistas fire the socketed grenade, instances at the totem limit.
- **Actor rerouting** (existing): the press places a totem, opening a Ballista pool: five Ballista Damage nodes
  (15% each), Trained Turrets (crit), Kept at Bay (attack speed) (**checked** to exist; whether the guide takes
  them is unreadable).
- **Cooldown recovery as DPS** (existing): a cooldown grenade on a totem with 100% increased cooldown recovery;
  Payload's trade.
- **Quality as a stat line** (existing): an extra totem and a shorter fuse (**checked**).
- **Filler to satisfy a count** and **Low-level utility gems** (existing): 30 supports, ten per colour, for Gem
  Studded's three-way tie.
- **Weapon set 2 job** (existing): the split ammo while leveling; the life-flask snapshot.
- **A rule changer** (existing): Reverie makes life flasks constant. Split Personality is a candidate **new**
  entry, "borrowed starting point": a jewel lets the tree path from a second class's start.
- **Cost redirection** (existing): From Nothing's Blood Magic beside Atalui's Bloodletting; the guide doesn't
  explain the pairing (unverified).

**What PoB might not model:**

- Ballista count as damage: not established. Mortar Cannon's totem stats are unmapped (**checked**, coverage
  flag); measure before setting Count (the group's Count multiplies its damage) to the limit.
- Cluster Grenade's 50% gain as fire after consuming 15 Heat is possibly unmodelled (**checked**, coverage); the
  guide never mentions Heat.
- Payload's second activation parses (**checked**); whether PoB's DPS counts it is not established.
- Gem Studded's three-way tie: PoB grants one colour on a tie (PG-1).
- The snapshot is game behaviour: not modelled. Reverie's constant flask: not established.
- Split Personality: the creator says PoB couldn't plan the Templar start; unchecked at the pin. A plan copying
  the tree would keep the refunded Mercenary points.
- Whether Berserk's Rage reaches the totem's attacks: not established.
