## 2. What the build is

- **Damage:** attacks thrown from a one-handed spear, converted to lightning (Lightning Spear and Storm
  Lance) or fire (Explosive Spear), with Elemental Armament II on almost every damage skill.
- **Packs:** Lightning Spear. The thrown spear bursts on impact and fires lightning bolts at other enemies
  around it (checked: 5 bolts at gem level 20, +2 at 20% quality). The guide calls it one of the game's best
  clears, with the hedge "conditionally".
- **Bosses:** throw Storm Lance, then Explosive Spear three times. Storm Lance lodges a spear that zaps nearby
  enemies; when another skill detonates it, it fires a 16-bolt volley at 405% of base and expires. Explosive
  Spear has the Detonator skill type (checked). The guide never states the link, but the rotation is a
  placed-detonator combo: plant a lance, then blow it up.
- **Rares:** Fury of the Mountain (a Bear shapeshift slam from a talisman), then Storm Lance.
- **Frenzy Charges:** Fury of the Mountain is said to generate them for Storm Lance, not Lightning Spear.
  At the pin all three spear skills consume one when available, for a stronger mode (checked): Lightning
  Spear splits into three copies; Storm Lance fires every 0.2 s, self-detonates and leaves Shocked Ground;
  Explosive Spear explodes at once in a cross with Ignited Ground.
- **Ascendancy:** Amazon, for Predatory Instinct and Critical Strike; Deadeye may be faster, but the Amazon
  maps more comfortably on a low budget.
- **How it plays:** ranged, several buttons, not tanky (the page's own weaknesses). The one "How it Plays"
  note warns that lodged Storm Lances are capped and expire, so spamming it wastes charges.

## 3. The kit

**Weapon and off hand.** A rare one-handed spear (the author prefers the Akoyan Spear base) on weapon set 1.
Weapon set 2 holds a talisman: a Changeling Talisman while levelling, then Amor Mandragora (checked: a unique
Changeling Talisman with added physical damage and Druidic Prowess per 20 Rage spent). The off hand is empty from Act 2 to early maps for Dance with Death (checked:
25% more skill speed with an empty off hand and a one-handed martial weapon). Budget Setup and End Game drop
the keystone and fill the off hand with a sceptre (Omen Sceptre; "Guiding Palm of the Mind" in End Game,
base unverified).

**Main skills and supports (End Game).**
- Lightning Spear: Innervate, Elemental Armament II, Fork, Pinpoint Critical, Rapid Attacks II.
- Storm Lance: Multishot II, Elemental Armament II, Close Combat II, Prolonged Duration II, Blindside.
- Explosive Spear: Overabundance I, Short Fuse I, Elemental Armament II, Elemental Focus.
- Fury of the Mountain: Brambleslam, Rageforged I, Rage III, Branching Fissures II, Rapid Attacks III.
- Elemental Surge (the skill the Amazon node grants): Blind II, Concentrated Area, Elemental Armament II,
  Elemental Focus, Pinpoint Critical.
- Mirage Archer holding Lightning Spear, with Overcharge, Fork and Cooldown Recovery II.

**Other skills.** Herald of Thunder (Magnified Area II, Elemental Armament II, Elemental Focus); Sniper's Mark
(Charge Profusion II, Mark of Siphoning II); Refutation with duration and cooldown supports, a buff that
spends all Runic Ward to block every blockable hit for 4 s and applies Parried (50% more attack damage
taken) to blocked enemies (checked); Purity of Lightning; Eternal Rage with Precision I and Clarity II.

**Spirit costs** (Early Mapping): Eternal Rage 100, Herald of Thunder 30, Wind Dancer 30, Precision I 10,
Precision II 20, Clarity I 10; Enfolding Dawn's 100 Spirit pays for them all.

**Uniques and when.** Enfolding Dawn (body) and Amor Mandragora from Early Mapping; End Game adds The Black
Insignia (helmet), Beacon of Azis (amulet) and The Brass Dome (body). Only Amor Mandragora was looked up.

**Ascendancy.** Predatory Instinct first (A2 tab), Critical Strike, then a choice: Mystic Harvest with
Essentia Sanguis for leech, or Stalking Panther with Evasion, Deflection and Ghost Dance. Elemental Surge is
in the endgame gem list. Checked at the pin: Predatory Instinct reveals weaknesses on rares and uniques for
50% more damage against them; Critical Strike turns 25% of hit chance above 100% into crit chance; Elemental
Surge gives 3 Lightning Surges whenever a charge is consumed; Mystic Harvest lets leech read elemental damage;
Stalking Panther doubles helmet, glove and boot evasion and halves the body armour's. Essentia Sanguis is
not among the pin's 19 Amazon nodes (unverified what it is).

**Tree.** Only direction: Strength early (Eternal Rage needs 108), then attributes evenly. The main counter
(points left of 123, my reading) runs A1 106 down to Early Mapping 15 and End Game −5; the weapon-set
counters read 8 of 20 only on Budget and End Game.

## 4. The levers

The guide gives no ranked damage list. In its own order of emphasis:

1. **Flat added damage on the weapon and both rings**, the most important slots for "any attack build". The
   spear craft: a magic base with T3+ increased physical, Essence of Abrasion, then Omen of Greater
   Exaltation with a Greater Exalted Orb.
2. **Accuracy**, which the author says is cheap to get on an Amazon. Critical Strike converts excess hit
   chance to crit; the Amazon's Penetrate node (not named by the guide) adds physical damage equal to 25% of
   the weapon's accuracy (checked).
3. **Frenzy Charges spent on Storm Lance**, and Predatory Instinct's 50% more against rares and uniques.
4. **Spirit** for Eternal Rage, Herald of Thunder and Wind Dancer: about +30 on body armour or amulet early,
   then Enfolding Dawn.
5. **Gem quality and sockets:** campaign drops go to Lightning Spear (Gemcutter's Prism, "+1 bolt"; Greater
   Jeweller's Orb) and Storm Lance (Lesser Jeweller's Orb).

**Skippable, by the guide:** every support except the Spirit gems is optional, quality of life; a lower Rage
support is fine; without 30 spare Spirit, run Eternal Rage and drop Herald of Thunder and Wind Dancer; Frost
Bomb goes around level 20. Skill levels are never mentioned.

## 5. Leveling

- **A1 (1–15):** spears from level 1, "a bit slower" than other archetypes. Explosive Spear (two lodged),
  Spear Throw and Frost Bomb, Contagion for packs; Lightning Spear (the Red Vale) clears from level 6; Storm
  Lance (three lodged, Ogham Village) at 14; Herald of Thunder; Pounce with a talisman on set 2. Bases:
  Hardwood (1), Ironhead (5), Hunting Spear (10); league-mechanic Exalted and Artificer's Orbs go on a
  level-16 weapon.
- **A2 (15–31):** Ferocious Roar (Raging Cry) for Rage, Sniper's Mark for mana, Multishot II on Storm Lance,
  Predatory Instinct, Dance with Death. A vendor regex finds movement-speed boots and flat-damage spears and
  gloves. Bases: Winged (16, craft fully), War (21), Hunting Spear (26).
- **A3 (31–45):** Explosive Spear's third spear at gem level 8; Wind Dancer (Maim, Blind I). Bases: Barbed
  Spear (33, full craft), Broad Spear (40).
- **A4 (45–53):** Lightning Spear gains Innervate. Bases: Crossblade (45), Seaglass (51, full craft), Sword
  Spear (52). Keep a skill gem ready for Fury of the Mountain.
- **Interlude (53–65):** the second Interlude first (its sword boss is awkward), then level 58 by Kriar
  Village, where Eternal Rage drops. Ferocious Roar and Wind Dancer give way to Eternal Rage and Fury of the
  Mountain (Brambleslam, Rage III, Rageforged I kept at level 1). Bases: Branched (54), Jagged Spear (59).
- **Early Mapping:** Akoyan Spear, Enfolding Dawn, Amor Mandragora; Fire Penetration II on Explosive Spear;
  Sniper's Mark joins the rotation for damage.

## 6. Gear and budget

- **Early Mapping** buys, in order: spear, rings, Stellar Amulet, gloves, boots, Enfolding Dawn, Amor
  Mandragora. Defence advice: energy shield and evasion gear, except the body armour.
- **Budget Setup** swaps Sniper's Mark for Refutation, adds a Beacon of Azis and an Omen Sceptre, and keeps
  Enfolding Dawn. Requirements stay 60/50/30.
- **End Game** order: spear, The Black Insignia, Amor Mandragora, the off-hand sceptre, rings, Beacon of Azis,
  The Brass Dome, gloves, boots. Adds Purity of Lightning, Mirage Archer and Elemental Surge, brings Sniper's
  Mark back beside Refutation, and drops Pounce and Wind Dancer; requirements rise to 60/85/55.
- **1 Div Poison Variant**, a for-fun challenge: Splinter of Lorrata always poisons and deals no elemental
  damage, Original Sin turns elemental to chaos, so every hit poisons and elemental damage scales it; Toxic
  Domain's cooldown is the clear. Storm Lance takes Arakaali's Lust and Deadly Poison II, Lightning Spear
  Tacati's Ire and Bursting Plague; Despair, Herald of Plague, Berserk. Unverified (no lookups left).
- No currency figures for any tab, and no item tiers.

## 7. Defences

- End Game: with Refutation's block window in the kit, cap resistances first; life and energy shield with
  evasion would be ideal, though the author says you realistically won't need them.
- Earlier tabs: energy shield and evasion on every slot but the body armour. Stalking Panther, one of the
  offered ascendancy picks, halves body-armour evasion (checked), which fits leaving that slot to Spirit.
- Leech through Mystic Harvest, or Ghost Dance with evasion and deflection.
- Campaign picks: a charm slot (Act 2), mana regeneration or ailment threshold (Act 3), all attributes
  (Act 4), movement speed or global defences (Interlude).
- No life, energy shield, resistance or max-hit numbers. The page calls the build not tanky.

## 8. What players pick

The page says little about common practice:
- Lightning Spear is among the game's best clears, with a hedge.
- Deadeye may be better and faster; the Amazon is the comfortable low-budget pick.
- Weapon and rings carry flat damage for any attack build.
- In the campaign, buying and crafting a better spear base is the usual route.
- The 3.2K favourites are the only popularity signal the page gives.
