## 2. What the build is

The damage source is **Apocalypse**, granted by Shaman's Bringer of the Apocalypse (checked). At the pin it is a
buff spell (checked): Glory builds from hits that deal elemental damage; at 100 Glory you may cast it for a 12 s
buff that triggers one of three disasters every 0.75 s: Hailstorm (cold), Meteor (fire, lands after 2 s) or Crash
Lightning (three pulses, a final strike after 3 s). The guide says it picks its own target.

**Trampletoe** (unique boots, level 27, checked): 15% movement speed and "Deal 30% of Overkill damage to enemies
within 2 metres of the enemy killed". A disaster kills one enemy, its overkill kills the neighbours, theirs kills
the next, and the pack chain-reacts.

**The loop, link by link:**

1. **Pressed: Entangle**, a few casts at map start or on a boss, fills Glory. Entangle is physical at the pin
   (checked), and Glory needs elemental damage on the hit; the page doesn't say what supplies it. Turning of the
   Seasons (Shaman: 10% of damage as extra of a random element, checked) would; which the build relies on is
   unverified.
2. **Pressed: Apocalypse**, from weapon set 2, the damage set; then **Freezing Mark**, set 1 only, which swaps back
   to the tank set. The guide says the buff keeps set 2's damage, a snapshot (unverified: no line at the pin says so).
3. **Unpressed:** a disaster every 0.75 s; its kill seeds Trampletoe's chain, which the guide says uses set 1's
   stats, since set 1 is out when the enemy dies (unverified).
4. **Unpressed: sprinting.** Nothing is cast, so the sprint never breaks.

**The open link:** at the pin the buff lasts 12 s (more with Prolonged Duration II) and costs 100 Glory, yet the
guide says one cast covers the rest of the map. The page never says how it renews: the disasters' own elemental
hits may refill Glory for a recast, or 0.5.5 changed it. It does lapse: the weaknesses say Apocalypse ends when a
wave thins to one enemy, and Spark or the Sniper's Mark trick restarts it.

**Packs** die about 0.7 s after the player arrives. **Bosses:** Entangle for Glory, Freezing Mark to freeze through
immunity phases, Flame Wall (Arbiter's Ignition) and Mana Tempest. Those three elements fill **Rising Tempest** on
Apocalypse, 12% more elemental damage per skill of a different element used recently (checked): 36%, as the guide
says. **Glory shortcut:** Quipolatl's Thesis in the helmet, Sniper's Mark with Eternal Mark, then Entangle with
Pinpoint Critical and Inexorable Critical fills Glory in about 1 s (Quipolatl's Thesis isn't in the pin's data:
unverified). The author: you "Barely have to hit any buttons".

## 3. The kit

- **Weapon sets:** set 1, worn nearly always (tank, sprint, Trampletoe's damage): Sacred Flame (sceptre) and
  Saffell's Frame. Set 2 (Apocalypse): Galvanic Wand and Sunsplinter, a buckler with +1, +2 and +3 to fire, cold
  and lightning skill levels in some order (checked; the guide says +7, the pin's variants sum to +6). Without
  Sacred Flame: a sceptre with 140+ Spirit, no Elemental Conflux, Trinity on both sets.
- **Assignment,** the page's loudest rule: Apocalypse and Trinity on set 2 only (green points), every other gem on
  set 1 only (red points), or no damage and many deaths.
- **Apocalypse:** Prolonged Duration II, Zenith II, Rising Tempest, Dialla's Desire, Fire Mastery.
- **Other skills:** Mana Tempest (Efficiency II, Cooldown Recovery II, Lifetap, Morgana's Tempest); Blasphemy with
  Elemental Weakness and Temporal Chains (Ritualistic Curse, Magnified Area II, Slow Potency); Mana Remnants;
  Entangle at gem level 7 (Rapid Casting II, Branching Fissures II, Zenith II, Pinpoint Critical, Inexorable
  Critical II); Flame Wall; Trinity; Freezing Mark and Sniper's Mark with mark supports; Elemental Conflux; Purity of
  Fire (Cannibalism II, Clarity II, Vitality II, Her Declaration). No reservation figures. Str 55, Dex 50, Int 95.
- **Uniques:** Trampletoe is the enabler (the author bought it in Act 3; Ritual maps drop it); the endgame adds
  Scold's Bridle, Cloak of Flame, Sacred Flame, Saffell's Frame, Sunsplinter, Kalandra's Touch and The Anvil.
- **Ascendancy:** Bringer of the Apocalypse at the Act 3 trial; the text also leans on Reactive Growth, Avatar of
  Evolution and an exposure aura that matches Turning of the Seasons ("Enemies in your Presence have Exposure",
  checked). No order.
- **Tree:** unreadable; the prose's aims are gain-as-extra, generic and area damage for the chain, sprint speed,
  damage taken from mana, recoup, and curse and slow magnitude.

## 4. The levers

No ranked list; the page's summary gives them in this order:

1. **Set 2's snapshot:** a very good wand plus Sunsplinter. Apocalypse's gem carries all three element tags
   (checked), so each element's level line reaches it.
2. **Scold's Bridle's spell damage** (60–100% at the pin, checked; the guide says 100%).
3. **Trinity on set 2**, fed by Apocalypse's mixed elements.
4. **The chain**, on set 1: gain-as-extra, generic increased, area and elemental damage keep the splash lethal.
   That they rescale a 30% overkill splash is not in the item text (unverified).
5. **Debuffs:** Rising Tempest, Elemental Weakness on Blasphemy, early Frost Bomb (bugged with Shaman's exposure
   aura unless cast from outside Presence, the guide reports).

**Sprint speed scales the clear.** The page's case: the build sprints about 90% of the time, never stops to cast and
buys sprint speed, so it out-clears nearly everything but Reverse Chill, Temporalis and Gemling Cast on Dodge, all
"way more expensive"; chain kills don't clutter the screen. No numbers. Its speed sources: Trampletoe's 15%, and the
belt: Midnight Braid (tank), Darkness Enthroned holding Guatelitzi's Thesis and Rune of the Chase (mid budget),
Headhunter or Mageblood (high), a rare (low). At the pin (checked): Darkness Enthroned's boots variant takes its two
sockets' bonuses as if it were boots, at 50–100% increased augment effect; Guatelitzi's Thesis gives 25% increased
movement speed while sprinting and loses 5% of maximum life per second while sprinting; the tree has Marathon Runner
(12%), Unfettered (10%) and 3% small nodes while sprinting. Rune of the Chase has no sprint line at the pin.

**Skippable:** crit (but Entangle's, for Glory), expensive items, and at higher budget the boss-time Blasphemy swap.

## 5. Leveling

- **Level 36 and 37:** Essence Drain and Contagion (Swift Affliction III on both) clear on set 1; Bonestorm (Armour
  Break III, Zenith II) and Apocalypse (Spell Cascade) take bosses on set 2. Entangle (Living Lightning II) and Orb
  of Storms (Living Lightning) make Living Lightning, said to use Bonestorm's Impale, and Arcane Surge, which the tree
  pays. Also Blasphemy with Temporal Chains, Mana Tempest and a self-cast Elemental Weakness. Gear: Withered Wand and
  Tasalian Focus (set 1), Ashen Staff (set 2). The two tabs' gem panels match, Apocalypse included.
- **Act 3:** ascend to Shaman for Apocalypse; on bosses, cast it and keep up Bonestorm.
- **Level 64, No Trampletoe:** the same; Apocalypse swaps Spell Cascade for Concentrated Area.
- **The swap is Trampletoe** (level 27, checked), whenever it is found or bought; the author switched on day 1 in
  Act 3 and used Apocalypse for everything after.
- **Level 56, Have Trampletoe:** Apocalypse-only at gem level 13: Apocalypse (Prolonged Duration II, Zenith II,
  Concentrated Area), Mana Tempest, Blasphemy (Temporal Chains, Elemental Weakness), Mana Remnants, Entangle 7,
  Purity of Lightning, Freezing Mark 7. Gear: two Attuned Wands, Prismatic Rings, Stellar Amulet, Doedre's Tenure,
  Fine Belt, Trampletoe.
- **63** adds Trinity; **74** Fire Mastery on Apocalypse; **84** the mark supports; **92** Rising Tempest, gems near
  20. The gear doesn't change from 56 to 92.
- **Sekhema Carry** (league start, low-density Trial of Sekhema): Reaping Staff on set 1, Attuned Wand on set 2,
  Trinity on both; adds Reap, Frost Bomb (Potent Exposure) and Elemental Weakness.
- The quest widget lists the options; the capture doesn't show the picks.

## 6. Gear and budget

- **Recommended, in priority order:** Scold's Bridle, Sacred Flame, Galvanic Wand, Saffell's Frame, Breach Ring,
  Kalandra's Touch, The Anvil, Cloak of Flame, Sirenscale Gloves, Mageblood. **Giga Tank:** Midnight Braid for the
  belt. **Dodge Roll (0.5):** Galvanic Wands, Gold Ring, Surefooted Sigil, Vaal Gloves, Trampletoe. **Ridiculous:**
  Runeseeker's Call, Rathpith Globe, Absent Amulet, Atziri's Splendour and more; for six-player 200% Delirium,
  unoptimised, not the author's own.
- **Budget:** cheap early on common uniques, then better corruptions and Exceptional uniques with an extra socket.
  Three elements let it take caster weapons others skip (+fire spell weapons were cheap in 0.4), which suits SSF.
  League start: an amulet with + spell skills and resistances. No currency figures.

## 7. Defences

- **Reactive Growth** (checked): 10% less elemental damage taken; adapt to each hit's highest element; 10% less of
  that element per matching Adaptation. **Avatar of Evolution** (checked): 5% of physical taken as each element,
  plus two lines the lookup cut.
- **Self-damage for Adaptation:** Scold's Bridle (checked: take 25–100% of mana costs paid as physical damage) and
  Cloak of Flame make each cast a fire self-hit and an Adaptation; three give 60% less fire taken, the guide says
  (the lines seen don't reach 60%: unverified). Entangle must cost enough mana: raise its level.
- **Layers:** 90% all resistances; most physical taken as elemental; damage split between life and mana; recoup on
  both; Enfeeble on bosses (no tab lists it); Temporal Chains on Blasphemy; freezes through boss phases.
- **Claims, no numbers:** it tanks Uber Arbiter's one-shots and Simulacrum's last wave; the tank version ignores
  waystone modifiers. The weak moment is Apocalypse's cast in set 2: step aside first.

## 8. What players pick

Little: a league-start amulet with + spell skills and resistances is what "you'll likely use"; fire-spell weapons
were under-played in 0.4, hence cheap; the belt is flexible. The rest is the author's practice. At 20 favourites it
is niche, picked for its loop: its lines belong in a kit marked "(thin: one study)".
