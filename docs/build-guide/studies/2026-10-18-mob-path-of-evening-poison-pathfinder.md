## 2. What the build is

A bow build whose damage is poison sized from the bow's physical hit: the guide's lever list leads with physical
damage and calls added elemental and increased chaos damage useless (§4).

**Ascendancy.** Pathfinder. At the pin, Overwhelming Toxicity doubles the number of your poisons an enemy can
carry, at 50% less poison duration, and Relentless Pursuit leaves your speed unaffected by slows (both checked):
the guide's immunity to every slow. The Early Endgame note also credits Pathfinder with built-in poison spreading;
no Pathfinder node at the pin spreads poison (checked, all 24), so it is a 0.5.5 change or means Herald of Plague
(unverified).

**Packs.** Poisonburst Arrow's burst "does not Hit" but poisons everything within 2.4 metres (checked); Fork
splits it into packs (the guide). Kills then chain:
- Herald of Plague spreads the most damaging poison from a killed enemy within 1.8 metres (checked).
- Herald of Blood explodes enemies with Blood Loss when you kill them (checked). Bleed III sits on Poisonburst
  Arrow and Wind Dancer, and Bleed III, Poison III and Bursting Plague on the Herald, so each explosion should set
  up the next. The endgame page credits it with screen-wide clear; the chain is unverified.
- Bursting Plague adds Plague equal to the poison's damage per second (capped at 20% of the enemy's life); a death
  with Plague triggers a burst of the stored Plague as physical damage (checked).

**Bosses.** Lay Gas Arrow's cloud, fire Toxic Growth into it until it fades, repeat. The cloud lasts 4 s, poisons
without hitting and grows up to 80% in radius (checked). Toxic Growth rains five pustules (limit five) that hit on
landing and explode after 15 s; poisoned pustules detonate sooner and harder, storing the expected damage of the
poisons put on them for up to 300% more explosion damage and 1.5 metres more radius (checked). That the cloud
poisons the pustules is the guide's mechanism (unverified). Before Gas Arrow, Plague Bearer sets the pods off.

One Stormcaller Arrow shocks the boss: about +70% for roughly 30 s, says the endgame page (unverified). Its bolt
has 500% more chance to shock, shocks as though dealing 50% more damage, and spreads shock 3 metres (checked). The
Early Endgame note adds Voltaic Mark for up to 100% more damage, but Voltaic Mark is in no gem list.

## 3. The kit

- **Weapon:** a high-physical bow (Tribal Bow in Act 4, rare Warmonger Bow late). Slivertongue, which the guide
  prices at one Chaos Orb, carries Act 3 to tier 15 maps: a level-39 Zealot Bow with 40–48 to 65–72 added
  physical, +4–6% crit chance, life and mana leech, and arrows that fork then pierce (checked). Visceral Quiver
  throughout.
- **Weapon set 2:** the endgame page's bolded rule. Rigwald's Ferocity, on Poisonburst Arrow, gives 30% more damage
  and 10% reduced attack speed in set II, and 30% increased speed with 15% less damage in set I (checked). Each set
  has a 20-point weapon-set tree.
- **Main:** Poisonburst Arrow with Deadly Poison II (100% more poison magnitude, 30% less hit damage, checked),
  Bleed III, Bursting Plague, Rigwald's Ferocity, Fork.
- **Bosses:** Toxic Growth with Long Fuse II, Concentrated Area, Deadly Poison II, Arakaali's Lust (8% more hit
  damage per poison on the enemy, up to 40%) and Escalating Poison (+1 poison from the skill, 20% less duration;
  both checked). Gas Arrow with Multishot II, Deadly Poison II, Persistent Ground III (stops fire setting the cloud
  off early, unverified), Encroaching Ground, Overreach.
- **Debuffs:** Stormcaller Arrow with Shock, Lasting Shock, Overcharge, Commiserate and Inexorable Critical II
  (Lightning Attunement on the endgame page). Bloodhound's Mark with Mark for Death II, Mark of Siphoning II,
  Efficiency II, Uruk's Smelting.
- **Spirit:** Herald of Plague (Chaos Mastery); Herald of Blood (Poison III, Bleed III, Bursting Plague, Deadly
  Poison II, and Overreach or Magnified Area II); Wind Dancer (Blind II, Maim, Poison III, Bleed III, Life Leech
  III); Ghost Dance (Compressed Duration II, Clarity I). Spirit totals aren't captured.
- **Uniques:** Ryslatha's Coil on every late tab: 30–40% more maximum and 30–40% less minimum physical attack
  damage, +80–100 life (checked). The guide says it adds a lot of physical damage; it widens the range (my
  reading). Headhunter "also works well". The leveling page also lists Plaguefinger (elemental damage counts for
  poison magnitude), Corpsewade (poison-spreading boots), Snakebite (+1 poison stack, checked) and Voltaxic Rift
  (shock with all damage); the rest unverified.
- **Attributes at endgame:** Str 50–55, Dex 100–110, Int 25–35.

## 4. The levers

Both pages repeat one list, called the complete list of main scalers; it reads as a priority order:
1. physical damage on the bow; 2. damage with bow skills (quiver); 3. added physical to attacks (quiver, rings,
gloves); 4. skill gem levels (amulet, bow, quiver); 5. more poison stacks; 6. poison, ailment and damaging-ailment
magnitude, three separate stats that all apply; 7. crit chance and crit damage bonus; 8. poison duration and
faster poison; 9. shock; 10. added chaos to attacks (rare); 11. increased attack, physical and projectile damage.

**Skip:** added elemental damage (unless Plaguefinger); attack speed (feels good, adds no damage); chance to poison,
since the skills already poison (checked: Toxic Growth's explosion always poisons, Poisonburst Arrow's burst and
Gas Arrow's cloud poison without a hit); increased chaos damage, which the guide says affects hits only
(unverified).

**Stacks.** The Act 1 note says an enemy carries one of your poisons at first, why Poisonburst Arrow is weak early
(the guide's word). Each source of more is checked: +1 from Escalating Poison (its skill only), from the notables
Building Toxins and Stacking Toxins, from Snakebite or an Idol of Thruldana weapon rune; Overwhelming Toxicity
doubles the total. Two of these cut duration (20% and 50% less), hence duration's place on the list; Poisonburst
Arrow's poison reads skill effect duration (checked).

**Mana:** physical damage leeched as mana on rings, gloves or bow; 2% mana on kill on Sapphire Jewels.

- **Swap points:** Lightning Arrow and Lightning Rod clear until level 14 (The Executioner), when Toxic Growth
  unlocks; Gas Arrow unlocks at 22 (overview), and Plague Bearer pops the pods until then. The Act 2 note says to
  keep Plague Bearer's level up, yet every widget shows it at 4.
- **Campaign gear:** life, resistances, a big physical bow and quiver, added physical on rings and gloves,
  movement-speed boots; a vendor regex finds the boots and bows.
- **Quest picks:** charm charges, stun threshold, flask recovery, the three +5% resistances, the shark fin's 30%
  and the interlude's 15% to global defences.

**The handover.** The leveling page runs into the endgame; the endgame page has only Live and Endgame, so the
Slivertongue stage its overview advertises lives only on the leveling page's Early Endgame tab. The two Endgame
tabs differ in four places: Stormcaller Arrow's fifth support (Inexorable Critical II against Lightning
Attunement), Herald of Blood's (Magnified Area II against Overreach), the body (Sleek Jacket against Falconer's
Jacket) and attributes (Dex 100/Int 35 against Dex 110/Int 25). The endgame page drops the useful uniques and
the quest picks (all "Not Specified"), and adds the weapon-set rule, the map routine, the shock line and the belt
notes. Neither says when to leave Slivertongue for a rare bow.

## 6. Gear and budget

- **Bow progression:** any physical bow, a Tribal Bow by Act 4, Slivertongue (level 39) to tier 15, then a rare
  Warmonger Bow, possibly one per set (§1).
- **Stat priorities:** physical damage on the bow; added physical on quiver, rings and gloves; skill levels on
  amulet, bow and quiver; physical leech as mana; life, resistances, movement speed.
- **Endgame bases:** Kamasan or Skycrown Tiara, Prismatic and Ruby rings, Lunar or Stellar Amulet, Sleek or
  Falconer's Jacket, Adorned Wraps or Barbed Bracers, Daggerfoot or Quickslip Shoes.
- **Buy order:** each tab numbers the slots, but the capture separates the numbers from the slots, with no reasons.
- **Budget:** no prices. Slivertongue and Ryslatha's Coil are the cheap pieces; the endgame page's only weakness is
  expensive high-end gear; Headhunter is the luxury.

## 7. Defences

No numbers. Named layers: slow immunity (Relentless Pursuit, checked); Wind Dancer and Ghost Dance, with Blind
and Maim from Wind Dancer's supports; life leech (Slivertongue, Life Leech III); quest defences and resistances.
It calls itself tanky, but warns that Rangers are fragile in the early acts. The tree's defence nodes weren't
captured; whether it takes Pathfinder's Sustainable Practices (half of evasion also grants elemental damage
reduction) is unknown.

## 8. What players pick

The guide never says what is standard. The nearest: a good physical bow is all the campaign needs; Lightning
Arrow with Lightning Rod is the better setup for most of Act 1; Slivertongue reaches tier 15 and Ryslatha's Coil
is the cheap belt; the do-not-prioritize list (§4) names what players overvalue. The favourites, 819 against 199,
say most readers use it as a league starter.
