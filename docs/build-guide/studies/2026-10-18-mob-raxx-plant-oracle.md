## 2. What the build is

- **Damage source:** two Druid plant spells dealing physical damage. Entangle clears: a fissure crawls forward and
  vines from it latch onto nearby enemies, damaging and slowing them (pin). Thrashing Vines kills elites and
  bosses: vines sprout at random in a target area and crash down, turning toward nearby enemies (pin).
- **Ascendancy:** Oracle. The overview calls Forced Outcome the heart of the build. It grants Inevitable Critical
  Hits (pin), where a hit that fails to crit re-rolls until it does, losing crit bonus per re-roll ([oracle] chain
  4). With every cast a crit, Cast on Critical keeps firing Thunderstorm.
- **The engine:** Thunderstorm's rain makes plants in its area Overgrown (pin), and the guide treats keeping them
  "watered" as the big damage boost. At the pin, Overgrown Thrashing Vines deal 50% more damage and are 50%
  larger; Overgrown Entangle fissures expire 25% slower and attach up to 3 more vines per enemy, on top of 3; and
  Accelerated Growth II triggers a blinding explosion off each plant that Overgrows. The data names every
  Overgrown stat "when wet".
- **Packs:** run ahead casting Entangle; monsters chase you over the fissures.
- **Bosses:** place Sigil of Power near the boss, not on it; spam Entangle until the sigil reaches stage 4; drop
  one Thrashing Vines under the boss as it appears; then stand in the sigil casting three Entangles per Thrashing
  Vines. Spamming Thrashing Vines, the guide insists, loses damage and drains mana. The pin explains why: it keeps
  one growth area (limit 1) for 8 seconds, growing vines every half cast time, so a recast only replaces it.
- **Feel:** near one-button, sold as clearing all content without top gear. Weaknesses: chaos resistance,
  gear-dependent defence, mana on long fights.

## 3. The kit

**Weapons.** Set 1, for every plant spell: a rare Bone Wand with a Cultist Focus (Cheap), Tasalian Focus
(Expensive) or the unique Palm of the Dreamer (Mirror Tier). Set 2: a rare Chiming Staff, whose implicit grants
Sigil of Power (pin). The plant spells are bound to set 1 only.

**Entangle (20).** Cheap: Zenith II, Accelerated Growth II, Branching Fissures II, Heft, Physical Mastery. The upper
tabs replace Heft and Physical Mastery with Uul-Netol's Embrace (pin: 40% of physical gained as extra chaos, and
armour break from the chaos dealt) and Dialla's Desire (pin: +1 level, +5% quality). Branching Fissures II splits
each fissure twice, the branches at 30% less damage and 35% less area (pin).

**Thrashing Vines (20).** Rapid Casting II, Physical Mastery, Considered Casting, Concentrated Area, Heft. Mirror
Tier swaps Rapid Casting II for Zarokh's Refrain (pin: three echoes, with 40% increased area); the changelog says
its cooldown costs nothing because the skill isn't spammed.

**Cast on Critical (20), every tab.** Thunderstorm, Shock, Efficiency II, Boundless Energy II, Zenith II.
Thunderstorm is cut so it sits at exactly level 18 after +spell levels (16 with a +2 amulet). The guide's reason:
18 still guarantees the Drench, and so the shock, at the least mana.

**Mana Remnants (20).** Clarity II, Harmonic Remnants II, Remnant Potency I (Mirror Tier: Clarity I and adds Cool
Headed and Vitality I).

**Sigil of Power (19, set 2).** Prolonged Duration II, Cooldown Recovery II, Magnified Area II, Rapid Casting II. At
the pin it gains a stage per half of maximum mana spent inside it, at most one a second, up to 4 stages of 14%
more spell damage each.

**Mirror Tier only.** Blasphemy with Temporal Chains, Heightened Curse, Magnified Area II, Slow Potency and Clarity
II; Impurity on set 1 with Mysticism II, Herbalism II, Cannibalism II and Vitality II.

**Attributes.** The page lists support requirements (Str 30, Dex 10, Int 65; Mirror Tier 55/15/85). The skills ask
more: Entangle, Thrashing Vines and Thunderstorm each need 86 Str and 86 Int at level 20 (pin).

**Uniques and when.** Cheap: Nightscale gloves (mana regeneration gated on a recent crit, [oracle] chain 5).
Expensive: Headhunter, after which the blurb says everything is comfortable. Mirror Tier: Kalandra's Touch, Palm of
the Dreamer, a Heroic Tragedy (the author's is a 7894 Vorana), Legacy of Lifesprig, Idol of Yeena.

**Tree.** Only the changelog's names: Sudden Escalation (PoB showed more damage), Ruin (on the way to the right
side), and Cooked, anointed on the amulet on Cheap and Expensive (a real notable, pin: a Megalomaniac variant
allocates it; its text unchecked). On Mirror Tier a Raven-Touched Shard puts Cooked on the helmet, freeing the
amulet's anoint for Zarokh's Gift, a jewel socket the guide rates higher. Whether it takes Wildsurge Incantation
([oracle] chain 3) isn't captured. The pin's plant nodes: eight small Plant Skill Damage nodes (12% increased),
Boundless Growth (20% chance for plants to Overgrow at once), Return to Nature (Overgrown plant skills break 50%
increased armour) and Wildsurge Incantation.

## 4. The levers

No ranked list; this order follows the overview, the buy orders and the changelog.

1. **Constant Overgrowth:** sure crits → Cast on Critical → Thunderstorm over the plants.
2. **Thrashing Vines placed once per boss,** Entangle between casts.
3. **Sigil of Power at stage 4,** primed by Entangle's mana spend.
4. **The set 1 wand and focus:** the Cheap tab's first and third buys, around the staff.
5. **Mana held high:** Zenith II on Entangle and the trigger (pin: 30% more spell damage above 90% of maximum
   mana, 25% increased mana cost efficiency); the changelog calls it a big help with mana.
6. **Headhunter** (Expensive's first buy), then Uul-Netol's Embrace and Dialla's Desire on Entangle, then Zarokh's
   Refrain's echoes on Thrashing Vines.

**What it says you can skip:** Heroic Tragedy below Mirror Tier; hunting a good one at all, since they can't be
judged before buying: any strong jewel will do. Chaos resistance can sit short of the cap if it's high. Never spam
Thrashing Vines.

## 7. Defences

- **Layers:** energy shield (the strengths list), on Sacramental Robe, Ancestral Tiara and Sekhema Sandals (their
  defence types unchecked); Amethyst Rings, presumably for chaos resistance; control: each attached Entangle vine
  slows 15% (pin; three vines, six when Overgrown; the slow cap unchecked) and Accelerated Growth II blinds (pin).
  Mirror Tier adds Temporal Chains with Slow Potency, Impurity and Vitality supports.
- **Numbers:** none: no energy shield total, effective HP or maximum hit.
- **Mana is the soft spot:** the main skills, the trigger and the sigil all draw on mana, and long fights run it
  dry. Remnants, Zenith II's efficiency, Efficiency II, the low Thunderstorm and the 3:1 rhythm are the answers.

## 8. What players pick

The page says little about common practice. Every place it does:

- The Expensive tab is "where I assume most people will end up" among players enjoying the league.
- The build is one of the best options for comfort without top-end gear (overview).
- Heroic Tragedy isn't worth it below Mirror Tier (changelog, Jun 1).
- Zenith II belongs on every endgame setup; the author says it should have been there from the start (Jun 11).
- The author tunes with PoB: Sudden Escalation went in because PoB showed more damage (Jun 6).

**Against [oracle].** Two creators reached the same core independently: the two plant spells, Forced Outcome,
Thunderstorm in Cast on Critical with Efficiency II, the supports in §9's second kit line, and Sigil of Power on
set 2. They part on the resource. Amonera moves costs to life
(Atalui's Bloodletting, Lifetap) and defends with mana (Mind over Matter, Eldritch Battery, Harmony Within)
([oracle] chain 2, 9). Raxx keeps costs on mana because his payoffs read it: Zenith II above 90% mana, and the
sigil's stages, which grow only from mana spent (pin). A life-cost Entangle would leave the sigil without stages:
this study's inference, untested. Amonera also triggers Bone Cage and uses Prism of Belief for Entangle levels
([oracle] chain 5, 8); Raxx does neither.

**Candidate pattern shapes:**

- **Overgrowth, a state on your own plants** → State-keyed payoffs (existing), as a variant: the always-on state
  sits on the build's objects, and each plant skill has lines keyed to it. Its supply chain (crit → Cast on
  Critical → Thunderstorm → Overgrown → explosions) → Inputs and outputs (existing, [oracle] cited). *Checked*.
- **Forced Outcome** → Forced condition (existing). *Checked*.
- **The staff's Sigil of Power on set 2** → Weapon set 2 job and Skill granted by an item base (existing).
  *Checked*.
- **Sigil stages from mana spent, primed by spam** → Cost to a gated resource (existing). A caution for Cost
  redirection: a payoff reading the resource spent dies when costs move pools. *Checked* (sigil text); the clash
  *unverified*.
- **Thunderstorm cut low** → Low-level utility gems. **The vines' slow, the blind, Temporal Chains** → Control as
  defence (both existing). *Checked* gem text; the level threshold *unverified*.
- **New: a single-area skill placed once.** A sustained skill with a limit of one and a fixed duration deals damage
  at a rate set by its cast time, so it is cast once per window while a spammable skill fills the gaps, and cast
  speed scales its rate, not its uses. Search: gem lookups for "Limit 1" beside a "% of this Skill's Cast Time"
  line. *Checked* for Thrashing Vines; the cast-speed reading is the study's.
- **New, unverified: an anoint moved to another slot** (Raven-Touched Shard puts Cooked on the helmet) so the
  amulet takes a jewel-socket anoint; near Radius jewel instead of an anoint.
