## 2. What the build is

- **Damage:** physical bear-form slams on a Talisman, plus endgame cold for Herald of Ice. Ascendancy: Shaman.
- **Packs:** Rampage, a channelled bear charge that slams as it runs (checked: Shapeshift, Bear, Channel, Travel,
  Slam, ConsumesRage; Talisman only), with Walking Calamity up and Herald of Ice exploding what shatters. The guide
  claims 120%+ movement speed in Rampage.
- **Bosses:** Walking Calamity, two Shockwave Totems, then run over them with Rampage (Late) or alternate Maul and
  Furious Slam (Act 4 to Mid).
- **How it plays:** a Rage economy. Maul builds it (checked: 5 Rage on hit); Furious Slam spends 10 for an enraged slam
  with a 3 m shockwave instead of 2 m and 4 s of Jagged Ground (checked); Rampage spends Rage to prolong the channel,
  ignores the cost for its first 2.5 s and gains no Rage while running (checked). Map loop: let Rage refill, freeze one
  enemy with Lunar Assault for the Bhatair's Vengeance buff, Walking Calamity, Rampage; then Slam and Maul until Rage
  is back.
- **Stance:** Maul before each Slam, because Maul stands the bear up and the next slam comes much faster. The penalty
  is in the gem text (checked: +0.4 s on Furious Slam, +0.5 s on Rampage "if not standing"); Maul's standing effect
  is the guide's claim.
- **Cons, by the guide:** Rage management and animations.

## 3. The kit

**Weapon.** A Talisman, the two-hander every bear and wolf skill needs (checked for Rampage and Pounce): Changeling
while leveling, Fury at Early Endgame, Maji from Mid. Fury, Maji, Rabid and Roaring Talismans carry +(7–10) Maximum
Rage (checked); the guide asks for that implicit.

**Off hand.** From Late Endgame, a sceptre through Lord of the Wilds (checked keystone: a non-unique sceptre beside a
talisman, at 50% less Spirit and 50% less reservation efficiency for non-minion skills). The guide wants 140+ Spirit
for Charge Regulation and Herald of Ice, two or three sockets, and a Shrine Sceptre, whose bases grant a Purity of
Fire, Ice or Lightning (checked). It says only a Purity base works, without saying why.

**Main skills (Late Endgame):**
- Rampage: Momentum, Efficiency II, Burgeon II, Aftershock II, Stomping Ground.
- Maul: Rapid Attacks II, Rage III, Heft, Cold Attunement, Life Leech III.
- Furious Slam: Fist of War II, Heft, Aftershock II, Close Combat II, Heavy Swing.
- Shockwave Totem: Overabundance II, Urgent Totems III, Rapid Attacks II, Uruk's Smelting, Armour Break III.
- Walking Calamity: Elemental Armament II, Prolonged Duration II, Elemental Focus, Concentrated Area, Xoph's Pyre.
- Lunar Assault, the freeze carrier: Bhatair's Vengeance, Freeze, Prolonged Duration II, Rapid Attacks II, Elemental
  Armament II.

**Utility:** Pounce (Holy Descent, Rapid Attacks III); Ferocious Roar (Raging Cry, Tireless); Herald of Ice (Magnified
Area II, Heft); the sceptre's Purity of Fire with Clarity II, Cannibalism II, Vitality II, Herbalism II; Charge
Regulation bare. 125 Str, 35 Dex, 40 Int. Min Max puts Rigwald's Ferocity and Uul-Netol's Embrace on Rampage and
Dialla's Desire on Walking Calamity.

**Weapon sets.** Lock the main weapon to both sets; Walking Calamity and Lunar Assault go on set 2, all other actives
except auras on set 1, and set 2's passive points go to duration, so Walking Calamity lasts about 30 s and the
Bhatair's buff about 60 s. Min Max swaps the roles (everything on set 2, the two buffs on set 1) so Rampage gets
Rigwald's Ferocity's set-2 side (checked: Set II 30% more damage, 10% reduced attack speed; Set I 30% increased attack
speed, 15% less damage).

**Uniques, by stage:** Ngamahu's Chosen, a charm for instant maximum Rage (campaign); Polcirkeln (the Act 1-3 text
reserves a ring for it); Defiance of Destiny (Mid, Late); Headhunter (Late on); Heart of the Well with damage as extra
cold, and From Nothing (Late on); Morior Invictus, Megalomaniac and Prism of Belief (+2 or +3 to Rampage) on Min Max.

**Ascendancy order:** Druidic Champion on the way (checked: 1% more spell damage per 2 Rage, which feeds the leveling
Volcano); Furious Wellspring, the Rage engine and the stated reason for Shaman over Oracle (checked: no inherent Rage
loss, 6% of maximum Rage regenerated per second, mana regeneration modifiers apply to Rage, +7 maximum Rage, +5 to
skills' Rage cost); Reactive Growth (checked: 10% less elemental damage taken, plus adaptation); Turning of the Seasons
(checked: Exposure in Presence, 10% as extra damage of a random element). Sacred Flow (+40 Spirit per empty charm slot,
checked) is the Spirit fallback; the guide would rather keep the charms.

**Tree direction:** damage, life regeneration and some area near the Druid start; Primal Spirit (skill speed), then
Harbinger of Disaster; Radial Force if area is short; Personal Touch and Bestial Rage early; the three Claw notables
(Sharpened, Swift, Piercing Claw, the last 15% elemental penetration while shapeshifted, checked); Singular Purpose;
Crushing Verdict, singled out for Slam and Rampage; Reverberating Impact and Brutal. Endgame: a From Nothing jewel
jumping to the Blood Magic area for Projectile Bulwark, Prism Guard, Vengeful Fury and In Your Face; without it, drop
the Adrenaline Rush path and socket for Projectile Bulwark and Vengeful Fury.

## 4. The levers

In the order of the weapon paragraph each endgame tab repeats:
1. **Weapon physical damage.** The leveling tabs say the weapon is most of the damage: replace it often.
2. **"Gain extra damage as Physical"** on the talisman, called huge and needed for the explosions.
3. **Levels:** +attack levels on the weapon, +melee levels on gloves ("mandatory") and amulet (+3 on Min Max).
4. **Attack speed,** mostly comfort.
5. **A Rage implicit.**

**Explosions (Late, Min Max):** Bhatair's Vengeance from a Lunar Assault freeze (checked: 1% of damage as cold per 2
Rage you have, for you and allies in Presence, 15 s base; the guide says maximum Rage, the same at full); Polcirkeln
(checked: enemies your hits chill can be shattered as though frozen); flat cold on rings and gloves; Heart of the Well's
extra cold; extra physical on the talisman. After 0.5 cut Bhatair's, it takes more cold and extra physical; until
then, Herald of Ash.

**Speed:** movement speed is a damage-and-clear lever, not only travel: Rampage's run speed reads movement speed
modifiers instead of attack speed (checked). 35% boots, movement speed on jewels.

**Rage regeneration:** mana regeneration on amulet and rings, through Furious Wellspring (checked).

**Jewels (Early, Mid):** life on kill, attack damage or damage while shapeshifted, skill speed while shapeshifted.

**Skippable:** Blood Magic (a ring's physical damage leeched as mana fixes mana; Rampage alone needs no fix); Prism of
Belief and Uul-Netol's, bought last; Defiance of Destiny on Min Max, at 3,100 life, unless survival suffers.

## 5. Leveling

**Act 1-3.** Changeling Talisman and Rope Cuffs. Maul (Rapid Attacks I, Rage I), Furious Slam (Fist of War I, Fire
Attunement), Volcano (Overabundance I, Considered Casting), Shockwave Totem (Overabundance I, Urgent Totems I), Herald
of Ash, Pounce (Rapid Attacks I, Mark for Death), War Banner; gems arrive in that order.
- **Bosses:** Pounce in, two Volcanoes, spam Furious Slam to set them off; drop Volcano when Shockwave Totem arrives.
- **Packs:** Pounce, Maul, then Furious Slam at 10 Rage.
- **Moving:** Pounce, a werewolf leap on a cooldown (checked), as often as possible until Rampage.
- **Spirit:** War Banner or Scavenged Plating; the latter needs Mark for Death on Pounce (Pounce triggers a socketed
  Mark, checked).
- **Trap:** Fire Attunement with cold or lightning on the talisman; on a cold talisman take Cold Attunement.

**Act 4 - Inter.** Ancient Mitts, Azure Amulet. Rampage joins (a tier-11 gem, checked) with Momentum, Efficiency II,
Stomping Ground, Aftershock II; Walking Calamity and Ferocious Roar (Infernal Cry, Tireless, Raging Cry) join; Maul
moves to Cold Attunement and Rage III. Charge Regulation replaces War Banner from level 50. With Furious Wellspring,
Rage sits near full, so Walking Calamity and Rampage carry travel. Tree: Singular Purpose, Crushing Verdict,
Reverberating Impact, Brutal.

**Swap points:** Volcano → Shockwave Totem; Pounce → Rampage for travel; War Banner → Charge Regulation at 50; Herald
of Ash → Herald of Ice at Mid.

**Rough edges:** the text asks for Rage II on Maul, the panels show Rage I then III; the Act 1-3 text puts Fire
Attunement on Maul, its panel on Furious Slam.

**Campaign rewards:** stun threshold, then resistances wherever offered.

## 6. Gear and budget

Numbered equipment priority per tab:
- **Early Endgame:** Stone Cuirass, helmet, Long Belt, boots, Gold Amulet, Fury Talisman, gloves, ring. Herald of Ash;
  no sceptre.
- **Mid:** Paragon Greathelm, Maji Talisman, Ruby Ring, Defiance of Destiny, Warlord Cuirass, War Wraps, Long Belt,
  Carved Greaves, Polcirkeln. Herald of Ice arrives.
- **Late** ("already EXPENSIVE"): Imperial Greathelm, Shrine Sceptre, Defiance of Destiny, Warlord Cuirass, Headhunter,
  Faithful Leggings, Prismatic Ring, War Wraps, Maji Talisman, Polcirkeln.
- **Min Max:** Paragon Greathelm, Maji Talisman, Prismatic Ring, Breach Ring, Gold Amulet, Morior Invictus, Massive
  Mitts, Headhunter, Faithful Leggings, Shrine Sceptre. Polcirkeln leaves the list, though the notes still rely on
  cold explosions.
- **Min Max buying order:** Headhunter first (for juiced-map farming); a strong weapon and Morior Invictus; armour and
  life elsewhere; an amulet with +melee and rarity; the rest; Prism of Belief and Uul-Netol's last.

**Stat priorities** beyond §4: body armour and T1–T2 life; boots resistances and life; an amulet anointed with Bestial
Rage; mana leech on one ring ("mandatory"), chaos resistance on rings. Budget is in words only; no currency figures.

## 7. Defences

- Armour with life, and % of armour applied to elemental damage; Defiance of Destiny, the main survival buy; Reactive
  Growth.
- Capped chaos resistance and a life flask with 30% instant recovery against damage over time.
- Life on kill from jewels (Early, Mid); Life Leech III on Maul.
- The only number is 3,100+ life on Min Max; no resistance, armour or max-hit figures.

## 8. What players pick

The page almost never says what others do. Its comparative lines: fast "compared to other melee options"; spared
0.5's energy shield nerfs by not using energy shield, implying other Druids did; Shaman over Oracle for Furious
Wellspring; Defiance of Destiny "a very solid option"; Blood Magic unnecessary. No line says "most players" or
"standard": the kit below is one popular creator's, not a survey.

   prolong the channel and gains none while running; Shaman's Furious Wellspring regenerates 6% of maximum Rage a

4. **Levers:** weapon physical damage and extra physical, +melee levels on gloves and amulet, then movement speed,

6. **Clear:** Herald of Ice on shatter, Polcirkeln making chilled enemies shatterable, and Bhatair's Vengeance on Lunar

**Candidate pattern shapes:**
- **Condition carrier** (existing): Lunar Assault carries Bhatair's Vengeance, as in [walking-calamity]: a second
  independent build, a candidate for "seen in two or more".
- **Weapon set 2 job** (existing): a new example where the job is the set's own passives (duration), one weapon in
  both sets; Rigwald's Ferocity then picks which set is "main" (checked lines).
- **Pivot and broadcast bridges** (existing): Furious Wellspring makes mana regeneration count for Rage (checked).
- **A rule changer** (existing): Lord of the Wilds (off hand) and Polcirkeln (chill counts as freeze for shatter);
  both checked.
- **Travel by action** (existing), with a caution: Rampage is a travel skill that reads movement speed, so here
  movement speed is the buy (checked).
- **Placed detonator** (existing): the leveling Volcano set off by Furious Slam (*unverified*; Volcano not looked up).
- **Stance primer** (new): a cheap builder leaves you in a stance that removes the finisher's time penalty, so the
  builder is also a speed buff. Search: "if not standing", "when Shapeshifting" in gem text (checked lines).

**What PoB might not model** (coverage at the pin):
- Herald of Ice's "Enemies you Shatter explode" is mapped but read nowhere, so a compile may miss the explosions.
- Bhatair's Vengeance's buff is "possibly unmodelled"; a custom modifier (1% extra cold per 2 Rage) would stand in.
- Rampage's movement-speed run, free 2.5 s and no-Rage rule, and every stance or shapeshift time penalty are
  "possibly unmodelled": the tempo is a hand calculation. Furious Slam's 10-Rage spend is unmapped; its enraged stat
  set parses.
- Furious Wellspring, Lord of the Wilds and Turning of the Seasons parse fully.
- Not established: Polcirkeln's chill-shatter line, Rigwald's set-dependent lines, weapon-set passive durations.
