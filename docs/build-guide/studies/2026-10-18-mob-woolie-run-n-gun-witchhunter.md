## 2. What the build is

A lightning crossbow Witchhunter that fires while moving. All three of its ammunition skills (Galvanic Shards,
Stormblast Bolts, Explosive Shot) carry the Usable While Moving type (pin), and the guide sells it on attack and
movement speed and a reduced movement penalty while attacking (the source of that reduction isn't named).

- **Packs:** Galvanic Shards fires 8 fragments per shot whose hits release chaining lightning beams (stat set 2,
  272% of base at gem level 20, all physical converted to lightning, pin); Herald of Thunder finishes the
  rest. Clearing is one button.
- **Bosses:** Stormblast Bolts loads a 5-bolt clip; each shot rains 3 charged bolts around the target that sit
  for 10 seconds (up to 30 active) and explode only when a Detonator skill hits them (pin). The explosion is
  the damage: 470% of base, against 72% for the bolt's impact (pin). The detonator is Explosive Shot, socketed
  in Mirage Archer, so a dodge roll spawns a mirage that fires it (pin: Mirage Archer's text; mirages deal 30%
  less damage and last 6 seconds).
- **Rotation (bosses):** fire four or five Stormblast shots while the boss can't yet be hit, wait, dodge roll
  to detonate, then keep firing standing close (for Close Combat II), with Emergency Reload when the clip runs
  dry. The guide says a wait of about 3 seconds gets past a boss's one-shot protection (unverified).
- **Ascendancy:** Witchhunter, for Sorcery Ward (§7). Its stated weaknesses: reloads until the lineage
  supports, mobile bosses, and squishiness at low evasion.

## 3. The kit

- **Weapon:** one crossbow, locked to both weapon sets, so no second weapon is needed; no off hand. Rampart
  Raptor (3–4 sockets, Glacial Runes) while leveling, a rare from Act 3, Desolate Crossbow in the endgame.
- **The weapon-set split:** Galvanic Shards, Herald of Thunder and Wind Dancer on set I only; Stormblast Bolts
  and Mirage Archer on set II only; Eternal Rage, and later Berserk, on both. The stated purpose is a second
  Crossbow Shot button that fires Stormblast while the first fires Galvanic Shards. Each set has its own 20
  weapon-set passive points (the tree counters).
- **Clear skill:** Galvanic Shards with Elemental Armament II, Innervate (Mobility at HYPER) and reload help:
  Double Barrel II, Fork and Projectile Acceleration III while leveling; from the 1-divine tab, Ratha's Assault
  (extra bolts and attack speed, but bolts load only when you dodge, pin) and Arjun's Medal (25% chance on a
  kill to load a bolt into every crossbow skill, pin), with Fork, replaced by Dominus' Grasp from Mid.
- **Boss skill:** Stormblast Bolts with Elemental Armament II, Concentrated Area, Close Combat II, Rapid
  Attacks II and Lightning Attunement; at Late, Rigwald's Ferocity replaces Lightning Attunement; at HYPER,
  Rakiata's Flow replaces Rapid Attacks II.
- **Mirage Archer:** Explosive Shot socketed, with Rapid Attacks II and Cooldown Recovery II, plus Prolonged
  Duration II from the 1-divine tab and Magnified Area II at HYPER.
- **Other skills:** Herald of Thunder (Elemental Armament II, Longshot II, Elemental Focus, Blind II); Wind
  Dancer (Blind II, Maim, Life Leech III, Magnified Area II); Emergency Reload (Lifetap, Cooldown Recovery II,
  Efficiency II); Sorcery Ward (Cold Mastery); Eternal Rage (Vitality I, Precision I); Berserk (Vitality II)
  from Late. Mortar Cannon with Explosive Grenade backs up Mirage Archer against rares and fills support slots
  for a passive the guide calls Gem Enthusiast, where blue supports don't count (not looked up).
- **Uniques:** Hyrri's Ire by about 3 divines, Alpha's Howl by about 10, Heart of the Well when affordable
  (§6).
- **Ascendancy order:** Zealous Inquisition (10% chance for kills to explode for their maximum life, pin) >
  Obsessive Rituals (grants Sorcery Ward at 50% less armour and evasion, pin) > Ceremonial Ablution > Pitiless
  Killer (Culling Strike). From Late, Zealous Inquisition is respecced into Judge, Jury, and Executioner
  (Decimating Strike). Weapon Master (100 passive points become weapon-set points, pin) is never taken; the
  guide doesn't say why.
- **Tree direction:** the tree isn't text. From Mid it starts through Hard to Kill instead of Ricochet; the
  anoint is Unbound Forces, or the dearer Acceleration. The counter shows 35, 15, 0, −7 and −16 of 123 points
  left across the tabs, so the last two trees seem to overspend.

## 4. The levers

In the guide's priority order:

1. **The crossbow.** Physical and lightning DPS plus "+level" on skills, either Attack or Projectile levels
   (Stormblast and Galvanic both carry the Projectile tag, pin). The first purchase after reaching maps.
2. **Flat damage** on rings and gloves (physical or lightning); at Late it calls more of it on rings a big gain.
3. **Heart of the Well's** gain-as-extra-lightning line, then its lucky-lightning line, which gets expensive.
4. **Rage:** Alpha's Howl's Spirit pays for Berserk on top of Eternal Rage.
5. **Lineage supports:** Rigwald's Ferocity first, then Rakiata's Flow, in Stormblast.
6. **HYPER:** Prism of Belief at +2 Stormblast (or Galvanic, for juiced maps); From Nothing for Lightning
   Quick; the Delirium instill Stimulants on offence.
- **Jewels:** lightning, crossbow, penetration and speed rolls, plus one roll of life on kill.
- **What it skips:** Against the Darkness (optional); Zealous Inquisition once damage is enough.

## 5. Leveling

- **Acts 1 to early 3:** Permafrost Bolts freezes and Fragmentation Rounds pops the freeze for single target.
  First Spirit gem (Act 1, Freythorn): Herald of Ice.
- **Tier 5 gem:** Galvanic Shards with Herald of Thunder becomes the clear; Herald of Ice goes; the freeze pair
  stays for single target.
- **Tier 8 Spirit gem:** take Mirage Archer but leave it off until Stormblast Bolts (tier 9, pin), which the
  auras note places in Act 3 after the Azak Bog. Then Mirage Archer goes on with Explosive Shot, and the
  freeze pair goes.
- **Fresh-start links:** lower tiers of the listed supports (Rapid Attacks I, Elemental Armament I, Cold
  Attunement); Fork only with an early Lesser Jeweller's Orb.
- **Gear:** leveling uniques (Rampart Raptor, two Blackhearts for flat damage, Skin of the Loyal, Rondel of
  Fragility and others) that a fresh league start ignores; a rare crossbow with lightning mods at Act 3; life
  and resistances elsewhere. Emergency Reload, lineage supports and Rage arrive only on the endgame tabs.

## 7. Defences

- **Sorcery Ward:** a barrier that takes elemental damage from hits, sized at 30% of armour plus evasion (up
  to 32,000) and recharging 6.1 seconds after it stops taking damage (pin, gem level 20). Ceremonial Ablution
  lets it take physical and chaos hits too (pin). The guide's shorthand: "30% of Evasion = HP".
- **Its cost:** Obsessive Rituals' 50% less armour and evasion (pin). The guide's 0.5 notes give this as a nerf
  from 35% and estimate about 15% less evasion and ward overall, partly offset by a 15% buff to evasion bases
  and a better deflection formula (unverified).
- **Evasion stacking:** Hyrri's Ire, Leather Bound Gauntlets (+1 evasion per point of armour on the gloves,
  pin) with pure-armour gloves, quest rewards for global defences, the Hard to Kill path, and the Beastial
  Skin instill. At the pin Hyrri's Ire rolls 100–150% increased evasion, doubles evasion while not hit
  recently, and gives 10–20% of damage as extra cold (pin). The guide's 0.5 note gives its evasion as 200%
  after the nerf, which the pin's range doesn't match.
- **Recovery and control:** Life Leech III on Wind Dancer, one jewel roll of life on kill, life regeneration
  and Vitality II (called mandatory) against Berserk; Blind II on Wind Dancer and Herald of Thunder, Maim.
- **Numbers:** none on resistances, life or maximum hit, and no evasion threshold for "squishy".

## 8. What players pick

The guide never says what most players do or what is standard; its popularity signal is the Verified tab and
4.7K favourites. What it marks as best or required:

- Desolate Crossbow is the best base.
- Heart of the Well's gain-as-extra-lightning line is its best mod.
- Rigwald's Ferocity is the first expensive lineage support.
- Vitality II is mandatory with Berserk.
- Mirage Archer must wait for Stormblast Bolts.
- Against the Darkness is "NOT NECESSARY".
- Herald of Thunder does the rest of the clear after Galvanic Shards.

## 9. For the library
