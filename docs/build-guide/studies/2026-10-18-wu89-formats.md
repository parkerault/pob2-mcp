# WU-89's formats, for WU-91 (M-20)

The run-folder formats that WU-89's scripts (`run-log.mjs`, `check-run.mjs`, `render-audit.mjs`) parse, check and
display for amendment 8 (`../spec/amendment-8-engine-lens.md`, "the amendment"). WU-91 writes the skill text to match
these exactly. The amendment is the rule; this file is the shape. Everything in WU-81's formats
(`2026-10-18-wu81-formats.md`) still holds, except where a section below changes it.

The fixture in these formats is `tests/fixtures/pipeline/run-rival`; its tests are
`tests/unit/buildGuideEngineLens.test.ts` (AC-W73).

## Card ids

- **The rival's card** (the card a researcher with fresh context writes to find a faster structure, D-408) is
  `r<k>-x-<n>`: `k` the number of brainstorm rounds run before its step, `n` counting the run's rival cards in
  round `k` from 1. The first rival of a run is almost always `r1-x-1`. Its file is `cards/r<k>-x-<n>.md`.
- `x` joins `a`–`c` (researchers) and `g` (made at a gate) as card letters everywhere a card id goes: answers,
  `concepts/<id>/`, `probes/<id>/`, `reports/gates/R1b-<id>-a<n>.json`, dossier rows.

## The card

- **The obvious card's kit** (the build an average player makes first, D-403): a Pitch item, after the five pitch
  lines and before `Playstyle:`:
  `- Obvious build: <the kit it follows>`
  - Only `r1-a-1` carries it, and only when the brief names a skill or a playstyle. Any other card with it, an empty
    value, or a brief with neither, fails the checker (rule `card`).
  - The brainstorm display prints it after the five pitch lines, before `Unusual:`: `   Obvious build: <kit>`.
- **The structure check** (R1b's paper check of the card's levers, flipped assumptions, counts and premium slots,
  D-406): an H3 in the verification half, under `## Verification half`:

  ```
  ### Structure check

  - <one line per step: the levers, each flipped assumption, the counts, the premium slots>
  - A stronger structure? <the flip, in a sentence, and the rough multiplier>
  ```

  - The items are top-level `- ` lines; indented lines join the item above.
  - `- A stronger structure? …` is optional, and when present it is the section's last item (else rule `card`).
  - The heading must sit under `## Verification half` (else rule `card`). Its place among that half's H3s is free;
    `run-rival` puts it first, before `### Verified`.
  - The verification display prints the line under the card, after "Found in verification":
    `   A stronger structure? <text>`.
- The checker doesn't require either one (a run recorded before amendment 8 has neither).

## The deck, `cards.md`

- **The engine part** (what the build's damage consumes and what supplies it, D-405): one part on a deck line,
  `engine: <consumed family> from <supply family>`, each one word of `data/rules/vocabulary.md` (`none` included),
  lower case. Example: `- r1-a-1 "Blood Hunt bleed burst" | <adds> | engine: bleed from on-hit`.
  - Two cards share an engine when both words match. An engine whose consumed family is `none` is no engine: it
    counts toward nothing.
  - Every dealt researcher's line (under `Round <k>`, ids `r<k>-[a-c]-<n>`) carries one once the deck names any
    engine, or once the run has an `RV` step. Gate-made lines and not-dealt lines may carry one; neither counts.
  - Malformed parts, two engine parts on a line, and words outside the vocabulary fail (rule `deck`).
- **No more than half a round on one engine** (D-405): in each round, an engine's count among the round's dealt
  researcher cards is at most `floor(dealt / 2)`: 4 of 8 or 9, 3 of 6 or 7. Over it, the round passes only when:
  - its R1a-assemble entry's `shortReason` contains `short: <n> cards share one engine`, `<n>` that engine's count
    (it may hold another reason beside it), or
  - the user's note for the round names the engine's consumed family (its hyphen may be a space: "chilled ground").
    The note for round `k ≥ 2` is the `note` of the `(k−1)`-th `more` answer at the brainstorm or verification
    gate; round 1 has none.
  - The checker doesn't check that the header says the note exempted an engine; WU-91's text should say it in the
    `How they differ` line.
- **The header names each engine and its count:** `How they differ` (still one line) contains
  `<consumed> from <supply> (<n>)` for every engine of the deck's dealt researcher cards, all rounds counted.
  Example: `Six engines: bleed from on-hit (4), bleed from on-crit (1), …, rage from on-hit (1); <what differs>.`
- **Waits on the pin** (a card resting on an entry the PoB pin doesn't know, D-400): the part
  `waits on the pin: <entry>`, `<entry>` the digest entry's name, on a line that also carries
  `can't be finalized yet: <why>` (else rule `deck`). Example:
  `… | can't be finalized yet: its spear isn't in the PoB pin | waits on the pin: Crimson Test Spear`.
  The brainstorm display prints, after the card's "Can't be finalized yet: <why>." line:
  `   Waits on PoB: <entry> isn't in the PoB version this run uses, so the build can't be measured yet.`
- **The rival's card is no deck card:** no `r<k>-x-<n>` line anywhere in `cards.md` (rule `deck`); its row is in the
  dossier.

## The run log

- **Stage `RV`** (the rival, D-408), no `concept`:
  - `{"stage": "RV", "attempt": 1, "outcome": "done"}` when it wrote its card;
  - `{"stage": "RV", "attempt": 1, "outcome": "none-found", "tried": "<what it tried, in a sentence>"}` when it
    found no faster structure. `tried` is required there, and refused anywhere else.
  - `missing` as for every step: one uncounted re-run.
  - Field order in the entry form: `stage, concept, attempt, outcome, priceOutcomes, probes, shortReason, tried`.
- **Where it runs** (the checker's order rules):
  - **The run's own rival** is the first RV to return its file right after the R1b-assemble that closes the first
    verification round, before that gate's display. After `done`: R1b for its card, then a second R1b-assemble
    pass, then the verification gate. After `none-found`: the verification gate.

    ```
    R1b <pick 1> … R1b <pick 3>, R1b-assemble, RV done, R1b r1-x-1 (retries as usual), R1b-assemble, [the gate]
    R1b <pick 1> … R1b <pick 3>, R1b-assemble, RV none-found, [the gate]
    ```

  - **A rival the user asks for at a gate** runs between that visit's display and its answer, like a gate question
    step, and leads nowhere: the order rules read past it. Its card is verified when a later answer picks it
    (`verify:r1-x-2`, or `pick:` at the brainstorm gate), as any card.
  - The checker doesn't require the rival (a run recorded before amendment 8 has none). Every `done` RV has its
    card file, and every `r<k>-x-<n>` file has its `done` RV (rule `rival`).
- **The verification round's cap** (D-287): at most 3 cards compiled per verification round, the rival's R1b aside.
  The cap message keeps its words: `verification round <n> compiles <m> cards; at most 3 per verification round
  (D-287, NFR-23), the rival's aside (D-408)`.

## R1b's verdicts

Two new terminal codes. Like `not-a-skill` and `wrong-ascendancy`, each ends the card's verification with no compile
and no retry: the verdict has `verdict.compiled: "fail"`, `compiles: 0`, `recordPath: null`, the run log's entry
`outcome: "fail"` with `probes: 0`, and no skeleton folder.

| Code | When | Finding | The card |
|---|---|---|---|
| `not-in-pin` | the card's damage source or a load-bearing link is a digest entry the PoB pin doesn't know (D-400) | `{"part": "compiled", "code": "not-in-pin", "message": "not compiled: <entry> isn't in the PoB pin"}` | can't be finalized yet |
| `not-dealt` | the rival's card fails R1a-assemble's identity or sniff check, which R1b runs on it first (D-408) | `{"part": "compiled", "code": "not-dealt", "message": "not dealt: <the check that failed>"}` | failed |

- `not-dealt` is only the rival's: on any other card it fails rule `gate-report`.
- A retry after either fails rule `order`, and so does the verification gate's `pick:` of the card. A pre-supplied
  pick of such a card stops the run, as for the other two codes.

## The dossier, `dossier.md`

- **Its columns** (D-407 adds each card's rough clear speed after its EHP), exported by the checker as
  `DOSSIER_COLUMNS_D407`:
  `| Card | Round | Status | Synergy | Named source DPS | Life | EHP | Clear speed | Key prices | League | Main risk |`
  - A run with an `RV` step must use these. A run without one may keep D-355's ten columns (`DOSSIER_COLUMNS`), so
    recorded runs still check.
  - **The Clear speed cell:** the skeleton record's `measures.clearSpeed.speed` with `(rough)`, or
    `<speed> (PoB gives no area)` when `measures.clearSpeed.noArea` is true and it names a skill; `—` on a failed or
    not-yet-finalizable card, like its numbers, prices and league.
- **The rival's row** comes from R1b-assemble's second pass, below the round's other rows; nothing in the row marks
  it beyond its id.
- **Statuses:**
  - a `not-in-pin` card: `can't be finalized yet: waits on the pin`;
  - the rival's `not-dealt` card: `failed after 1 attempt: not dealt: <the check it failed>; reports/gates/R1b-r1-x-1-a1.json`.
- **The template.** WU-89 left `templates/dossier-template.md` at ten columns: its example rows and
  `tests/static/buildGuidePipeline.test.ts` (the template's row length and its `DOSSIER_COLUMNS`) pin ten, and
  R1b-assemble.md says "its ten columns exactly". WU-91 moves the header and the rows to the eleven, R1b-assemble.md's
  sentence to "eleven columns", and the static test's list to `DOSSIER_COLUMNS_D407` (its regex reads
  `export const DOSSIER_COLUMNS = [...]`; point it at `DOSSIER_COLUMNS_D407`).

## The displays

Movement speed is a multiplier (PoB's `EffectiveMovementSpeedMod`; the record's `measures.movementSpeed` since
WU-88), printed as the bonus over base in whole percent: `1.251` is `+25% over your base speed`, `0.97` is
`-3% over your base speed`; `not measured` without one.

- **The verification gate**, each card's block, in order:

  ```
  <k>. <Name>[, the rival]: <status in words>.
     The rival: a researcher with fresh context wrote it to clear faster than the other cards.   (the rival only)
     Why pick it: …
     How it clears: … (the card's estimate)                (first, only when PoB gives the clear skill no area)
     Clear speed: <speed> (a rough estimate from <skill>'s area).
                | <speed> (PoB gives no area).
                | <speed> (a rough estimate; PoB measured no clear skill).
     Movement speed: +<n>% over your base speed.
     How it clears: …                                       (here otherwise; "(an estimate; measured at the design)"
                                                             when the skeleton record has no measures)
     Bosses: …                                              (the card's Bosses line: the boss floor here)
     Damage: …
     Toughness: …
     Key prices: …
     The catch: …
     Found in verification: …
     A stronger structure? …
     Made at the gate (visit <n>): … / Changed at the gate (visit <n>): …
  ```

  The Clear speed and Movement speed lines appear when the skeleton record has `measures.clearSpeed` (WU-88's
  records). The no-area wording uses the record's `speed`, never its `band`. A clear speed with no clear skill
  (`skill` null) gets no no-area wording. After the cards, when the run's last RV found nothing:
  `The rival, a researcher with fresh context, found no structure that clears faster. What it tried: <tried>`.
- **The design gate**, each rung's block, in order: `- Clear speed: <the D1 gate's word> (rough).` (or
  `<the record's speed> (PoB gives no area).`), `- Movement speed: …` (from the D1 gate's record, its `recordPath`),
  `- The Arbiter of Divinity: …` (on `b` and `n`), `- Damage: …`, `- Effective HP: …`, then the anoint, gear, gems
  and tree. Its opening sentence says what the figures mean in that order: "Clear speed is a rough estimate from the
  main skill's area; movement speed is PoB's, over your base speed; damage is …; effective HP is …".
- **The brainstorm gate** adds the obvious card's line and the waits-on-the-pin line (above).
- The final display is unchanged.

## `data` citations to Markdown data files

- **The forms:** a research file's footnote `[^<id>]: data | <path under data/>#<id>`, and a plan claim's source
  `data:<path under data/>#<id>`. Those two are the places the checker resolves citations; it reads no card's Links
  table. A Links table cell can't hold an unescaped `|`, so a card that cites one there writes `data \| <path>#<id>`.
- **Resolution** (rule `markers`):
  - a `.md` path names an entry: an H3 `### <Name>` whose next line is `- Id: <id>`, the id matching
    `^[a-z0-9]+(-[a-z0-9]+)*$` and unique in its file (D-400's digest, D-401's `uniques.md` and `gems.md`, D-402's
    monster table);
  - in `rules/nodes.md`, whose entries are lines, an all-digit id names the line whose parenthesis starts with that
    node id, `- <Name> (<node id>, <ascendancy or keystone>) …` (`rules/nodes.md#39595` is Way of the Stonefist's);
  - any other path is a JSON `facts` file, as before.
  - The failures name the file and say what's missing: no file, no entry, a duplicated id, or no node line.

## render-audit.mjs

Each rung's Checks clear line reads movement speed from the record's `measures.movementSpeed`, else
`outputs.EffectiveMovementSpeedMod` for a record from before WU-88. The line's form is unchanged:
`- Clear speed: <speed>, a rough estimate from <skill>'s area. Movement speed: +<n>%.`
