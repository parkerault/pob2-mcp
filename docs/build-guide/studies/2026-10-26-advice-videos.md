## What each gave, and what didn't hold

**`vid-scale-defence` (video Ae7Txcr2tn4, defence).**
- Taken: defence sorted by where a hit meets it (avoidance, mitigation, a pool, then sustain), in "Defence layers in
  hit order". A slot's defence granted from another slot's (Subterfuge Mask, Iron Slippers) is in "Derived defence
  layer". Minions as a buffer is in "An ally takes the hit first".
- Didn't hold: the rule that three of the four layers make a character sturdy, since a measured build has three and is
  one-shot. The evasion section rests on a Path of Exile 1 table. Subterfuge Mask was +2 then and is +1 at the pin.

**`vid-defence-guide` (video qsnj3QJBxs0, defence).**
- Taken: one hit traced through the checks in the order PoB uses. Chaos drains energy shield at double rate, which is
  why Chaos Inoculation (immune to chaos and bleeding at the pin) suits energy shield builds.
- Didn't hold: its evasion table is Path of Exile 1's, and it reads the table's evade chances as chances to be hit.
  Its counter that spaces out evaded hits isn't in PoB, which averages.

**`vid-secret-defence` (video dthtaESho5A, defence).**
- Taken: instant-recovery flasks, damage taken from mana before life, reading the max hit rather than EHP for
  one-shots, physical hits taken as an element (Cloak of Flame, Lightning Coil), stun and ailment thresholds, slows,
  and Blasphemy's lower curse magnitude. These are in "Defence the gate can't see", "Mana as a hit pool" and "Shift
  physical hits into a resisted type".

**`vid-deflection` (video azI3zGx5C-c, defence).**
- Taken: 0.5 made high deflection reachable on an evasion build. The video grows deflection from evasion and adds
  Blind with Pocket Sand, Trained Deflection and Hyrri's Ire. These are in "Deflection beside evasion" and "Blind as
  defence".
- Partly held: its title's claim. In EHP deflection is worth ×1.61 at most against evade's ×5.05, but a deflected hit
  loses 40% whatever its size, which no armour share matches on a big hit.

**`vid-build-process` (video KGdyaWB3q54, build design).**
- Taken: start from the skill; give the build one tool for packs and one for bosses; pick the ascendancy by which of
  its nodes feed the idea; path the tree for jewel sockets; when a piece fails, keep the goal and swap the means.
  Controlled Metamorphosis and Spectral Ward are in "Radius jewel instead of an anoint" and "Derived defence layer".
  The process points wait for the next amendment.

**`vid-scale-damage` (video BkOraAt7tYk, damage).**
- Taken: the order of the damage equation, which agrees with PoB and backs the library's damage-order rule, and the
  enemy's side of the equation (curses, exposure, penetration), for the coming damage entries.

**`vid-secret-damage` (video U5ZSQYkPsNE, damage).**
- Taken, for the coming damage entries: penetration past zero (Leopold's Applause), the lowest-resistance sceptre
  (Sacred Flame), Yoke of Suffering, the monster-level cap on Elemental Weakness, and Original Sin. Several of these
  are things PoB reads wrong or not at all at the pin.
