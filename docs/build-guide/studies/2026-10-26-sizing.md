# Sizing amendment 12's open candidates (2026-10-26)

**Why.** The defence study's proposals list the candidates for amendment 12 to the build-guide spec. Some are marked
"size first": the spec can't set a threshold, or decide whether the change is worth building, without a number. This
study measured those numbers on the defence study's 30 level-90+ published builds (`2026-10-26-defence-layers.md`).
It measures and explains, and decides nothing. Builds are cited by the library's page slug, shortened to the creator and
skill. The scripts, raw results and each part's full report stay in the user's private library.

**How.** Four agents worked on it:
- three measured with the engine at the pin (0.5.4, PoB v0.23.1), in parallel, one engine each, with trade off;
- a fourth priced the results on the trade site with the user's approval, one request at a time.

Damage is `MainDamage`, measured on the boss study's first boss skill under its realistic config. Toughness is
measured under the design's enemy for the non-budget rung. The parts used about 1.73M agent tokens in all:
- damage, 530k;
- defence, 487k;
- jewels and crit, 491k;
- prices, 226k.

## The defence gate

**The Arbiter's accuracy (G9): a correction.** The coordinator first told the user that no source gives the Arbiter's
accuracy, having checked only PoB, and the user ruled to fall back to PoB's average. Our own data has it:
`data/enemies/arbiter-of-divinity.json#base.accuracy` is 3,786 at monster level 79, from poe2db. That is 1.6 × the
design enemy's 2,357.
- At 3,786, the three evasion builds the Arbiter one-shots deflect 66%, 50% and 14% before any blind.
- So 34%, 50% and 86% of slams kill them, not the 16%, 33% and 79% the defence study gave at the design enemy's
  accuracy.
- The rest of the ruling stands. A deflecting build that survives a deflected slam isn't counted as one-shot. The
  share of slams that kill is an accepted risk, shown at the gate.

**Tree points to fix a one-shot (G2).**
- **Points:** 1, 3, 32 and 48 for the four one-shots.
  - The cheap two are a single choice: a small energy shield node on animeprincess's Twister Gemling, and Mind over
    Matter on Ignatius's Comet Infernalist. Each costs 3–6% damage once whole damage branches are freed to pay for it.
  - The big two don't pay. LazyExile's Amazon loses 63% of its damage to free 32 points, and the fix still slips back
    to a one-shot. The Beast Master has only 11 points of damage nodes to give up.
- **Does the current ranking find the fix?** Node power's existing EHP ranking finds it on one of the four, the
  Comet. On the evasion builds it offers evasion first.
- **Under the ruling,** only the Comet stays one-shot, so the current ranking already covers this set.
- **A trap:** a ranking by max hit alone offers Iron Reflexes, which turns all evasion into armour. It cleared the
  Twister's one-shot at half its EHP. A max-hit ranking would need node power's guard on EHP.

**Deflection on evasion builds (P11).**
- **The rating each target needs:** against map monsters (accuracy 2,357), 70%, 90% and 95% need 16,958, 29,057 and
  33,444. PoB's rounding makes these a little under the closed form's 7.29, 12.5 and 14.4 × accuracy. Half the cap's
  rating gives 70%.
- **Points to 70%:**
  - 6 of the 18 evasion builds are at 70% or within 3 points of it.
  - The 10 that start under 50% need 11–41 points (median 22).
  - Two hybrids can't reach 70% from the tree.
- **Points to 90%:** about half again as many: pure evasion median 31, hybrid 35.
- **Gear:** each top gear suffix adds 22% of evasion as rating, 25% on a body. Near 10k evasion, even every free slot
  falls short of 70%.
- **Against the best EHP nodes:**
  - Deflection points buy about a third of the EHP the same points buy spent on the best EHP nodes.
  - They cut the share of lethal Arbiter slams far more: the Amazon drops from 79% to 29% at 20 points, against 63%
    on the EHP route.
- **Blind's accuracy cut varies by build.** It scales with the build's increased blind effect, so blinded enemies
  read 0.68–0.81 × the base accuracy, not a flat 0.8.
- **Glancing Blows is no deflection source.** It makes deflection lucky but evasion unlucky, and it lowered EHP on
  every build it read on.

## Damage

**A resisting target (D2).**
- **Builds that follow resistance:** on the 13 builds whose damage follows enemy resistance normally, 40% on the main
  element (the waystone's top) costs a median 18%, and 75% costs 41%. With penetration the costs are 8% and 29%.
- **Builds that don't:**
  - 8 builds invert resistance with Rakiata's Flow, so a resisting monster raises their damage by 7–63%;
  - 4 ignore resistance on crits or with Fire Penetration II;
  - 1 uses the lowest resistance (Sacred Flame).
- **The Pinnacle figure already stands in.** It ranks builds much as the 75% figure does (rank correlation 0.80), and
  shows penetration on 10 builds. If a resisting-monster figure is added anyway, it should put 75% on all three
  elements, as the waystone modifier does. A single element misreads mixed and lowest-resistance builds.
- **The Pinnacle halves curses and exposure,** so it understates a curse's worth against a map rare.

**The curse level cap (D3).**
- **Finished builds:** no reference build runs a capped curse. All 10 that curse use gems of level 18–21, and the cap
  ends at 18.
- **The cap schedule:** 72 at gem level 14, 78 at level 17, and none from 18.
- **Two more debuffs** carry the same cap, which PoB also never reads (PG-86, widened): Frost Bomb's exposure and
  Thunderstorm's Wet.
- **So the check is a guard for planned low gem levels,** in the campaign and on entering maps: gem level 14 for area
  69, and 18 for areas 79 and 82.

**The emptiest pool (D4).** Per 10 points added to a finished build, at the Pinnacle:
- enemy damage taken: median +9.7%;
- resistance reduction: +9%, but negative on every inversion build;
- gem level, gain as extra and crit damage bonus: about +6% each;
- increased damage: +2.3%, from an implied pool of 337%;
- crit chance: +1.7%;
- a more multiplier: always +10%.

Depth predicts value where the tools show it, as 10 ÷ (100 + depth) for an additive pool. They don't expose the
increased sum or the product of the more multipliers. The brainstorm question can carry this: the enemy's two pools
are usually emptiest, and each point there is worth about four points of increased damage. It needs an inversion
branch: never reduce resistance on a build that inverts it, or buy it on one that ignores it.

## Jewels and crit

**Jewel sockets (T2).**
- **How jewel affixes roll:** PoB's data gives each jewel affix a single range, with no tiers. A rare jewel holds 2
  prefixes and 2 suffixes.
- **The best sets:**
  - attacks: an Emerald with damage against rares and uniques, attack damage, attack crit chance and attack crit
    damage, +7–17% at the top roll;
  - crit casters: a Sapphire, about +10%;
  - minions: a Sapphire with its 3 useful lines, +7–13%.
- **Roll level:** a middle roll gives about 70% of the top roll's gain.
- **Sockets:** on finished trees no empty socket sits within 3 points; the nearest are 4–8 away. A top-roll jewel is
  worth about 3.8 points of the best nodes, so a socket pays at about 3 points or less (2 for a middle roll). The
  candidate's per-socket test stands.
- **Prices** (Forbidden Rites, 2026-10-27, 1 divine = 672 exalted):
  - 3 good lines: about 5 divines at the middle roll for attacks, 22 at the top for casters, 69–100 for minions;
  - a 4th line, or the crafted "increased Effect of Prefixes/Suffixes" line: 130–400 divines.
- **So the budget rung buys a jewel's best 3 lines,** and the 4th line and the craft belong to the non-budget rung.
- **Boss or clear:** the damage-against-rares line leads the attack sets only because the measure is a boss. Lines
  should be scored on clear too.

**Crit against enemy-side multipliers on a budget (D7).**
- **Crit gear:** one budget crit item adds 9–42% on builds that crit:
  - a weapon with both local lines, +29–42%;
  - a focus, +14–16%;
  - an amulet, +9–15%.

  Rings roll no crit line.
- **Every budget crit item sells at the trade floor.** At tier 4, the amulet, focus and quiver cost 1 exalted, and
  the helmet and gloves about 2. The two crit lines add about 6 exalted to a quarterstaff and about 1 to a spear. So
  in currency the creator's claim, that crit costs more for less on a budget, fails. It may hold in affix slots:
  every crit line is a suffix, the same slot as resistances, so a crit item that also carries resistances costs
  more. That case wasn't priced.
- **The levers:** on these finished builds most enemy-side levers are already used or worth nothing. The exceptions
  are Rage with Berserk on attacks (+13–63%, for 30 spirit) and a reliable shock. The lever gems weren't priced.
- **Inversion again:** on a build that inverts resistance, a curse or exposure loses damage (−15% and −18%). But
  Rakiata's Flow, the support that inverts, costs about 185 divines, and not much less early in a league (the user).
  So budget builds don't invert, and curses and exposure stay levers there. The rule to skip resistance levers
  matters on the non-budget rung.

## Side findings

- **`load_config_preset` turns off checkboxes PoB has on by default** (B6).
  - It clears every option the preset doesn't hold, a checkbox to false (`src/handlers/configHandlers.ts:135`), though
    the tool promises PoB's default.
  - 14 options and 9 quest rewards go off. One Flicker Strike build fell to 5% of its damage.
  - The pipeline doesn't call this tool.
- **The trade limiter let a 429 through** (B7). One agent, alone on the server, drew a 600-second lockout at its
  19th search in about 4.5 minutes. That is inside the windows the site's headers state. The retry, 7 searches 60–94
  seconds apart, met no limit. The cause is unknown, since the refusal's headers went only to the server's error
  output. Until the limiter enforces a slower pace itself, the project's CLAUDE.md sets interim rules: one trade
  client at a time, at least 45 seconds between searches, at most 30 searches without asking, and stop on the
  first refusal.
- **`update_tree_delta` paths through weapon-set nodes, then refuses the path** (B8). Passing the full path works.
- **PoB doesn't read the crafted jewel "Effect of Prefixes/Suffixes" lines** (PG-99). A jewel copied from the game
  reads right, because its text carries the boosted values. A jewel a compile crafts reads unboosted.
- **One boss-study figure checked out.** The Balrog Fire Bear's figure looked as if it no longer reproduced: 27.5k
  today against the study's 15.7k. The boss study's own script, re-run, explains it. That build works only on weapon
  set I, which the study selected by hand, while its file saves set II. The study's figure and the boss floor stand.
